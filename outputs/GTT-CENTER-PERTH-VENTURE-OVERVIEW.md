## 1. What This Is

GTT Center Perth is a pre-launch concept for Western Australia's first premium wellness venue built around the Glucose Tolerance Test (GTT) — the mandatory ~2–2.5 hour gestational diabetes screening test undergone by an estimated ~33,570 WA women per year (KPMG analysis of ABS Births, Australia 2024). Instead of a bare pathology waiting room, clients spend the mandatory test window receiving massage, nails, hair, or brow services. The venue also operates a standalone afternoon wellness business open to any woman, pregnant or not.

**No direct competitor exists in WA.** The only identified comparable is MIWM (Melbourne) — a smaller, single-client-at-a-time model with no hairdressing offer.

## 6. Premises Requirement

A single Perth venue is required before recruitment, fit-out, or council approvals can begin. Functional requirements (validated against the 18-client/day operating model): ~239–290sqm depending on tier, ground floor, 8–10+ dedicated parking spaces, one solid-walled clinical collection room (the only genuinely clinical space — everywhere else is designed as a premium residential-feeling wellness space), 4 treatment rooms, 4 nail stations, 4 hairdressing chairs, existing plumbing/3-phase power strongly preferred. A live property search is underway with real Perth candidates identified; none yet inspected or leased.

## 7. Pathology Partnership

GTT Center Perth's chosen model (Option A) partners with an already-NATA-accredited pathology provider for collection oversight rather than building in-house accreditation (which would carry a 12–18 month timeline). Western Diagnostic Pathology (WDP) is the priority partner, in active, ongoing correspondence — several regulatory and operational questions already resolved directly with WDP; the commercial/rental terms figure remains outstanding. PathWest and Clinipath remain contingency options. **No partnership agreement is yet signed — nothing in this document should be read as confirming one.**
