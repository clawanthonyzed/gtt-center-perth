How it works

  1. 01

    Your blood collection

    Delivered alongside a licensed pathology collection service, in a private, purpose-built room — the one part of the venue built to full clinical spec.

  2. 02

    The wait, reimagined

    Between draws, you move to a warm, open lounge for a wellness service of your choice — massage, nails, hair, or brows — instead of sitting in a waiting room.

  3. 03

    Your privacy, designed in

    Full-length, opaque, lockable privacy curtains for every treatment space — no client ever sees another client mid-treatment. It's a design principle here, not an afterthought.
