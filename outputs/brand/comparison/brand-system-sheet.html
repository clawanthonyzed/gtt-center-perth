Material direction (docs/strategy/VISUAL-BRAND-DIRECTION.md SS6/SS20 -- written direction, quoted directly)
Preferred Limestone / travertine (honed, not tumbled), a single considered warm-mid-tone timber species used consistently, textured plaster/render in a warm neutral, natural-fibre textiles (linen, wool, cotton bouclé), brushed brass or bronze hardware, reeded/fluted glass used sparingly, one well-placed specimen plant per zone. Avoid Marble with heavy veining, gold hardware, chrome/polished nickel/brushed steel, raw or reclaimed timber, exposed concrete, velvet in saturated jewel tones, distributed generic planters. Target Natural + luxurious -- explicitly not rustic + boho. Refined-matte finish throughout, present but calm texture, clean confident joinery, understated solid-feeling hardware.
No visual material board (physical swatches, rendered interior) has been produced -- this is the written direction only, presented here as text rather than invented as a new visual concept, consistent with this task's packaging-only scope.
