Calculators

Current truth Both tools below recreate the exact live formulas already used in the dossier (Chapters 30, 31, 16); they are not a second model. All maths runs client-side in your browser, no server, no AI call. Every input change recalculates instantly.

Break-even & revenue calculator

Recreates Chapter 30's exact break-even formula and Chapter 31's 6/12/18-client sensitivity table for any AM client volume you choose.

Outside the disclosed fixed-headcount range. Below ~6 clients/day, Chapter 31's demand-driven staffing solver finds treatment headcount can actually flex down (e.g. 4 staff instead of 8 at a widened 45-min cadence): this calculator does not model that flex by default, so results below 6/day understate what a demand-flexed roster could achieve. Treat this range as illustrative only, not a planning figure. Use the toggle below to see the demand-flexed alternative.

AM revenue = clients/day × A$250 (Package 1, the conservative revenue-calculation price, Chapter 27) × 26.33 operating days/month (22 weekday + 4.33 Saturday, same daily volume assumed for both). PM revenue (A$36,225.69/month) and total opex (A$110,544.52/month) are held fixed: this matches the model's own disclosed limitation that committed headcount does not currently flex with booking volume, the same simplification Chapters 30 and 31 already use.

Staffing reliability calculator

Recreates Chapter 16's exact binomial reliability method: P(at least k of n staff available), each staff member independently unavailable at the given rate. The 8% default is a disclosed planning assumption (Chapter 16), not verified WA industry data.
