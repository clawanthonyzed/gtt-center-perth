## 1. Executive Summary

**The business.** GTT Center Perth turns the 2 to 2.5 hour wait for the Glucose Tolerance Test (GTT) into a restorative visit. The GTT is a standard pregnancy screening test. Mothers fast, then have blood drawn three times over two hours. Today the wait is spent in a pathology waiting room. At GTT Center Perth the mother books and pays in advance, has her blood drawn on site in a private collection room by our own employed phlebotomists, and uses the time between draws for massage, nails, hair or beauty services in a calm lounge. In the afternoons the same premises sells wellness services and packages to pregnant women who are not having the test.

**Why it should work.** Demand is not optional: nearly every pregnant woman is screened. Greater Perth has about 26,790 births a year, which is roughly 515 tests a week. Our research found no equivalent venue in WA. This is our own finding, not an independent audit.

**The model.** Staff are employed (casual or permanent), not subtenants. Up to 18 morning clients a day, Monday to Saturday, from a 07:00 first booking. Clients book 2 to 4 weeks ahead and a waitlist backfills cancellations. Staff are rostered to each morning's confirmed bookings. Lab testing and courier are put to tender between PathWest and Western Diagnostic Pathology (WDP). GTT earns no pathology revenue.

**Three conditions travel with these numbers.**
1. **Rent.** The cost model uses A$8,000 a month. The approved rent budget is A$9,500 to A$10,500 a month. The right-hand column shows the A$10,500 gross ceiling (rent, outgoings and car bays all in).
2. **Fill rate.** The model assumes every released morning slot fills (100%). Each 1% of fill at capacity is worth about A$1,185 a month. At 85% fill, profit is about +A$17.4k a month. At 70% fill the business makes a small loss. This is why the waitlist and the 2 to 4 week booking window are core to the plan.
3. **Cadence.** The 8.266 break-even relies on the 45-minute pair cadence for mornings of 10 or fewer bookings (adopted 2026-10-11). Full mornings run at the 25-minute cadence.

**Funding position.** The planning need is A$336,198 to A$361,198 (A$251,198 pre-opening capital plus A$85,000 to A$110,000 working capital). Funds on hand are about A$200,000 (as stated in the repository, not re-verified since 2026-07-29). The gap on the peak-funding basis is about A$67,771. The funding route is TO CONFIRM.

**Where we are.** No venue is secured. No lab agreement is signed. Both are launch gates (Section 11). Hiring and fit-out wait behind them.

## 2. Value Proposition

### 2.1 The problem

| Today | What mothers get |
|---|---|
| Wait time | About 2 to 2.5 hours (some sources say up to 3) |
| Setting | A pathology waiting room, with draws at set times |
| Condition | Fasted, tired, unable to eat, unable to leave |
| Care during the wait | None |

The test is not optional. The choice that remains is where to have it.

### 2.2 The solution

| Element | What GTT Center Perth provides |
|---|---|
| Blood collection | On site, in private collection rooms, by GTT's own employed, certified phlebotomists |
| The wait | Pregnancy massage, facials, nails, hair and brows between draws. A draw never interrupts a service. |
| Lounge | Venue and lounge access (water, herbal tea, birth plan tablet) is free inside every package |
| Booking | Book and pay online in advance. Waitlist for popular mornings. |
| Results | The lab tests the samples and sends results to the mother's doctor. GTT does not diagnose or interpret results. |

### 2.3 What is not in the launch scope

- 3D keepsake ultrasound is a possible Phase 2 idea only. It would be entertainment and keepsake only, never diagnostic.
- Spray tan has been removed from the concept.
- Cafe and retail income is set to A$0 in the financial baseline.

### 2.4 Honest view of the advantage

Demand is real and non-discretionary, and we are first. The experience itself is not hard to copy. The lasting advantage has to be built from midwife and obstetrician referral relationships, a compliant collection room and a trusted lab partner.

## 5. Clinical Operations

### 5.1 Who does what

| Task | GTT Center Perth | Lab partner |
|---|---|---|
| Venue, collection rooms, chairs | Provides | Inspects and signs off |
| Employs the phlebotomists | Yes | Must engage or credential them under its own quality system |
| Draws blood | Yes | No |
| Courier and lab testing | No | Yes (winning lab) |
| Medicare billing for the test | No. GTT earns nothing from pathology. | Yes |
| Results to the doctor | No | Yes |
| Diagnoses or interprets results | Never | Not applicable (the doctor does) |

Marketing must not say "we perform GTT testing". The phlebotomist's scope is pathology collection only.

### 5.2 Scheduling standard (adopted 2026-10-11)

| Item | Standard |
|---|---|
| First booking | 07:00, both chairs start together |
| Chairs and phlebotomists | 2 chairs, 2 in-house phlebotomists on every morning with any GTT booking |
| Full mornings (15 or more bookings on a weekday, 17 or more on a Saturday) | 25-minute pair cadence, 9 pairs, **18 clients**, 8 therapists. Last first-draw 10:20. |
| Quiet mornings (10 or fewer bookings) | **45-minute pair cadence**, up to 10 clients, 4 therapists. Last first-draw 10:00. |
| Last first-draw limit | No later than 10:30 (lab guidance) |
| Booking | 2 to 4 weeks ahead, prepaid, plus a waitlist |
| Trading days | Monday to Saturday |

The 25-minute and 45-minute schedules were checked by the repository's scheduling solver (run 2026-10-11) with zero chair clashes. The 45-minute cadence applies to low-volume mornings only. Applied to every morning it would cap capacity at about 10 clients a day.

### 5.3 One client, one visit

| Clock | Step |
|---|---|
| Arrival | Arrives fasted. Check-in, request form, Medicare card, two identity checks. |
| 0 minutes | Draw 1 (fasting, about 5 minutes), then the glucose drink |
| After Draw 1 | Service 1 |
| 60 minutes exactly | Draw 2 |
| After Draw 2 | Service 2 |
| 120 minutes exactly | Draw 3. The mother may leave only after this. Snack offered at once. |

The client stays on site and seated or escorted throughout. Services are low-activity only. Specimens are labelled at the chair, stored in a medical fridge at 2 to 8 degrees C (kept apart from food), and handed to the courier once, at the end of the morning.

### 5.4 Lab tender (dual-lab strategy, locked 2026-10-10)

GTT employs two certified in-house phlebotomists. Courier and lab analysis are put to tender between PathWest and WDP for the best per-test fee schedule. Neither lab has an exclusive commitment. Clinipath is an optional third bidder only if it replies.

| Criterion | PathWest | WDP |
|---|---|---|
| Per-test fee | TO CONFIRM | TO CONFIRM |
| Courier pickup | Confirmed 2026-09-01 for the correct tubes. Times TO CONFIRM. | Feasible with the correct tubes. Overnight storage conditional. No fixed cutoff. |
| Tubes and bags | Free if volume is large | TO CONFIRM |
| Glucose drink | Not supplied | TO CONFIRM |
| Turnaround, result delivery, minimum term | TO CONFIRM | TO CONFIRM |
| Approved Collection Centre (ACC) arrangement | TO CONFIRM | TO CONFIRM |

**Caveat that is not yet solved.** Only the pathology partner can hold the Commonwealth ACC approval for a collection room, and two competing labs cannot both hold it for the same room. Options to test are one ACC-holding lab per room (we plan two rooms), a separate arrangement per room or per lab, or one lab holding it while the other does courier and analysis only if its rules allow. This must be verified with both labs and an adviser before it is relied on. Nothing is signed and neither lab has quoted a fee.

## 7. Marketing Strategy

### 7.1 Principle

The main customer channel for the morning GTT is the referring practice, not paid advertising. Every pregnant woman already has a GTT referral. The task is to make the practice aware that a better place to have it exists. The afternoon customer is different: she is discretionary and finds us through Instagram, Google and word of mouth.

### 7.2 Morning GTT: referral network

| Element | Plan |
|---|---|
| First-wave targets | 22 named practices already identified: 12 private midwifery practices and 10 obstetric and gynaecology clinics. None contacted yet. |
| Why these | Independent enough to recommend a collection venue. Hospital-based public clinics tend to have a default pathway, so the public-sector channel is lower priority. |
| Test batch | First 3 midwifery practices with a direct personal contact. Expand to the remaining 19 only after all 3 have a recorded outcome and at least 1 gives a genuine "yes, we would refer". |
| Approach | Personal visit or call first, then a premium referral card, a one-page practice information sheet, and an invitation for the practice's staff to experience the service once open |
| GP channel | No GP practice is named in the repository. Method: target bulk-billing GP clinics with high antenatal volume near the confirmed venue. |
| Timing | Pre-launch activity that depends on a confirmed venue address |

Each visit must end in one recorded outcome: the practice displays cards, accepts a complimentary visit, or declines. A visit with no recorded outcome does not count.

**Referral metrics (framework, not a forecast):**

| Metric | Definition |
|---|---|
| Outreach completion | Practices visited out of 22 |
| Adoption rate | Practices displaying cards out of practices visited |
| Referral share | Morning bookings tagged to a practice out of all morning bookings |
| Bookings per practice per month | Identifies top referrers for quarterly check-ins |

Tracking uses the "How did you hear about us?" field in the booking system. It is a named Venue Manager responsibility from week 1 of opening.

**Referral law.** Offers to practices are limited to a complimentary visit and information. Any formal referral credit or partner pricing for health practitioners needs solicitor advice first (see Section 5.5).

## 8. Team and Staffing

### 8.1 Principle: demand-responsive rostering (Anthony, 2026-10-11)

Staff are rostered to the number of clients booked that morning. Clients book weeks ahead and a waitlist is kept. This does not change steady-state profit (18 clients a day needs the full roster). It cuts the ramp-up cost: payroll for Months 1 to 3 falls from A$280,787 to A$214,844.
