How a visit works

  1. Book and pay online in advance.
  2. Arrive fasted. Check in and consent.
  3. Draw 1 (about 5 minutes), then service 1.
  4. Draw 2 at 60 minutes, then service 2.
  5. Draw 3 at 120 minutes. Relax in the lounge.
  6. One courier pickup of the day's samples at the end of the morning. GTT does not diagnose or interpret results.
