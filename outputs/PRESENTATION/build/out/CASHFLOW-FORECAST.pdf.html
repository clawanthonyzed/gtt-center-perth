5. Notes

  1. No debt assumed. All A$336,198 is received before opening. No loan drawdowns, interest or repayments are modelled.
  2. Pre-opening capital (A$251,198, including the full contingency) is spent in Month 0. This is conservative.
  3. GST. Monthly BAS, paid the month after (financial model). All sales are treated as taxable. GST treatment is general information only and must be confirmed with the accountant. The model's own P&L carries no GST line, so the model-basis payback is Month 8 and the after-GST payback is Month 11.
  4. Receipts timing. Clients prepay at booking. No debtor or creditor timing is modelled.
  5. No ramp-up, fill rate is the central risk. Fill is assumed 100% from Month 1. At 85% fill, peak funding stays A$251,198 but payback is Month 16 (before GST timing) and Month 35 (after GST). At 70% fill the business does not pay back and the A$85,000 reserve is used up in about 9 months (financial model).
  6. Lab fee is assumed A$0 to GTT (model assumption). Tender fees are to be confirmed.
  7. Rent is the A$8,000 headline per Anthony's instruction. Rent during fit-out is not in the base.
  8. Not in the model: income tax, depreciation, interest, loan funding, cafe equipment, fit-out for the larger floor area.
