4. Notes

  1. Cadence and rostering. The 25-minute pair cadence is the only cadence: first booking 07:00, pairs at 07:00, 07:25, 07:50 and so on, last first draw 10:20, 9 pairs, 18 clients a morning at most. Staff are called in to match the confirmed slots each morning, AM and PM; idle staff are never rostered. Booking is 2 to 4 weeks ahead. Phlebotomists are rostered adaptively (Anthony, 2026-10-17): 1 phlebotomist when 9 or fewer are booked, 2 when 10 to 18, none in the afternoon, with a named second collector on call on light mornings, paid only if called in (assumed). This saves A$234.51 a weekday morning (A$6,682.23 a month) on light mornings and lowers break-even to 7.250. The pathology partner and the Medicare and lab-employment rules may require the lab to supply collectors; if so the roster is the lab's decision.
  2. No ramp-up and fill rate. Fill of rostered slots is assumed to be 100% from Month 1. At 85% fill, steady-state net is about +A$17.4k per month (A$17,413.65 before GST timing). At 70% fill the business makes a small loss (-A$359.10 a month). With no ramp-up to cushion it, fill is the central risk (financial model).
  3. GST. GST treatment is general information only and must be confirmed with the accountant. The P&L is GST-inclusive with no line for GST paid; the GST-stress column shows the downside. Each percentage point of the AM package that is GST-free is worth A$107.71 a month at 18 clients a morning (financial model). 3a. 10% post-test discount. Adopted 2026-10-17 for any expecting mother who completes a GTT test, on standalone treatments or packages, morning or afternoon. It is not deducted from the figures above: at A$117 a booking it costs A$277 to A$2,218 a month at 5% to 40% take-up (A$1,386 at 25%), and pays for itself if 10% of redeemers are new bookings (24% if extra hours must be paid). Take-up is an assumption.
  4. Lab fee. The model assumes A$0 to GTT. Each A$10 per client would cost about A$4,739 per month at capacity (financial model). Tender fees are to be confirmed.
  5. Rent. A$8,000 is the headline per Anthony's instruction. The real search budget is A$9,500 to A$10,500 per month (financial model).
  6. Award rates. Wage rates are the award casual rates (1 July 2026). Verify with Fair Work before acting.
  7. Not in the model: income tax, depreciation, interest, rent during fit-out, early-release staffing upside.
