## 3. Break-even working

**Adaptive rostering on the 25-minute pair cadence (first booking 07:00, last first draw 10:20)**

| Step | Working | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Fixed costs per month | Rent, other overhead, cost of goods, PM labour and Venue Manager with on-costs | A$36,738.11 |
| 2. Add the AM roster for a morning of up to 9 clients booked one per 25-minute slot | 1 phlebotomist + 4 therapists, with on-costs, 22 weekdays + 4.33 Saturdays | + A$35,572.95 |
| 3. Total costs at that roster | A$36,738.11 + A$35,572.95 | **A$72,311.06** |
| 4. Less non-AM revenue | PM services held at full steady state | - A$24,585.37 |
| 5. Costs AM GTT must cover | A$72,311.06 - A$24,585.37 | **A$47,725.69** |
| 6. Revenue per daily AM client per month | A$250 x 26.33 trading days | **A$6,582.50** |
| 7. Break-even | A$47,725.69 ÷ A$6,582.50 | **7.250 AM clients per day** |

7.250 is 40.3% of the 18-client capacity, a margin of safety of 10.750 clients per day (59.7%). This is the break-even on the 25-minute cadence with phlebotomists rostered to the bookings. The roster behind it (1 phlebotomist and 4 therapists) is what the cadence needs when bookings fall one per slot: at most two first services overlap at once, so 4 therapists cover up to 9 clients, and the three draws of each client never overlap, so one chair carries them.

**The break-even depends on how bookings land in the 25-minute slots**

| Booking pattern | Roster at the break-even | Break-even AM clients per day | GST-stress break-even |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bookings spread across the slots (one client per slot, spaced) | 1 phlebotomist + 4 therapists | **7.250** | 8.138 |
| Planning case: bookings fill from 07:00, one client per slot first | 1 phlebotomist + 4 therapists | **7.250** | 8.138 |
| Bookings stack in pairs from 07:00 (repo solver as published) | 2 phlebotomists + 8 therapists | 12.655 | 14.083 |
| Planning case with a paid 3-hour second phlebotomist shift on every light morning | 2 phlebotomists (one for 3 hours) + 4 therapists | 7.758 | 8.696 |

The headline is the planning case, **7.250** (was 8.266 when 2 phlebotomists were rostered on every morning). The GST-stress break-even is 8.138 while bookings stay one per slot; if they stack in pairs both chairs run and it is 14.083 (the roster then steps up to 6 or 8 therapists).

## 4. Notes

1. **Cadence and rostering.** The 25-minute pair cadence is the only cadence: first booking 07:00, pairs at 07:00, 07:25, 07:50 and so on, last first draw 10:20, 9 pairs, 18 clients a morning at most. Staff are called in to match the confirmed slots each morning, AM and PM; idle staff are never rostered. Booking is 2 to 4 weeks ahead. **Phlebotomists are rostered adaptively (Anthony, 2026-10-17):** 1 phlebotomist when 9 or fewer are booked, 2 when 10 to 18, none in the afternoon, with a named second collector on call on light mornings, paid only if called in (assumed). This saves A$234.51 a weekday morning (A$6,682.23 a month) on light mornings and lowers break-even from 8.266 to 7.250. The pathology partner and the Medicare and lab-employment rules may require the lab to supply collectors; if so the roster is the lab's decision.
2. **No ramp-up and fill rate.** Fill of rostered slots is assumed to be 100% from Month 1. At 85% fill, steady-state net is about +A$17.4k per month (A$17,413.65 repo basis). At 70% fill the business makes a small loss (-A$359.10 a month). With no ramp-up to cushion it, fill is the central risk (model §13).
3. **GST.** GST treatment is general information only and must be confirmed with the accountant. The P&L is GST-inclusive with no line for GST paid; the GST-stress column shows the downside. Each percentage point of the AM package that is GST-free is worth A$107.71 a month at 18 clients a morning (model §11).
3a. **10% post-test discount.** Adopted 2026-10-17 for any expecting mother who completes a GTT test, on standalone treatments or packages, morning or afternoon. It is not deducted from the figures above: at A$117 a booking it costs A$277 to A$2,218 a month at 5% to 40% take-up (A$1,386 at 25%), and pays for itself if 10% of redeemers are new bookings (24% if extra hours must be paid). Take-up is an assumption.
4. **Lab fee.** The model assumes A$0 to GTT. Each A$10 per client would cost about A$4,739 per month at capacity (model §10). Tender fees are TO CONFIRM.
5. **Rent.** A$8,000 is the headline per Anthony's instruction. The real search budget is A$9,500 to A$10,500 per month (model §9).
6. **Award rates.** Wage rates are the award casual rates (1 July 2026). Verify with Fair Work before acting.
7. **Not in model:** income tax, depreciation, interest, rent during fit-out, early-release staffing upside.
