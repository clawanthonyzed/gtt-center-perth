## 3. Break-even working

**Demand-responsive rostering basis (LOCKED by Anthony 2026-10-11)**

| Step | Working | Result |
|---|---|---|

| 2. Add one 2 + 4 roster every morning | 2 phlebotomists + 4 therapists, with on-costs | + A$42,255.18 |
| 3. Total costs at the low tier | A$36,738.11 + A$42,255.18 | **A$78,993.29** |
| 4. Less non-AM revenue | PM services held at full steady state | − A$24,585.37 |
| 5. Costs AM GTT must cover | A$78,993.29 − A$24,585.37 | **A$54,407.92** |
| 6. Revenue per daily AM client per month | A$250 x 26.33 trading days | **A$6,582.50** |
| 7. Break-even | A$54,407.92 ÷ A$6,582.50 | **8.266 AM clients per day** |

## 4. Notes

1. **45-minute cadence.** The 45-minute pair cadence for quiet mornings (10 or fewer bookings) was adopted by Anthony on 2026-10-11. Full mornings (15+ weekday, 17+ Saturday) keep the 25-minute cadence. Only the 25-minute cadence reaches 18 clients per day and the +A$35,186.40 steady state.
2. **Fill rate.** Fill of rostered slots is assumed to be 100%. At 85% fill, steady-state net is about +A$17.4k per month (A$17,413.65 repo basis). At 70% fill the business makes a loss (model §13).
3. **GST.** GST treatment is general information only and must be confirmed with the accountant. The repo P&L is GST-inclusive with no line for GST paid; the GST-stress column shows the downside.
4. **Lab fee.** The repo assumes A$0 to GTT. Each A$10 per client would cost about A$4,739 per month at capacity (model §10). Tender fees are TO CONFIRM.
5. **Rent.** A$8,000 is the headline per Anthony's instruction. The real search budget is A$9,500 to A$10,500 per month (model §9).
6. **Award rates.** Wage rates are the repo's award casual rates (1 July 2026). Verify with Fair Work before acting.
7. **Not in model:** income tax, depreciation, interest, rent during fit-out, early-release staffing upside.
