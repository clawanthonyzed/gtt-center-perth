# PART 1: BRIEF FOR LEASING AGENTS AND LANDLORDS

## 1. Who we are

A premium, appointment-only pregnancy wellness lounge in Perth. Pregnant women attend for their 2-hour glucose tolerance blood test (GTT) and receive pregnancy-safe treatments while they wait. In the afternoons the same premises offers wellness and beauty services (massage, facials, nails, hair).

- 100% booked appointments. Not a walk-in or foot-traffic business.
- Blood collection is performed by our own employed phlebotomists. Specimens go to an accredited pathology laboratory partner for processing.

## 2. Where we are looking

Perth metro. Clients drive in from across the metro area, so road access and parking matter more than passing trade.

| Priority | Area | Why it suits us |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Osborne Park** (Hutton St, Scarborough Beach Rd, Balcatta Rd, Warrick St / Odin Dr, Joseph Banks) | Mitchell Freeway access, strong on-site parking stock |
| 2 | **Joondalup** (Boas Ave, Lakeside Dr / Grand Blvd, Shenton Ave, Davidson Tce / Joondalup Dr) | Northern corridor hub, near Joondalup Health Campus |
| 3 | **Cannington / East Cannington** (Manning Rd / George St, Albany Hwy, Welshpool Rd East) | Albany Hwy / Roe Hwy, near Bentley Health Campus |
| 4 | **Myaree / Murdoch** | Kwinana Freeway / Leach Hwy, Murdoch health precinct |

We will also consider strong options elsewhere in metro Perth, especially near a hospital or medical precinct, provided parking and access are strong.

**Property types that suit us:** existing medical, allied-health or consulting suites (first choice); medical/office business-park units; retail or showroom shells with good parking (if plumbing can be added).

# PART 2: INTERNAL LEASE CHECKLIST (DO NOT SEND)

> **General information only.** The WA law points below are general information, not legal advice, and are from general knowledge, not from the repo (the repo does not cite the WA Act anywhere). **Verify every point with a WA leasing lawyer before relying on it.**

## A. Commercial Tenancy (Retail Shops) Agreements Act 1985 (WA)

| Topic | General position (verify with a WA leasing lawyer) | What we do |
|---|---|---|
| **Does the Act apply?** | It covers "retail shop" leases: premises under 1,000 sqm used mainly to sell goods or provide services to the public by retail, or premises in a retail shopping centre. Hair, nails and beauty look like retail services. Pathology collection and pregnancy clinical services may not. Our mixed use makes this a genuine grey area. | Ask the lawyer to confirm in writing which regime applies. If the Act does **not** apply, every protection below must be negotiated into the lease. |
| **Disclosure statement** | If the Act applies, the landlord must give a disclosure statement and a copy of the proposed lease before the lease is entered (a minimum notice period applies). Missing disclosure can give the tenant remedies. | Do not sign until we hold the disclosure statement and the full lease. |
| **Minimum term** | The Act has a 5-year minimum term rule (initial term plus options), which a tenant can contract out of only by following the Act's process. | Our 3 + 3 target (6 years) already clears 5 years. Confirm the option is genuine and enforceable. |
| **Rent review limits** | For Act leases, a market review cannot be a ratchet (only-up) clause, and each review should use one clear method. | Seek fixed % or CPI. Strike out ratchet clauses whether or not the Act applies. |
| **Outgoings** | Landlord must disclose estimated outgoings and give annual statements. Some costs cannot be passed to the tenant. | Get an itemised outgoings budget, a cap, and audit rights. |
| **Make-good** | Not fixed by the Act. Set by the lease. | Exclude plumbing, HVAC, accessible WC and other landlord-useful works from reinstatement. Get a photo condition report at handover. |
| **Assignment / subletting** | For Act leases, the landlord cannot unreasonably withhold consent where the Act's conditions are met. | Seek "consent not to be unreasonably withheld" in all cases. |
| **Bank guarantee / bond** | Set by the lease. Check whether a cash bond, a bank guarantee, or both are asked for. | Target a bond capped at a reasonable multiple of monthly rent. Any personal guarantee capped at 6 months' rent, never open-ended. |
| **Incentives** | Set by negotiation (rent-free, fit-out contribution). Key money (a premium just to grant the lease) is generally prohibited under the Act. | Ask for 3 to 6 months rent-free during fit-out first, then a fit-out contribution. |
| **Lease preparation costs** | The Act limits what the landlord can charge the tenant for preparing the lease. | Ask the lawyer to confirm before accepting any cost-recovery clause. |
| **Disputes** | The WA Small Business Development Corporation (Small Business Commissioner) handles retail tenancy disputes. | Note for later. |

Source: `docs/architecture/COMMERCIAL-LEASE-RED-FLAG-CHECKLIST.md`.

| Area | Red flag |
|---|---|
| Term / exit | Demolition or redevelopment clause with little or no compensation |
| Term / exit | Absolute bar on assignment or subletting |

| Term / exit | No real option period (shorter than 3 + 3) |

| Rent | No rent-free or incentive offered for a heavy fit-out |

| Use | Landlord silent on blood collection and clinical waste (get written acknowledgement) |
| Use | No exclusivity in a multi-tenancy centre |
| Money | Open-ended personal guarantee |
| Money | Bank guarantee much larger than the stated bond |
| Money | GST treatment not stated (10% swing on rent) |
| Fit-out | Full make-good to base building with no exclusions |
| Fit-out | No defined landlord consent process or timeline for fit-out works |
