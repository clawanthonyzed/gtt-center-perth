## Criteria (from the commercial site brief)

Size 257 to 290 sqm | gross rent A$9,500 to A$10,500 a month (base plus outgoings, excluding GST) | room for 2 collection rooms | parking 8 to 10 client bays plus 3 to 5 staff | ground-level step-free entry | plumbing for basins (at least 8 hands-free basins on day one) | zoning that permits health, beauty and a small refreshment counter.

**How the gross monthly rent is worked.** Annual base rent divided by 12, plus outgoings. Where a listing does not state outgoings, an indicative band is used: A$89 to A$147 per sqm a year. That band comes from the only two outgoings figures held for Perth commercial sites in the venture file (6/325 Harborne Street, A$23,785 for 268 sqm, about A$89 per sqm; 45 Stirling Highway, Nedlands, about A$147 per sqm). It is an indication, not a quote; the real figure must come from the agent.
