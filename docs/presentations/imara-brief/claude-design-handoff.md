## A. Presentation objective

**Audience:** Imara, Anthony's partner. She has context that SOLENA exists as a project but has not yet seen a structured explanation of the business.

**What she needs to understand, in order:**
1. What SOLENA is, and why the opportunity exists.
2. What the client experiences, morning and afternoon.
3. How the venue runs day to day.
4. How the business makes money, in numbers she can check herself.
5. What has already been resolved, and what she and Anthony still need to decide together.

**Tone:** confident, factual, warm where the brand calls for it. This is a business document two co-owners use to make a decision together, not an external pitch deck.

**Non-negotiable content rules, apply while writing every page:**
- The working name is SOLENA throughout. ELOWEN appears only on the naming page.
- No em dashes anywhere in the document. Use commas, full stops, colons, parentheses, or restructure the sentence.
- Use "the client" rather than repeated use of "her."
- State once, near the start of the financial section: "All financial figures are in Australian dollars unless otherwise stated." After that, use $ rather than "A$."
- No internal project language: no "founder locked," "founder decision," "honest caveat," references to which draft or timetable version was sent to a partner, no GitHub references, no mention of a prior presentation's errors or corrections.
- No dropped or rejected concepts appear anywhere, not even as a note that something was removed.
- No property addresses, no specific buildings, no square metre figures.
- SOLENA is one venue, one brand, one business, with two revenue streams (AM GTT, PM wellness). Never "one venue, two businesses."
- Use "profit," not "margin," when referring to a dollar amount. Reserve "margin" only for a correctly calculated and clearly labelled percentage, and call it "operating profit margin" when used.

## B. Page-by-page content

### Page 1. Cover
SOLENA as the primary wordmark, the largest element on the page. Beneath it, one line: "A premium wellness venue for pregnancy glucose tolerance testing, and beyond." Prepared for Anthony and Imara, dated September 2026.

### Page 2. The opportunity
Every pregnant woman in Australia takes a mandatory gestational glucose tolerance test, usually between 24 and 28 weeks: a fasting appointment with three blood draws across roughly two hours. The ordinary experience is an unremarkable pathology waiting room. SOLENA's opportunity, stated exactly this way: "Two hours of waiting now filled with self-care. The choice of massage, beauty, hair or nails built quietly into the schedule." Because the test is mandatory and recurring, demand is steady rather than something that has to be created from nothing.

Market context: Perth's metro area sees an estimated 515 gestational glucose tolerance tests a week, roughly 2,232 a month.

### Page 3. What SOLENA is
"SOLENA operates as a premium wellness venue throughout the day. The morning experience is focused on GTT clients, while the afternoon operates as a normal premium wellness salon offering the same high quality wellness and beauty services." One venue, one brand, one client experience, two revenue streams: AM GTT and PM wellness and beauty.

SOLENA is a wellness venue with an employed collection service inside it. SOLENA employs the phlebotomists who collect the sample. An external, independently accredited pathology partner performs laboratory transport and processing. SOLENA is not a pathology laboratory.

### Page 4. The customer journey
See Diagram F1 for the full sequence. Key points to state with confidence, not as work in progress:
- Clients discover SOLENA through social media, a planned marketing campaign, word of mouth, and referrals from midwives and obstetricians.
- Food and drink preference can be selected at the time of booking.
- Once the final blood draw is complete, food and drink is available, whether pre-selected at booking or purchased at that point.
- After the AM appointment, a client may choose to continue with a wellness service. A 10% online discount applies when this additional service is pre-booked.
- The client discovers SOLENA's normal PM wellness services during the visit and can return for them independently in future, with no further blood collection required.

### Page 16. Break-even
Fixed monthly operating cost is $107,884 at the fully staffed, 18 client design. SOLENA covers that cost at **12.655 clients a day**, which is 70.3% of the 18 client target, roughly 76 clients a week, and about 333 clients a month.

Perth's metro area sees an estimated 2,232 GTT tests a month. Break-even represents approximately 15% of that market.

The buffer between break-even and the 18 client target is 5.3 clients a day, roughly 30% of the target volume.
