## 1. Business Naming — Options Presented, No Name Finalised

**This document does not finalise a name, and should not be read as attempting to.** `docs/brand-guide.md`'s own governance is explicit and unambiguous: "GTT Center Perth" is a working placeholder, and "Anthony has confirmed a premium brand name and full branding identity... will be created later, on his own timeline — this is not a currently-open decision awaiting an answer." Nothing in this repo, and no analysis performed for this document, produces evidence strong enough to override that founder decision or force a winner — so none is forced.

**What is presented instead — a summary of naming directions already explored in this repo, none adopted, for reference when Anthony does take this up:**

| Direction | Example(s) already in this repo | Strength | Weakness |
|---|---|---|---|
| Descriptive/place-name (current placeholder) | "GTT Center Perth" | Immediately clear what it is and where; strong SEO for a first-mover in WA | Clinical-sounding, doesn't carry the brand's warmth; reads as a placeholder because it is one |
| Concept-driven (already flagged in `poppy-marketing.md` §3, never adopted) | "Wait and Glow" | Genuinely memorable, captures the core reframe ("the wait, reimagined") this venture's whole positioning already leans on; the strongest concept-driven option already on record | Loses the immediate GTT-specific clarity; would need supporting copy to explain what the business does at first contact |
| Softer venue-framing | "The GTT Room", "GTT Lounge" | Retains searchability, softens the clinical framing slightly | Still clinical-first; "Lounge" is already used descriptively elsewhere in this venture's own copy ("GTT wellness lounge"), risking dilution if also used as the name |

**Recommendation on process, not on a name:** when Anthony does take this up, `docs/business-plan.md` §7's own finding — no direct GTT-integrated competitor exists in WA specifically (both known comparables, MIWM and The GTT Lounge, are east-coast) — means naming has real latitude; there is no entrenched local competitor name to differentiate against. This is presented as decision context, not a push toward any option above.

## 2. Positioning Statement

Synthesised directly from `docs/brand-guide.md` §1 and `docs/business-plan.md` §7, not a new positioning — restated in one clear statement for the first time:

**"Western Australia's first venue built specifically around the mandatory GTT wait — turning a clinically necessary 2-3 hour appointment into a genuine, premium wellness experience, without pretending to be either a pathology lab or a day spa."**

This is a direct restatement of `docs/brand-guide.md`'s own "who we are" framing, combined with `docs/business-plan.md`'s own confirmed differentiator (first WA venue of this kind — both known comparables, MIWM Melbourne and The GTT Lounge Sydney, are east-coast, neither operating in WA).

## 3. Target Customer Profile

Reused directly from `docs/business-plan.md` §4, not re-derived — this document adds no new demographic claim beyond what is already disclosed as a planning assumption, not an independently surveyed fact:

- **Gestation:** 24-28 weeks (the GTT testing window) for the primary AM audience; a broader range for PM standalone/afternoon services.
- **Geography:** Perth metro, drive-time dependent on the eventual confirmed venue.
- **Socioeconomic:** assumed mid-to-upper SES, given the discretionary wellness spend layered on a mandatory medical test — **explicitly flagged in the source as "not independently surveyed, a planning assumption,"** not restated here as a confirmed fact.
- **Referral pathway:** midwife/OB-GYN referral, online search, word of mouth — the same three channels this document's own Early Demand Validation Strategy (§4 of that document) builds directly on.

## 4. Key Differentiators

Reused directly from `docs/business-plan.md` §7's Competitive Analysis, confirmed still current:

1. **No direct GTT-integrated competitor in WA** — both known comparables (MIWM Melbourne, The GTT Lounge Sydney) operate exclusively on the east coast.
2. **Multi-client concurrent model** — this venture's committed capacity (up to 18 clients/day at Table 1) contrasts with MIWM's own reported single-client-at-a-time model.
3. **Full-service breadth** — massage, nails, hair, and beauty in one venue, a wider service menu than the "smaller, less premium" positioning `docs/business-plan.md` itself attributes to The GTT Lounge Sydney.
4. **Clinical credibility without clinical coldness** — `docs/brand-guide.md`'s own "clinical confidence" + "premium warmth" pillar pairing, a genuine differentiator against both a pure pathology-collection-centre experience and a generic day spa that has never handled a mandatory clinical test.

## 6. Tone of Voice — Confirmed, Not Changed

`docs/brand-guide.md` §1 already establishes a complete, well-considered tone-of-voice system (calm/confident/direct for headings, warm/real/dry for Instagram, reassuring/practical for bookings, clear/plain/never-alarming for clinical information). **This document makes no change to it** — it is reused as-is, confirmed still fit for purpose against the positioning and differentiators above, with one addition: the voice rules' explicit ban on words like "amazing," "incredible," "game-changer," and "journey" already rules out exactly the kind of overclaiming language a first-mover-in-WA positioning could otherwise be tempted to lean on — worth noting as a genuine, already-built-in discipline, not a gap.

`docs/VERIFICATION-TRACKER.md` item 37 confirms: a second venue's timeline is set only once the first venue is running smoothly, with no earlier estimate implied anywhere.
