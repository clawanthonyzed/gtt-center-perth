# Fit-Out Equipment & Furniture Schedule — Master Procurement List

**Date:** 2026-08-21 | **Companion document to:** `docs/architecture/FIT-OUT-DESIGN-PROCUREMENT-BRIEF.md` §20. **Status:** Draft procurement schedule, not yet sent to any supplier, no purchase authorised.

**Source discipline:** every figure below is either reformatted directly from `docs/architecture/ITEMISED-PURCHASE-LIST.md` and `docs/equipment-costs.md` (both already-researched, MEDIUM/LOW-confidence market-range estimates, not real quotes), or explicitly marked as a genuine gap. No new dollar figure is invented in this document.

**Confidence key (unchanged from the source documents):** **MEDIUM** = market-range estimate cross-checked against a named real supplier category, not an actual quote. **LOW** = wide range, genuinely uncertain pending a real quote.

**Station-count basis — see the brief's own §3.2 flag:** this schedule is built against the currently-costed model (1 Blood Collection Room/2 chairs, 4 combined Massage/Beauty treatment rooms, 4 nail stations including pedicure chairs, 4 hairdresser chairs/2 backwash) because that is the only model this venture has real, sourced cost research for. **Where the brief's own §3.1 program (2 Blood Collection Rooms, 3 Massage + 4 Beauty = 7 stations, 4 Nail + 4 Pedicure) would require more units than shown here, the additional quantity is marked "PENDING §3.2 RECONCILIATION" rather than invented.**
