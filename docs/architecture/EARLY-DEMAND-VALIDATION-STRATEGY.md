## Grounding Principle — What's Realistic for a New, Unbranded Venue

**This document does not assume any referral relationship already exists.** `docs/poppy-marketing.md` and `docs/reed-partnerships.md` contain a real, named list of Perth midwifery practices, OB/GYN clinics, and maternal-child-health contacts — but `docs/referral-partnership-plan.md`'s own status is unambiguous: "Not yet actioned... outreach to referring practices is a pre-launch marketing activity... which itself depends on a venue location being confirmed." **No clinical referral volume can be assumed for Month 1 from a venue with zero track record and zero prior relationship with any of these practices.** This document's core, honest finding is that clinical referral trust takes real time to build — meaningfully longer than the gap between "venue confirmed" and "Month 1 of trading" is likely to allow, no matter how early outreach starts. **The genuinely achievable Month 1 lever is direct-to-consumer demand (waitlist conversion), not clinical referral volume** — this document is weighted accordingly, not evenly across every channel.

## 1. Direct-to-Consumer Waitlist Strategy — The Primary Month 1 Lever

**Why this is the right primary lever:** unlike a clinical referral relationship, a consumer waitlist requires no pre-existing trust with a third-party practice — only awareness and a real reason to book. `docs/business-plan.md` §8 already references an (unverified) target of "300+ waitlist names before opening" via Instagram and referral-card distribution — this document treats that figure as a directional target, not a committed number, consistent with the source's own "not re-verified this session" caveat.

**Recommendations:**
- **Start waitlist-building the moment fit-out begins** (Week 9-10 per `docs/grace-startup-plan.md`), not at soft-launch — behind-the-scenes fit-out content is genuinely engaging for an expecting audience and costs nothing beyond the already-planned Instagram presence (`docs/brand-guide.md` §6).
- **Convert the waitlist into real Month 1 bookings deliberately, not passively.** Recommend a structured pre-booking window in the final 1-2 weeks before opening (aligning with `docs/grace-startup-plan.md`'s Week 17-18 test-operations phase) where waitlist members are offered first access to real appointment slots — a scheduling/priority-access tactic, not a pricing change (any pricing incentive would require separate approval and is explicitly not modelled or assumed here, consistent with the "no financial model changes" constraint).
- **Track a real conversion rate from Day 1** — `docs/architecture/COMMERCIAL-VALIDATION-FRAMEWORK.md`'s own Revenue Ramp Evidence Framework already flags that no conversion-rate evidence exists anywhere in this repository; this is the single cheapest way to start closing that gap, and should be treated as a KPI from the first waitlist signup, not only from opening day.

## 2. Midwife/OB-GYN Referral Relationships — A Medium-Term Lever, Started Early

**Realistic framing:** the named practice list already exists (`docs/poppy-marketing.md` §1) and is a real, usable starting point — but a cold-contacted practice will not meaningfully refer clients to an unproven, unbranded venue within its first few weeks of trading. **Recommendation: begin outreach the moment a venue is confirmed** (the actual gating dependency, per `docs/referral-partnership-plan.md`'s own sequencing), explicitly treating the first 1-2 months of relationship-building as trust-establishment, not a Month 1 volume source. Realistic value: a meaningful contributor to Month 2-6 ramp, not Month 1.
- `docs/referral-partnership-plan.md`'s own "What's Missing" section already flags a genuine gap worth closing early: no GP-specific outreach research exists yet. **GP referral is assessed here as a plausible but secondary channel, not a primary one** — the mandated GTT test is typically ordered within an existing midwife/obstetrician-led care pathway in WA, not requiring a GP touchpoint in most cases; GP referral is worth a light, low-cost outreach pass alongside the main midwife/OB list, not a dedicated campaign.
- Verify the named practice list's own currency before outreach begins — `docs/poppy-marketing.md`'s own list "has not been independently verified for accuracy/currency," per `docs/referral-partnership-plan.md`'s own disclosed gap.

## 3. Community Partnerships — A Genuinely Lower-Friction Channel for a New Venue

**Why this matters for Month 1 specifically:** unlike clinical referral (which requires professional trust), a cross-referral partnership with an adjacent pregnancy-focused business requires only mutual commercial benefit — a genuinely faster relationship to establish for a brand-new venue. `docs/afternoon-marketing-plan.md` already identifies a real, specific example: Perth bump/pregnancy photographers, already noted as "already spending on prenatal yoga, pregnancy photography, maternity clothes" — i.e. this venture's own target customer is already a customer of these adjacent businesses. Recommend prioritising: pregnancy/bump photographers (cross-referral both directions, already flagged), prenatal yoga/pilates studios, and maternity-wear retailers — none currently researched as named contacts in this repo, a genuine gap worth a light research pass before outreach, not assumed to already exist as relationships.

## 4. Maternal-Child-Health (MCH) Nurse Relationships

Distinct from midwife/OB (a public-sector, not private-practice, channel) — `docs/poppy-marketing.md` §1 already lists Perth MCH contacts. Realistic framing: likely slower-moving than private practice outreach (government processes), but broader population reach once established. Treat as a parallel, lower-priority-but-worth-starting-early track alongside §2, not a Month 1 lever.

Directly reconciled against the already-approved marketing budget in `docs/architecture/MVP-OPENING-DECISION-REVIEW.md` §2.3: a A$1,000 marketing contingency reserve, held rather than spent immediately, released on a real Week 1-4 booking-vs-ramp trigger. **This document does not propose increasing that reserve** — it proposes what the reserve should actually be spent on if the trigger fires: targeted Perth-metro social advertising aimed specifically at the 24-28-week-gestation window (the primary AM target audience, per `docs/business-plan.md` §4), not a generic brand-awareness campaign, since the goal at that point is filling specific near-term GTT slots, not general awareness.

`docs/architecture/AM-STAFFING-RAMP-OPTIMISATION.md` found that even the most aggressive defensible staffing optimisation still leaves Month 1 close to, but not comfortably above, AM-segment break-even. **This document's contribution is on the other side of that equation — moving real Month 1 volume up, not moving break-even down further** (which the staffing document already found has a hard limit). ## 7. Risks Identified

- **The 300+ waitlist target is unverified** (`docs/business-plan.md`'s own disclosed caveat) — this document treats it as directional, and recommends tracking real signups from Day 1 of fit-out rather than assuming the figure will be hit.
- **No conversion-rate evidence exists anywhere in this repository for any channel** — every recommendation above is a reasoned, disclosed tactic, not a guaranteed outcome; `docs/architecture/COMMERCIAL-VALIDATION-FRAMEWORK.md`'s own already-flagged gap remains genuinely open until real trading data exists.
- **Overclaiming clinical referral speed is a real risk this document deliberately avoids** — presenting the named practice list as a Month 1 volume source would be inconsistent with this venture's own stated brand-new, unbranded status, and is explicitly not done here.

## 8. Recommended Next Decisions

1. **Begin waitlist-building (Instagram, referral cards) the moment fit-out begins**, not at soft-launch — the single cheapest, earliest-startable action in this whole document.
2. **Begin midwife/OB/community outreach the moment a venue is confirmed**, explicitly framed internally as Month 2+ ramp support, not a Month 1 solution — so expectations are set correctly from the outset.
3. **Commission a light research pass on named pregnancy-adjacent community partners (photographers, prenatal yoga, maternity retail)** before outreach begins — currently a genuine gap, not yet researched with the same rigour as the clinical-practice list.
