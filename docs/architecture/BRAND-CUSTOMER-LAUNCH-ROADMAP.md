## 1. Business Naming Decision Process — A Process, Not a Decision

**Same boundary as the prior phase, respected identically here: this document does not choose or narrow down a name.** `docs/brand-guide.md`'s own governance is explicit — naming is Anthony's own decision, on his own timeline, not a currently-open question awaiting external input. What follows is a lightweight *process*, offered for if/when Anthony chooses to engage it — not a deadline imposed on him.

**Suggested process, entirely optional and on his own schedule:**
1. Review the naming considerations already compiled (`docs/architecture/BRAND-IDENTITY-FRAMEWORK.md` §1 — three directions already explored: descriptive placeholder, concept-driven "Wait and Glow," softer venue-framing).
2. If desired, engage a naming/branding consultant, or run his own creative process directly — no repo-based recommendation on which.
3. Once a name is chosen, trigger a **full revision of `docs/brand-guide.md`** — already flagged in that document's own governance banner as needed ("not just a find-and-replace of the name") — before any further public-facing brand asset is finalised against it.

**One scheduling observation, not a deadline:** `docs/brand-guide.md` §10 lists logo files and Instagram profile assets as required "Before Phase 1 (Week 7 — Instagram launch)." If a final name is chosen before that point, the Week 7 assets can be built against it directly, avoiding a re-build once the waitlist audience is already growing under a placeholder identity. If the name isn't ready by Week 7, `docs/brand-guide.md`'s own placeholder system is explicitly designed to be usable in the meantime — this is a real, working fallback, not a blocker.

## 2. Branding Milestones — Reused, Not Recreated

Directly from `docs/brand-guide.md` §10's existing Brand Assets Checklist, cross-referenced not restated in full:

## 5. Waitlist Campaign Requirements — The Same Waitlist, Not a Second Campaign

**Explicit, direct link:** the waitlist campaign this document describes IS the waitlist `docs/architecture/PRE-LAUNCH-DEMAND-VALIDATION-PLAN.md` builds its go/no-go thresholds against (Gate 1: 500-700 signups by the end of the pre-lease evaluation window). This is not two disconnected marketing exercises running in parallel — one plan, one number, one campaign.

**What this means concretely:**
- The waitlist landing page (§3) and Instagram presence (§4) are the actual mechanisms that produce the signups §1's demand validation plan measures.
- Content strategy should prioritise genuine signup conversion over vanity engagement metrics — `docs/poppy-marketing.md` §5's existing 12-week content calendar (40% lifestyle/experience, 30% service content, 20% educational, 10% social proof, per `docs/brand-guide.md` §6) remains the right mix, but the explicit success metric for this specific pre-lease window is waitlist signups against the Gate 1 threshold, not follower count or engagement rate alone.
- Referral-card distribution (§1 of the demand validation plan) should be sequenced alongside the digital waitlist push, not treated as a separate later-phase activity — both funnel into the same waitlist.
