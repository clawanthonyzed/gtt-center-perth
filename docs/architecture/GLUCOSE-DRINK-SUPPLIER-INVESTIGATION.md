## 1. Why 75g Matters — Confirmed Clinical Requirement

`docs/gtt-clinical-protocol.md` confirms GTT Center Perth's protocol is **ADIPS 2014 Consensus Guidelines / FIGO 2015: 75g glucose, 2-hour test** (§2, "Standard"), and explicitly states this dose **cannot be substituted** — "Cannot substitute the 75g glucose drink with food (food-based OGTT is not equivalent for GDM diagnosis)" (§ Fasting Requirements/notes). A 50g product is a different clinical test (commonly used for a 1-hour non-fasting GDM screen, not the 75g/2-hour diagnostic OGTT this venture performs) and is **not a valid substitute or price comparator** for this venture's protocol.

## 2. POCD Scientific — Concentration Verification (Task Item 1)

**Finding: POCD Scientific's live, priced product is 50g, not 75g. It is NOT a valid comparator as currently listed.**

- The URL originally found (`https://www.pocdscientific.com.au/pathology/GTT_2`) was re-checked directly. The product on that page is titled **"GTT Drink 50g Clear (24)"** — confirmed 50 grams, 300ml single-serve, 24/carton, $55.00/carton (~$2.29/unit). The page description references that "either 50g or 75g concentrations" exist as a general product family, but only the 50g variant is shown live/priced at that URL.
- A distinct URL for the 75g variant exists (`https://www.pocdscientific.com.au/pathology/GTT`, titled "GTT Drink 75g Clear (24)" per search-index results) but on direct fetch **returns "This item is no longer available"** — no live listing, no price.
- **Conclusion: POCD Scientific is excluded from the price comparison below.** Its only currently live/priced product (50g) is the wrong concentration for GTT Center Perth's protocol; its 75g listing is dead. If POCD's 75g product is restocked in future, it would need to be re-verified from scratch (concentration and price) before being reconsidered — do not carry forward the $2.29/unit 50g figure as if it were a 75g price.

## 8. Could the Glucose Drink Be Sourced from China Instead? (Added 2026-09-04)

**Anthony asked directly whether this consumable could be sourced from China, the way furniture and fixtures are (`docs/architecture/CHINA-SOURCING-AGENT-ENQUIRY-PACKAGE.md`). Honest answer: technically possible, but very unlikely to be worth pursuing for this specific item, for reasons genuinely different from the furniture case — not a hard "no," but a considered "probably not worth it."**

**What was checked:** Alibaba does list general liquid-glucose/dextrose manufacturers and distributors (bulk pharmaceutical-grade glucose/dextrose syrup, mostly out of Shandong/Guangdong), so raw glucose supply from China genuinely exists as a category. However, no confirmed listing was found this session for the *specific* product GTT actually needs — a pre-dosed, single-serve 75g/300ml ready-to-drink OGTT test beverage, the exact clinical-grade consumable format sold by POCD/Surgical House/Diabetes WA/Medisa/Vital Medical. What Alibaba search results surfaced was general bulk glucose/dextrose supply and generic glucose-monitoring-device (glucometer/test-strip) manufacturers — a different product category, not a confirmed source for this specific pre-dosed test beverage.

**Why this is a materially different sourcing decision from furniture, stated honestly, not as false certainty either way:**
- This is a consumable given directly to pregnant clients as part of a clinical diagnostic test, not a chair or a shelf. Consistency of dose (must be genuinely 75g, every time, no variance) and product quality/safety matter in a way they simply don't for fit-out furniture.
- Any imported food/beverage product would need to meet **Food Standards Australia New Zealand (FSANZ)** import requirements — a genuine additional compliance layer that the furniture pathway (explicitly fit-out only, per `docs/architecture/CHINA-SOURCING-AGENT-ENQUIRY-PACKAGE.md`'s own scope, which excludes clinical consumables entirely) does not have to clear at all.
- **Local suppliers are already priced at $2.29-2.80/unit (§3 above) with no import lead time, no food-safety approval process to run, and no batch-consistency risk to manage from a new, unverified overseas source.** For a genuinely small per-unit dollar amount like this, the potential saving from China sourcing (even if a supplier were found) is unlikely to outweigh the added regulatory approval time, import lead time, and quality-consistency risk for a product used directly in a medical test.
- **This is Grace's honest assessment, not a hard fact or a closed door.** If Anthony wants this explored further despite the above, the concrete next step would be to identify a specific Alibaba supplier genuinely offering the exact pre-dosed 75g/300ml OGTT beverage format (not generic bulk glucose), verify their existing export experience with FSANZ-compliant food/beverage products into Australia, and get a landed-cost quote (product + freight + FSANZ approval process + import duty) to compare directly against the $2.29-2.80/unit local price — none of that has been done, and is not recommended as a priority given the small dollar amounts involved.
