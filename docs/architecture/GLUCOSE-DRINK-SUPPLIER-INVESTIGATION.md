## 1. Why 75g Matters — Confirmed Clinical Requirement

`docs/gtt-clinical-protocol.md` confirms GTT Center Perth's protocol is **ADIPS 2014 Consensus Guidelines / FIGO 2015: 75g glucose, 2-hour test** (§2, "Standard"), and explicitly states this dose **cannot be substituted** — "Cannot substitute the 75g glucose drink with food (food-based OGTT is not equivalent for GDM diagnosis)" (§ Fasting Requirements/notes). A 50g product is a different clinical test (commonly used for a 1-hour non-fasting GDM screen, not the 75g/2-hour diagnostic OGTT this venture performs) and is **not a valid substitute or price comparator** for this venture's protocol.

## 2. POCD Scientific — Concentration Verification (Task Item 1)

**Finding: POCD Scientific's live, priced product is 50g, not 75g. It is NOT a valid comparator as currently listed.**

- The URL originally found (`https://www.pocdscientific.com.au/pathology/GTT_2`) was re-checked directly. The product on that page is titled **"GTT Drink 50g Clear (24)"** — confirmed 50 grams, 300ml single-serve, 24/carton, $55.00/carton (~$2.29/unit). The page description references that "either 50g or 75g concentrations" exist as a general product family, but only the 50g variant is shown live/priced at that URL.
- A distinct URL for the 75g variant exists (`https://www.pocdscientific.com.au/pathology/GTT`, titled "GTT Drink 75g Clear (24)" per search-index results) but on direct fetch **returns "This item is no longer available"** — no live listing, no price.
- **Conclusion: POCD Scientific is excluded from the price comparison below.** Its only currently live/priced product (50g) is the wrong concentration for GTT Center Perth's protocol; its 75g listing is dead. If POCD's 75g product is restocked in future, it would need to be re-verified from scratch (concentration and price) before being reconsidered — do not carry forward the $2.29/unit 50g figure as if it were a 75g price.

## 4. Bulk/Wholesale Digging at GTT's Real Planning Volume (Task Item 2)

**GTT's real planning volume:** up to 18 clients/day, Monday–Saturday = up to 108/week. At full ramp, this is approximately 450–470/month (108/week × ~52/12 weeks ≈ 468/month). At 24 units/carton, that is **approximately 19–20 cartons/month at full ramp** — not day-one volume; ramp-up will be materially lower in early months.

**What was checked beyond the original six suppliers**, per the task's suggested search terms and independent leads:

- **"glucose tolerance test drink wholesale account pricing Australia"** — returned no supplier disclosing a genuine volume-tier price publicly. Diabetes WA's bulk-pricing-on-request offer is the only concrete lead surfaced, and it remains unconverted (phone contact required, not yet made).
- **"medical consumables wholesale account Perth pathology supplies distributor"** — surfaced several general Perth/WA medical distributors (Hurst Scientific, Australian Medical Solutions Group, Disposable Medical Supplies Australia, ParagonCare, Medshop). None were confirmed to stock the specific 75g GTT drink product with a visible price; ParagonCare and Australian Medical Solutions Group searches returned no GTT-drink-specific product page. Not pursued further than a search-level check — would need direct enquiry to rule in/out.
- **Yes Medical (yesmed.com.au)** — has a "Glucose & INR" category under Diagnostic Equipment but no live GTT-drink product page or price found.
- **Medisa's full GTT collection page** (`medisa.com.au/collections/gtt-drink`) — confirms only one SKU, currently on **Preorder**, "From $4.20" (implying an untested lower per-unit price at higher quantity, not itemised).
- **National Diagnostic Products, Australian Biosearch** — no evidence found that either stocks this specific product; general web presence for GTT/pathology topics only.
- **Livingstone International** — has a genuine "Bulk Buy Request" submission mechanism on its trade catalogue, but the product page itself is trade-gated (no visible price without an account/quote request). This is a real, not-yet-exhausted lead — not a dead end.
- **Vital Medical Supplies** — pricing on the product page notes "Please Sign in / Register for more," implying a trade/wholesale tier behind account creation. **Not explored further this session** — creating a supplier trade account was judged out of scope for a research pass without Anthony's instruction; flagged as an open lead, not pursued.

**Conclusion on bulk pricing: no supplier disclosed a genuine, quotable volume-tier price for ~450-470/unit monthly demand during this research pass.** The two real open leads (Diabetes WA's phone-negotiated bulk rate, and Livingstone's Bulk Buy Request) both require direct outbound contact GTT does not yet have the tooling to complete (no phone-calling capability, no account credentials) and are logged here as follow-ups, not resolved figures. No bulk figure has been invented or assumed for either.

**A second point worth flagging:** Surgical House ($2.31/unit) is already cheaper per-unit than Diabetes WA's listed sticker price ($2.80/unit), before any bulk discount is even considered — this reverses an assumption that might otherwise form from Diabetes WA being the first-found, WA-based candidate (as referenced in `docs/pathwest-clinipath-outreach-2026-07-27.md` §4e-vii's design notes). Surgical House is also WA-based (Osborne Park, Perth), so the same "local supplier" advantage Diabetes WA was credited with in that earlier note applies equally to Surgical House, and at a lower confirmed price.

**Recommendation:** Treat Surgical House as the current front-runner for E21 procurement, pending two still-open follow-ups that could change the picture:
1. Obtain Diabetes WA's actual bulk-order price via direct phone/email contact (1300 001 880 / info@diabeteswa.com.au) — if it beats $2.31/unit at GTT's real volume with a workable ordering mechanism (not capped at 15/order), it would overtake Surgical House.
2. Submit a Livingstone Bulk Buy Request to get a real quote — currently a complete unknown, not ruled out.

Neither follow-up has been actioned yet (no phone calls made, no account/quote requests submitted) — both are logged as open next steps, not completed research.

**Not yet factored into any of the above:** shipping/freight cost. No supplier's shipping cost was confirmed this pass. Given both Surgical House and Diabetes WA are Perth/WA-based, local pickup may be possible for either and should be asked about directly alongside the bulk-quote follow-ups above, since it could change the effective landed cost more than the sticker-price gap between them.
