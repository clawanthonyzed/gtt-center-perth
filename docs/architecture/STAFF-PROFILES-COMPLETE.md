# Complete Individual Staff Profiles

Status: current as of 2026-08-23. Every profile below stands alone: none cross-references another profile for its own content, per direct founder instruction. Position ID family references the authoritative register at `docs/architecture/STAFF-POSITION-REGISTER.md`.

All staff receive first aid/CPR training with explicit emphasis on incidents associated with blood collection: fainting, vasovagal response, bleeding, bruising, difficult venepuncture complications, and client distress, not a generic first aid course. Exact accredited-course requirements (HLTAID011 Provide First Aid or equivalent) still need direct confirmation from a registered training organisation before being written into a real employment contract; this is flagged, not invented.

## Venue Manager (VM01)

**Purpose:** Operational leadership of the venue and its people. Covers reception duties personally during the AM window. Not a "Managing Director" role in the sense of being removed from daily operations: hands-on, reception-facing, and the single accountable point for day-to-day operations at this venture's scale.

**Responsibilities:** Rostering and Fair Work compliance; weekly P&L review; incident management; pathology-partner liaison; AM reception from the venue's actual 07:00 opening; oversight of the demand-driven PM roster; emergency-only service cover (maximum one service, then returns to management duties).

**Required qualifications:** A genuine service qualification, Cert III/IV in Beauty Therapy, Massage, or Hairdressing (or dual), so the Venue Manager can step into treatment/service work in a genuine emergency, not just supervise.

**Preferred qualifications:** Prior venue/salon/day-spa management experience; First Aid/Fire Warden certification.

**Skills:** Rostering and Fair Work compliance literacy; Fresha administration; weekly P&L review literacy; incident-management composure; hands-on service delivery capability.

**Customer-service expectations:** First point of contact for every AM client at check-in; sets the tone for the venue's premium-not-luxury positioning; handles complaints and service recovery personally.

**Hygiene expectations:** Standard hospitality/beauty-industry infection control; oversight of the venue's cleaning line, not personally responsible for treatment-room cleaning between clients.

**Safety expectations:** First Aid/CPR (mandatory), Fire Warden (mandatory), trained first aider for anaphylaxis response; holds the venue's EpiPen; escalation contact for any adverse event.

**AM role:** Covers reception personally 07:00 opening, no gap. Wind-down/admin from approximately 12:00-13:00.

**PM role:** Overlaps the PM window 13:00-15:00 (own shift continues to 15:00). No presence 15:00-18:00; PM treatment staff cover reception during this period via Model C.

**Cross-training expectations:** Can step into a treatment shift in a genuine emergency only (maximum one service), using the salon-manager wage assumption for that time, not a full clinical or management substitution; this leaves reception/management duties uncovered simultaneously, so it is not a free substitution and is supported through cross-training/up-training rather than assumed automatic.

**Reception-cover capability:** Is the AM reception coverage personally; does not require another position to cover for them during the AM window.

**Emergency/first-aid expectations:** Trained first aider; EpiPen holder; escalation contact for vasovagal events, difficult venepuncture escalation beyond the second on-site phlebotomist, and any incident requiring 000.

**Reporting structure:** Reports to Anthony Zed / YETI Tipi Holdings directly, no intermediate management tier. Every other position reports to the Venue Manager.

**Position ID family:** VM01.

## Phlebotomist (PHL01-PHL04)

**Purpose:** Perform GTT blood collection under the pathology partner's accreditation umbrella. The single highest-consequence, zero-substitution role in the staffing model.

**Responsibilities:** Three exact-timed draws per client (T=0, T=+60, T=+120); glucose drink administration as part of the T=0 collection workflow; specimen labelling and chain-of-custody; collection log and specimen dispatch log maintenance; identity verification against the referral form before the first draw.

**Required qualifications:** Cert III/IV Pathology Collection, credentialed under the pathology partner's own NATA-accredited umbrella before performing any collection (a precondition, not a formality).

**Preferred qualifications:** Prior experience at a real collection centre alongside the Cert III/IV; this is a genuinely unresolved research gap, not independently confirmed as a WDP-specific requirement.

**Skills:** Venepuncture proficiency across patient presentations, including pregnancy-specific considerations; calm bedside manner at the journey's highest-anxiety point; precise specimen labelling and chain-of-custody discipline.

**Customer-service expectations:** Reassuring, efficient, and precise; the clinical mark timing (exactly +60/+120 minutes) is non-negotiable, so client-facing communication must set expectations accordingly.

**Hygiene expectations:** Pathology partner's own Licensed Collection Centre QMS induction; hand hygiene before and after every collection; sharps and biohazard disposal per the collection room's own compliance requirements.

**Safety expectations:** Vasovagal/fainting response protocol (keep seated or reclining, escalate to the Venue Manager immediately, call 000 if unconscious); anaphylaxis training current at all times; difficult-venepuncture escalation protocol (maximum 2 attempts, then the second on-site phlebotomist attempts; if neither succeeds, reschedule or refer per WDP's own clinical protocol, not an invented internal supervisor role).

**AM role:** 07:00-13:00, Chair A or Chair B, exactly 2 rostered per day, synchronised 25-minute pair cadence with the other on-site phlebotomist.

**PM role:** None. No PM clinical role exists for this position; fasting-window/GTT protocol requirements are AM-only.

**Cross-training expectations:** None. Zero substitution capability from any other position in the venture; this is the one role in the entire staffing model with no cross-cover mechanism.

**Reception-cover capability:** None. Phlebotomists do not cover reception at any point; the AM window has zero schedule slack for a phlebotomist to take on a secondary duty.

**Emergency/first-aid expectations:** Current anaphylaxis training; first responder for vasovagal events at the collection station; escalates to the Venue Manager immediately for anything beyond standard protocol.

**Reporting structure:** Reports to the Venue Manager. No internal clinical-lead position exists at this venture; clinical protocol authority sits with the pathology partner.

**Position ID family:** PHL01-PHL04 (2 committed simultaneous, 4 recommended employment pool, 3 the disclosed lower bound).

## Massage+Beauty Treatment Staff, AM (MB01-MB06)

**Purpose:** Delivers pregnancy massage and pregnancy-safe facial/beauty services during the AM GTT window, one dual-qualified role, not two separate hires.

**Responsibilities:** Two 45-minute treatment blocks per AM client pair cycle; time-discipline within the fixed cadence; cross-covers within the Massage+Beauty pool as needed.

**Required qualifications:** Cert IV HLT42015 (pregnancy massage specialisation) and Diploma of Beauty Therapy, dual-qualification preferred and the confirmed hireable pairing for this role.

**Preferred qualifications:** Prior experience with pregnant clients specifically; comfort working within a fixed, non-negotiable 45-minute treatment block.

**Skills:** Pregnancy-safe technique adaptation across both modalities (massage and facial/beauty); time-discipline within fixed 45-minute AM service blocks.

**Customer-service expectations:** Warm, efficient service that respects the fixed treatment window; awareness that clients are mid-GTT and may be fasting or fatigued.

**Hygiene expectations:** Standard beauty-industry infection control; pregnancy-safe product awareness (internal induction, no external accreditation body specific to this venture).

**Safety expectations:** Awareness of pregnancy-safe positioning and product restrictions; escalates any client distress to the Venue Manager.

**AM role:** 07:00-13:00, staggered start (07:05 for the first pair of 2, 07:30 for the second pair of 2, per the solver-verified individual staff-lane output). Solver-confirmed peak concurrency of 4 at both 12 and 18 clients/day.

**PM role:** None under this position ID; a PM shift for the same qualified individual is a separate rostered position (PMM01 or PMB01), not the same headcount.

**Cross-training expectations:** Massage+Beauty dual-qualification is the confirmed cross-cover mechanism between these two services. Not cross-qualified with Nails or Hair.

**Reception-cover capability:** None during the AM window; the AM schedule has zero slack for a secondary reception duty.

**Emergency/first-aid expectations:** Standard first aid awareness; escalates to the Venue Manager.

**Reporting structure:** Reports to the Venue Manager.

**Position ID family:** MB01-MB04 (4 committed simultaneous), MB05-MB06 (recommended relief pool, 6 total recommended, 5 the disclosed lower-reliability alternative).
