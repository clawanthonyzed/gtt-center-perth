# Children & Companion Policy

**Certainty label for this entire document: MODELLED (proposed operational policy)** — grounded in the actual operating model, room capacity, and premium positioning already established elsewhere in this repo, but NOT a legally verified requirement. ## 1. Why This Policy Exists

The AM operating model runs a tightly synchronized, exact-timing clinical schedule (9 pairs of clients, `docs/architecture/OPERATING-MODEL-18-CLIENTS.md` §2) inside a fixed room footprint with a hard-walled clinical room (the Blood Collection Room, the only room requiring solid walls, `docs/floor-plan-concept.md`) and several curtain-partitioned or open-plan treatment/lounge spaces. Whether and how children and companions may accompany a client has real, direct consequences for: room capacity, supervision liability, the calm/premium experience the brand positioning depends on, and clinical privacy in the one genuinely clinical room. No existing document in this repository addressed this before this round — this is a genuine, previously-unaddressed gap, not a rewrite of prior policy.

## 2. Proposed Policy — Children Accompanying a Client

**PROPOSED, not verified:**
- A client may bring a child with her to her AM (GTT) or PM (wellness) appointment.
- **The GTT Lounge (35sqm, open-plan, 8 reclining/comfort chairs, `docs/floor-plan-concept.md`) is the appropriate waiting space for an accompanying child** while the client is in the Blood Collection Room or a treatment room — it is designed as a calm, non-clinical space and has the physical capacity for this.
- **A child may NOT enter the Blood Collection Room during a draw**, for two independent reasons: (a) the room is genuinely clinical (venepuncture, specimen handling) and privacy/safety-critical for the client; (b) the room's own footprint (18sqm, `docs/floor-plan-concept.md`) and the synchronized two-chair timing model (`OPERATING-MODEL-18-CLIENTS.md` §2) have no slack for an additional, unplanned occupant during the exact-timed draw sequence.
- **A child accompanying the client into a treatment room (Massage/Beauty/Nails/Hair) is a case-by-case Venue Manager decision**, not a blanket yes/no — curtain-partitioned rooms have limited additional floor space, and some treatments (massage specifically) are not compatible with a child present in the room. Proposed default: children remain in the Lounge during treatment services unless the specific service and room genuinely accommodate a child safely (e.g. a parent having a manicure with a child seated beside her at the nail station, an open-plan area).
- **Who supervises the child while the client is in the Blood Collection Room or a treatment room is the client's own responsibility, not the venue's** — this is a proposed liability boundary, not a confirmed legal position (see §5).

## 3. Proposed Policy — Companions/Support People

**PROPOSED, not verified:**
- A client may bring one adult companion/support person to her appointment.
- The companion may wait in the GTT Lounge for the full duration of the client's visit.
- **The companion may NOT enter the Blood Collection Room during a draw**, for the same two reasons as §2 (clinical privacy/safety, zero physical/scheduling slack) — **with one disclosed exception to flag for Anthony's decision, not resolved here:** whether a support person should be permitted in the room for a specific clinical reason (e.g. a client with a genuine needle phobia, per real informed-consent/support-person practice at many pathology collection services) is a real question this document does not resolve — flagged as a founder decision, not defaulted either way.
- A companion accompanying the client into a treatment room follows the same case-by-case Venue Manager judgment as §2 — most treatment rooms are sized for the client and the treating staff member only, not an additional adult.

## 4. Lounge/Waiting Behaviour

**PROPOSED, not verified:**
- The Lounge is a shared space among multiple simultaneous clients, companions, and children (given the synchronized 9-pair AM schedule, at any given moment several clients/companions may be present at once, `OPERATING-MODEL-18-CLIENTS.md` §2).
- Consistent with the premium/calm positioning (`docs/strategy/PREMIUM-POSITIONING.md`), the proposed expectation is quiet, non-disruptive use of the shared space — this is a hospitality norm to communicate at booking/arrival, not a strict enforced rule with a stated consequence (none proposed here).
- **Capacity implication, disclosed:** the Lounge has 8 reclining/comfort chairs for a schedule that can have up to 9 client-pairs (18 people) moving through in overlapping waves across the AM window, before any companions or children are added. At the busiest overlap points, the Lounge could plausibly be at or near capacity from clients alone — companions and children materially increase this risk. **This is a genuine, disclosed capacity tension, not solved by this document** — flagged for the Venue Manager to monitor once trading, and potentially for Anthony to decide whether a "no companions during peak AM" or similar rule is needed once real booking patterns exist.

## 5. What Requires Professional Confirmation — Explicitly Flagged, Not Invented

**None of the following legal/regulatory claims are made or assumed by this document — each is a genuine open question:**
- **Supervision liability:** whether the venue carries any legal duty of care toward an unsupervised child in the Lounge while the parent is in a treatment room or the Blood Collection Room, beyond ordinary occupier's liability for any commercial premises. **Requires solicitor confirmation.**
- **Insurance implications:** whether the public liability policy (`docs/architecture/FINANCIAL-ASSUMPTION-REGISTER.md` — currently MODELLED/BALLPARK-ESTIMATE, not yet a real quote) covers incidents involving accompanying children/companions in the same way as clients, or whether this needs disclosing to the insurer/broker specifically. **Requires insurer/broker confirmation as part of the quote process already in motion** (`docs/insurance-broker-quote-request-draft.md`).
- **Landlord/lease conditions:** whether any future lease imposes occupancy limits or child-specific conditions on the premises. **Requires confirmation once a venue is under negotiation** (`docs/strategy/PERTH-PROPERTY-SHORTLIST.md`).
- **WA health/safety regulation specific to children in a facility with a clinical room:** whether any WA regulation (beyond the already-researched WA Skin Penetration Code and the pathology partner's own Licensed Collection Centre requirements, both scoped to the Blood Collection Room specifically) imposes additional requirements when children are present on premises. **Not researched this round — genuinely unknown, not assumed absent.**
