## Enquiry Text (Ready to Send)

**Subject: Sourcing enquiry, commercial fit-out furniture and fixtures, Perth wellness venue (exploratory, no commitment)**

We are opening GTT Center Perth, Western Australia's first venue built around the mandatory Glucose Tolerance Test (GTT) wait, a routine pregnancy screening requiring a 2-3 hour on-site wait. The venue offers massage, beauty treatments, nail and hair services, and a cafe during that wait. Blood collection itself is provided by a pathology partner, not by us directly; we are sourcing the wellness/beauty/cafe fit-out only.

We are looking for an Australian-based sourcing partner for a defined set of China-manufacturable commercial furniture and fixture categories (below), with independent quality control and inspection built in from the start. This is an exploratory enquiry: we would like to understand your process, capability, and indicative engagement terms before committing to any order. No supplier, product, or price has been agreed with anyone at this stage.

**Categories we are exploring for China sourcing (grouped, with approximate scale):**

1. Reception/Cafe joinery-adjacent furniture (display shelving, 2 units)
2. Nail stations (4 nail tables with dust collectors, 4 tool trolleys)
3. Hair styling stations (4 hydraulic styling chairs, 4 tool trolleys; professional hair dryers/straighteners are excluded, sourced via an Australian direct-trade account for warranty reasons)
4. A custom-length lighted salon mirror (1 unit, dimensions pending final venue confirmation)
5. Decorative/ambient lighting (4 floor lamps plus optional feature lighting)
6. General furniture (Lounge tables, staff lockers, storage shelving)
7. Treatment furniture (a Beauty treatment bed, and possibly a Massage table pending an internal format decision)
8. Pedicure furniture (4 pipeless/no-jet pedicure spa chairs, our highest-priority category)
9. Decorative hardware (mirrors, planters, low value)
10. Other commercial fit-out items (tablet mounts, cable management, cleaning equipment, linen storage)

**Explicitly excluded from this enquiry (Australian-sourced, not part of any China sourcing request):** phlebotomy chairs, a refrigerated centrifuge (sourced via our pathology partner), sharps containers and biohazard waste equipment, TGA-listed disinfectant, an AED, and named-brand hair dryers/straighteners.

**What we would need from you at this exploratory stage:**

- Your typical engagement model (commission-based, flat project fee, or another structure) and indicative rate
- Your factory vetting process, and whether you can provide reference projects for hospitality/salon/wellness-sector commercial furniture specifically (not just general furniture)
- Your quality-control process: material/finish sample approval, pre-production approval, and independent pre-shipment inspection (which inspection provider you use, e.g. SGS, Bureau Veritas, QIMA, or your own)
- Typical lead times for the categories above
- Your freight/consolidation process and whether you provide a landed-cost estimate (factory price, packaging, inspection, agent fee, freight, insurance, customs duty, GST, local delivery) or only a factory price
- Whether you can supply Certificates of Origin where relevant for duty treatment
- Your warranty/replacement-parts policy for goods you source

**What we are not ready to do yet:** commit to an order, share final dimensions (our venue is not yet secured, so exact sizes for site-dependent items like the mirror are not final), or finalise a specific product for any category. We would like an initial scoping conversation to understand fit before going further.
