## 2. Proof-of-Funds — Exact Required Form

**This was already flagged as a genuine gap in `docs/architecture/COMMERCIAL-VALIDATION-FRAMEWORK.md` §4.3 — it does not exist anywhere in this repository. Named precisely here, not left as a vague action item:**

A landlord-credible proof-of-funds package for this venture, given its trust/trustee structure (YETI Tipi Holdings PTY LTD as trustee for YETI Holding Trust) and self-funded position (Anthony + Imara joint savings, per this empire's own standing context), should take **one or a combination of these three concrete forms:**

1. **A recent bank statement or bank-issued proof-of-funds letter** (within 30-60 days of use, the standard freshness window landlords/agents typically expect) showing available capital at or above the approved planning figure — currently **A$251,198** (`data/canonical/startup_costs.yml#adopted_planning_scenarios`, the founder-approved Revised Recommended Opening Strategy) or the broader bounded range (A$357,390-577,180) if a landlord wants to see coverage of the wider historical range — held either in the trust's own operating account, or in the personal joint-savings account that is the actual current source of funds.
2. **A solicitor or accountant letter confirming YETI Tipi Holdings PTY LTD's authority to execute a commercial lease as trustee for YETI Holding Trust** — a real, standard requirement for a trust-structured tenant that an individual sole-trader tenant would not need, and worth having ready before a landlord's own solicitor raises it as a condition.
3. **If funds are currently held personally (Anthony + Imara joint savings) rather than already inside the trust's own account, a short letter or statement describing the intended capital-injection pathway** — confirming the funds will be transferred into the trust structure before or at lease signing, not left ambiguous. This is the most likely real-world gap given this venture's own disclosed funding structure, and the one most worth preparing in advance rather than discovering mid-negotiation.

**None of these three currently exists in this repository.** Items 1 and 3 require Anthony's own direct action (a bank statement/letter request, a decision on capital-injection timing) — not something this repo's own documentation can produce. Item 2 requires the solicitor engagement already flagged elsewhere in this repo (`docs/financial-setup.md` STEP 1's accountant engagement is a related but distinct action — a solicitor confirming trustee authority is a separate, not-yet-scheduled task).

## 4. Landlord Pitch Structure — Built on the Existing Framework

`docs/architecture/COMMERCIAL-VALIDATION-FRAMEWORK.md` §4.3 already found landlord discussion "the closest of the three [audiences] to being ready" and flagged the sequencing tension between insurance timing (`docs/financial-setup.md` schedules it Week 10, after lease signing) and many landlords' own expectation of a certificate of currency before signing. **This document does not re-derive that finding — it sequences the actual pitch conversation using it:**

1. **Business overview + concept presentation together** (§1, §3) — establish what the venue is and how it will feel, before any numbers.
2. **Financial summary** (§1) — establish credibility and capacity to sustain the tenancy.
3. **Proof-of-funds** (§2) — the concrete evidence behind the financial summary's credibility claim.
4. **Proposed tenancy use + terms sought** — health/beauty use class, lease length (target 3yr + 3yr option per `docs/location-scouting.md`), rent-free/incentive period ask (target 3-6 months during fit-out), personal guarantee position (capped, not open-ended).
5. **Insurance/compliance readiness statement** — even before a policy is bound, a statement that quotes are in progress and a certificate of currency will be provided before or at signing, addressing the sequencing tension directly and proactively rather than leaving a landlord to raise it as a concern.

**This structure does not change any commercial term or figure already established elsewhere in this repo** — it is a presentation sequence only, built to lead with the venue's strongest, most landlord-relevant assets (concept and concrete numbers) before asking for anything.
