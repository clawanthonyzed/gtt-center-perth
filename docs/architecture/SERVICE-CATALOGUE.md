## 3a. Retail Brands — Real Justification (F12)

**Anthony's own question: how did Gaia/Weleda/Mustela become the default?** Researched 2026-08-16, real findings:

| Brand | What it offers | Popularity/relevance | AU availability | Wholesale availability | Fit with positioning |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **GAIA Skin Naturals** | Australian-made natural/organic baby and pregnancy skincare (moisturisers, eczema care) | Established Australian brand, sold via multiple AU retailers (Queen Bee, Soul Mothers, eBay AU) | Yes, Australian-made and stocked | **Confirmed wholesale program exists** — dedicated "Become a GAIA Stockist" page (gaiaskinnaturals.com/pages/wholesale) | Strong — Australian-made, baby/pregnancy-specific, matches this venture's own market exactly |
| **Weleda** | International (Swiss) natural/organic skincare, established pregnancy/baby range | Long-established global brand, widely available in Australian pharmacies/health stores | Yes, well-distributed in Australia | Wholesale/stockist channel exists (not independently re-verified this pass) | Good — well-known name adds credibility, though less distinctively "local" than GAIA |
| **Mustela** | French pediatric/maternity skincare brand, widely sold internationally | Very widely recognised globally, strong maternity-hospital presence | Yes, sold in major Australian pharmacies (Chemist Warehouse, Priceline) | Wholesale/stockist channel exists via Australian distributor (not independently re-verified this pass) | Good — high brand recognition may reduce differentiation vs. a more curated/local selection |
