# WDP Partnership Activation Plan

Status: current as of 2026-08-24. Prepared for the hypothetical that Carole confirms WDP is happy to partner with GTT Center Perth. Treats "happy to partner" as the start of structured commercial/clinical due diligence, not a final agreement. Nothing in this document has been sent to WDP or any other party; it is preparation only, per standing instruction not to draft, send, or initiate further WDP correspondence.

## 1. Post-Positive-Response Execution Sequence

**Day 1 (the day Carole says yes):** (A) Anthony thanks Carole and proposes a substantive follow-up call/meeting. (G) Internally, Anthony reviews the 3 phlebotomist-employment models below and forms a preferred starting position, not a final one. (A) Circulate the meeting agenda (Section 4) ahead of the call.

**Day 2:** (A) Confirm logistics for the substantive call. (G) Anthony and Reed align internally on which of the 20 open variables (Section 3) are must-resolve-before-venue vs can-resolve-in-parallel.

**Week 1:** (C) The substantive call/meeting happens (Section 4 agenda). (B) WDP provides its own position on employment model, room requirements, and commercial structure preferences. (J) Do not sign anything yet, not even non-binding heads of terms, until the call has actually happened.

**Week 2:** (B/C) Follow-up on any information WDP could not answer live. (G) Anthony makes the founder decisions genuinely unlocked by WDP's answers (e.g. which employment model to pursue, once WDP states its own position). (I) If a non-binding heads-of-terms/letter of intent is appropriate, legal review of that document happens before it is signed, not after.

**Week 3:** (D/E) Only once heads of terms exist does active venue selection intensify with WDP's minimum room requirements as a hard search criterion. (H) Supplier engagement (China sourcing agent, Australian suppliers) can proceed for non-venue-dependent items in parallel, unchanged from the existing procurement execution queues.

**Thereafter:** Standard opening sequence resumes (`docs/architecture/PROCUREMENT-OPENING-SEQUENCE.md`, `docs/architecture/OPENING-READINESS-EXECUTION-PLAN.md`), now with WDP's actual room/clinical requirements as a confirmed input rather than an assumption.

**Category key applied throughout:** A. Doable immediately after a positive response. B. Requires WDP information. C. Requires WDP commercial agreement. D. Requires a venue. E. Requires landlord approval. F. Requires professional sign-off. G. Requires founder decision. H. Requires supplier engagement. I. Requires legal review. J. Should NOT be done yet.

**What should NOT be done yet (J), stated explicitly:** no venue should be signed on the strength of "happy to partner" alone; no equipment should be purchased against an assumed employment model before WDP states its own position; no employment contract should be issued to a phlebotomist before the employment model (GTT/WDP/hybrid) is settled; no fit-out design should be finalised for the Blood Collection Room before WDP's own room-sign-off criteria are confirmed in writing.

## 5. Commercial Terms Checklist (Before Signing Anything With WDP)

Legal entity (GTT Center Perth's own trading entity, confirmed before signing, not invented, see `docs/employment-contract-template.md`'s same open flag), term, renewal, termination, exclusivity (does WDP require GTT not to host another pathology provider, and vice versa), rent/fee structure (per Section 3), outgoings, fit-out responsibility and cost split, equipment ownership (who owns the centrifuge and other Blood-Collection-specific equipment at the end of the agreement), maintenance responsibility, consumables responsibility, staff (per Section 2), payroll, superannuation, insurance (each party's coverage and any cross-indemnity), professional/clinical liability, clinical governance (per Section 4, item 9), pathology accreditation responsibility (confirmed as WDP's, not GTT's, consistent with the existing model), records and privacy responsibility, complaints/adverse-event process interface, medical waste responsibility, infection control responsibility, cleaning responsibility for the Blood Collection Room specifically, utilities, IT/booking-system interface (does WDP need any data feed from GTT's own Fresha system), branding, marketing (can GTT reference the WDP partnership in its own marketing), client/patient ownership (confirm the client relationship remains GTT's, WDP's role is the pathology service only), results ownership, revenue/invoicing (does WDP invoice Medicare/the client directly, or via GTT), refunds, cancellation policy interface, business continuity (what happens if either party needs to close temporarily), disaster recovery, termination transition plan (what happens to the room/equipment/staff if the agreement ends). **Every item above requires solicitor review before signature; none of these terms has been invented or assumed as agreed anywhere in this document.**

## 6. Sequencing to Avoid Premature Spending (WDP Says Yes, No Venue Yet)

Positive WDP intent, then confirm substantive commercial/clinical assumptions (Section 4's agenda), then establish a preferred phlebotomist-employment-model position (Section 2, informed by WDP's own stated position, not decided in isolation), then obtain a non-binding commercial understanding/heads of terms if appropriate (Section 5, solicitor-reviewed before signing even a non-binding document), then confirm minimum venue requirements (WDP's Blood Collection Room criteria, Section 4 item 8), then use those requirements as a hard search criterion during venue selection (Anthony's own task, not touched here), then secure a venue subject to appropriate conditions (e.g. subject to WDP's final room sign-off), then site assessment, then WDP's actual room sign-off, then professional approvals (electrical/plumbing/HVAC/accessibility/fire safety), then fit-out design, then procurement release, then recruitment (per whichever employment model was confirmed), then systems, then soft opening, then launch.

**Where money should NOT be committed yet, stated explicitly:** no venue deposit or lease signature based on "happy to partner" alone, without at least the Section 4 agenda having been worked through. No phlebotomy equipment purchase (centrifuge specifically) until WDP confirms whether it supplies this. No employment contract issued to a phlebotomist until the employment model is confirmed. No Blood Collection Room fit-out design finalised until WDP's own room sign-off criteria are in writing, not assumed from this document's own placeholder understanding of them.
