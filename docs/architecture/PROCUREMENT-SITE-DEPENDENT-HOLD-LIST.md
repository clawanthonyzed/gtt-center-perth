# Site-Dependent Procurement Hold List

Status: current as of 2026-08-23. Every item in `docs/architecture/MASTER-PROCUREMENT-SHOPPING-LIST.md` classified C. SITE DEPENDENT or D. PROFESSIONAL VERIFICATION REQUIRED is held here, with exactly what must be measured or confirmed once a venue is secured, rather than a guessed quantity. Nothing on this list should be purchased before the relevant measurement/confirmation happens.

## Lighting

**What must be measured/confirmed:** ceiling height, ceiling grid/mounting type, existing electrical points and circuit capacity, lux/design requirements per area (task vs ambient), emergency lighting exit-path requirements, switching zones. **Held items:** X01-X14 (Reception, Cafe, Blood Collection, Nail, Pedicure, Massage, Hair Wash, Toilets, Staff Areas, Storage, emergency, feature/decorative). **Who confirms:** an Australian lighting designer/electrician, post-venue.

## Joinery (Reception counter, Cafe cabinetry, custom fit-out elements)

**What must be measured/confirmed:** wall dimensions, existing services (power, data, plumbing) behind the joinery run, door/circulation clearances, accessibility clearances. **Held items:** B01 (Reception counter), C19 (Cafe preparation counter). **Who confirms:** the local trade/joiner engaged for fit-out, post-venue.

## Cafe Fit-Out

**What must be measured/confirmed:** plumbing (water supply/drain for the coffee machine and boiling water tap), drainage, power (dedicated circuit for the coffee machine), ventilation (if required for the coffee machine's own extraction needs), equipment clearances around the display fridge and prep counter. **Held items:** C04 (chilled/boiling water tap install), C19 (prep counter), C20 (handwashing station). **Who confirms:** licensed plumber and electrician, post-venue; WA food-business EHO for the final layout sign-off.

## Blood Collection Room

**What must be measured/confirmed:** room/wall dimensions against the growth-first 3-chair-ready design, handwash-station placement and plumbing, power/data points, clinical layout (chair positions, partition placement), accessibility clearance (900mm minimum wheelchair clearance), any WDP-specific observation/safety requirement for the confirmed floor plate. **Held items:** E24 (clinical sink fitout), E31 (room construction), E32 (partition placement), E36 (emergency call button wiring), E38 (ventilation, 6 ACH). **Who confirms:** Australian building/compliance professional and licensed hydraulic trade, post-venue; WDP for any site-specific collection-room requirement beyond the general standard.

## Nail Station Ventilation (LEV)

**What must be measured/confirmed:** ducting route to outside, WorkSafe WA pre-application requirements for the specific floor plate, whether per-station downdraft/capture-hood LEV is compatible with the confirmed open-plan layout. **Held items:** H06 (LEV unit). **Who confirms:** a WorkSafe-WA-familiar HVAC/extraction contractor (not yet identified, a genuine dependency separate from the site-measurement itself).

## Toilets

**What must be measured/confirmed:** final toilet count (patient WC accessible, patient WC standard, staff WC), fixture positions, AS 1428.1 accessibility clearances. **Held items:** N01-N16 in full (all consumable/fixture quantities are toilet-count-dependent). **Who confirms:** building surveyor for accessibility compliance, post-venue.

## General Electrical

**What must be measured/confirmed:** GPO count and placement per room against the confirmed floor plate, circuit capacity for higher-draw equipment (coffee machine, LEV unit, hair dryers/straighteners running simultaneously). **Held items:** A05, G14, H21, J18, K02. **Who confirms:** licensed electrician, post-venue.

## General Plumbing

**What must be measured/confirmed:** hot/cold water supply and drainage for the Cafe, Blood Collection Room hand-hygiene station, Hair Wash basins, Pedicure chairs. **Held items:** C04, E24, I02, K02, K04. **Who confirms:** licensed plumber, post-venue.

## HVAC

**What must be measured/confirmed:** existing plant capacity, Blood Collection Room's own 6 ACH ventilation requirement, general venue air conditioning servicing. **Held items:** A04, E38. **Who confirms:** HVAC contractor, post-venue.

## Signage

**What must be measured/confirmed:** external signage dimensions against the actual shopfront, internal wayfinding against the confirmed floor plate. **Held items:** A08, W01, W02, W16. **Blocked additionally by:** the final naming decision (Dossier Chapter 34), not just the venue.

## Furniture Final Counts

**What must be measured/confirmed:** Lounge couch count (3 vs 4, set by actual floor area), side table/coffee table counts to match. **Held items:** D01-D03. **Who confirms:** no professional required; a straightforward floor-plate measurement once a venue is secured.

## What this list does not do

It does not guess a quantity for any of the above ahead of a confirmed venue. Every item here is already fully specified in `docs/architecture/MASTER-PROCUREMENT-SHOPPING-LIST.md` in every dimension except the site-dependent one identified above.
