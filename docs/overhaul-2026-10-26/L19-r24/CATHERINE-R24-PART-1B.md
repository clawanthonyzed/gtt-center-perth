## Does anything change the "reasonably accessible" and "reasonable hours" answer (s 23DNG(1)(ba),(bb))?
**No.** None of the 19 pages or 8 PDFs uses either phrase (searched and read, 2026-10-27). The phrases remain a ground for revoking an approved collection centre's approval, which GTT will not hold (R23 PART-2, L: Health Insurance Act s 23DNG). File 05 (Approved Collection Centres) lists the approval rules and has no access or hours test.
One page needs a flag. File 09 says "Premises where specimens are collected must apply to be an Approved Collection Centre." It is a plain summary, not the law: the Act's collection list (s 16A(5AA)) also allows collection by the treating practitioner's employer's staff (para (c)(iv)), and the same Department's file 08 says "practice staff" can take samples. Advisory #2 Q18 (row 21) also shows a lab test keeping its benefit when practice staff collect. So the legal answer stands (about 90 percent), but the Department's own summary could be read the other way. That is the best reason to put the question to the compliance team in writing, after the generic introduction and a real reply (PART-2).
