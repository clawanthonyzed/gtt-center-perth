## Deliverable
**1. What is GTT Center Perth?** A calm lounge in Perth for the pregnancy glucose tolerance test. You have your blood draws and relax between them with a massage, beauty, nails or hair service.

**2. Do I need a referral?** Our first question is "Do you have a referral?". Either way, you can book.

**3. What is Path A?** You book your test, then a 20 minute consult with our midwife, who writes your request. Most clients choose this.

**4. What is Path B?** You use a request from your own doctor or midwife. There is no GTT consult. If you have an outside request you can still choose Path A.

**5. What does it cost?** Path A: A$320 (Package 1) or A$370 (Package 2). Path B: A$300 or A$350. Package 1 has two 30 minute services. Package 2 has two services of up to 45 minutes. Both include lounge access and approved drinks.

**6. What is the lab fee?** A$27.85 is included in those prices. You pay it to the lab, which bills you after it gets the paperwork and samples. GTT never handles that money.

**7. Will I get money back from Medicare?** On Path A you can claim about A$55.00 yourself (about A$32.95 for the consult and about A$22.05 for the lab test). On Path B a request from outside GTT gets no Medicare benefit for a test done here. Lawyer to check this wording.

**8. Do I need to fast?** Fasting hours: NOT IN REPO. We will give you the lab's exact instructions when you book.

**9. When should I arrive?** Please arrive at least 10 minutes before your appointment, because late means from your appointment time onwards and we cannot fit a first blood draw in after that. We have no wiggle room for draws. For a consult there is a 10 minute grace window: if you are up to 10 minutes late you are still seen for the time left.

**10. What are the 3 draws?** One blood draw to start, then two more after set waits. A service sits between draws. The gaps are set by the lab: NOT IN REPO.

**11. What if I am unwell?** Tell us as soon as you can. You can change your booking up to 48 hours before, and we send reminders. Refund rules: NOT IN REPO, lawyer to check.

**12. How does the waitlist work?** It is free to join and needs no deposit. When a spot opens, the list is emailed and the spot goes public. Inside 48 hours both happen at the same moment. First to book and pay gets it.

**13. How do I get my results?** The lab sends them to the midwife who wrote your request (Path A). If they are normal you get a short text or email. If not, the midwife phones you. If she cannot reach you she leaves a message to call us, and sends an email saying she has tried to reach you. On Path B results go to your outside doctor or midwife.

**14. Is my information private?** Yes. Our messages never contain health details. Our printed day list shows first names only and is locked away then shredded at the end of the day.
