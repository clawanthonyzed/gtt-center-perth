## Deliverable

1. **Who we are.** GTT is a glucose tolerance test lounge for expecting mothers in Perth. Business name and contact details: NOT IN REPO. Lawyer to check.
2. **Do you have a referral?** This is the first booking question. If yes, you can use your outside referral (Path B) or book the package with our midwife (Path A). Path A is our preferred option. If no, you book Path A.
3. **Prices.** Path A is advertised at A$320 (Package 1) or A$370 (Package 2), including the lab fee. At checkout GTT charges A$292.15 or A$342.15. After about A$55 in Medicare rebates your own cost is about A$265 or A$315. Path B is A$300 or A$350 including the lab fee, and GTT charges A$272.15 or A$322.15. An outside request gets no Medicare benefit for a test done at GTT. Lawyer to check.
4. **The lab fee.** The A$27.85 lab fee is paid by you straight to the lab after it receives your paperwork and sample. It is never GTT money.
5. **Booking order.** On Path A you book your test first, then your 20 minute consult with our midwife, usually 1 to 28 days before the test on a different day. A same-morning consult is an option only if it ends before your draw.
6. **Arriving on time.** Blood draws: Please arrive at least 10 minutes before your appointment, because late means from your appointment time onwards and we cannot fit a first blood draw in after that. We have no wiggle room for draws. Consults have a 10 minute grace window: if you are up to 10 minutes late you are still seen for the time left.
7. **Changes within 48 hours.** You can change or cancel up to 48 hours before your appointment. We send reminders. Inside 48 hours changes depend on our refund rules: NOT IN REPO. Lawyer to check.
8. **Waitlist.** Joining is free with no deposit. If a time opens up more than 48 hours ahead, the list is emailed first, all at once, and it goes on the public page after 48 hours. If a time opens inside 48 hours, it is offered to the list and made public at the same moment. First to book and pay gets it.
9. **If you are unwell.** Tell us you cannot come and give a short code only, for example "unwell". We will never ask for a diagnosis. We will help you rebook. Refund terms: NOT IN REPO. Lawyer to check.
10. **Privacy.** We keep only what we need to look after you. Emails and texts never contain health detail or results. Your record is kept on a secure computer system. Printed day lists are locked away and shredded at the end of the day. Full privacy policy: NOT IN REPO. Lawyer to check.

Your rights under Australian Consumer Law are not limited by these terms. Lawyer to check.
