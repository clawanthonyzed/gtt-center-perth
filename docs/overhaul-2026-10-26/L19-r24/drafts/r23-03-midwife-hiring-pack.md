### 2. Eight interview questions
1. Tell us about your last antenatal role and what you liked in it.
2. How do you explain a glucose tolerance test to a nervous client in 20 minutes?
3. How do you write a request for a test, and what do you check first?
4. A result comes back abnormal. Walk us through the phone call.
5. She does not answer. What do you do next? (Look for: voicemail asking her to call GTT, then a follow-up email saying you have tried to make contact regarding results, no result detail.)
6. How do you protect a client's privacy at a front desk and on the phone?
7. You run 25 minute blocks from 08:15 to 10:30. How do you keep to time and stay kind?
8. Can you work the fortnight block, and are you happy to take your 30 minute meal break as rostered, fully off duty or off site?

### 3. Position description
- Title: Endorsed Midwife. Employer: GTT Medical Services. Place: GTT lounge, Perth.
- Hours: on site 08:00 to 16:00 (8 hours), Monday to Saturday as rostered. Fixed daily wage, paid whether or not clients book.
- Duties: see Path A clients for a 20 minute consult and write her request; consult blocks run from 08:15 to 10:30 to match the draws; after 10:30 take normal midwife bookings; phone abnormal results in private; for normal results the team sends a generic text or email with no health detail; keep records on the secure computer.
- You do not see Path B clients by default: their results go to their outside doctor or midwife.
- Meal break: 30 minutes, rostered, never worked through. You choose to stay (fully off duty) or leave. You do not choose the time.
- Reports to: the venue manager for the roster. Clinical oversight: NOT IN REPO.
