Step 1. Do you have a referral? Two buttons, neither pre-selected.
- Button 1: No, I would like the GTT midwife to do my consult. Small print: package with a 20 minute midwife consult; full price A$320 (Package 1) or A$370 (Package 2); about A$55 may come back from Medicare. Goes to Path A.

- A client can switch between the two until she pays.

Path A (preferred)
- A1. Package: choose Package 1 or Package 2.
- A2. Test time: Monday to Saturday, nine pairs from 07:00 to 10:20. Pairs from 08:40 show "Midwife consult on the same morning possible".
