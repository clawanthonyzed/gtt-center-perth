## Deliverable

1. Arrival
- Please arrive at least 10 minutes before your appointment, because late means from your appointment time onwards and we cannot fit a first blood draw in after that.
- Blood draws have no wiggle room. Consults have a 10 minute grace window: a client up to 10 minutes late is still seen for the time left in her consult.
- The venue manager opens at 06:30, greets each client and reads any booking flags.

2. ID and request check
- Two ID checks: full name and date of birth.
- Path B: the venue manager checks the outside request is there (original, phone or photo), the name matches the booking and a date is on it. She does not judge whether it is medically right.
- The Path B client signs the Medicare notice before any draw. No signature means no draw.

3. Fasting check
- Fasting hours: NOT IN REPO, the partner lab decides. Water only. No food, gum, mints, juice, coffee or nicotine.
- If she is not fasted, do not start. The venue manager decides and she rebooks.
- Path A clients with a same-morning consult see the midwife first and stay fasted.

4. First draw and draw window
- Draws run in pairs every 25 minutes from 07:00.
- Draw 1 (fasting) takes about 5 minutes. Draw 2 is exactly 60 minutes after Draw 1. Draw 3 is exactly 120 minutes after Draw 1.
- The partner lab decides if any timing changes.

5. The drink
- After Draw 1 she drinks the 75 g glucose drink within 5 minutes.
- Drink contents beyond this: NOT IN REPO, the partner lab decides.

6. Timing marks
- Service 1 fits between Draw 1 and Draw 2. Service 2 fits between Draw 2 and Draw 3.
- Package 1 has 2 services of 30 minutes. Package 2 has 2 services of up to 45 minutes.
- A short buffer, then she is escorted back seated for the next draw.

7. Waiting area
- She stays in the lounge for the whole test. Low-activity services only. No leaving the premises.
- One staff member is responsible for her. If that person is on a rostered meal break (code MB), the venue manager names another person first.

8. Each draw
- Phlebotomists, employed by GTT Medical, do every draw.
- Every draw uses a grey-top (fluoride oxalate) tube.
- Path B: the phlebotomist checks the request is for the pregnancy glucose test and meets the lab's rules (what the lab accepts: NOT IN REPO), then ticks outside request checked in the computer record. A missing flag means no draw. If unsure, ask the midwife (on site from 08:00) or phone the lab.
- Staff never write, copy or fix a request.

9. No strenuous activity
- No exercise, long walks or long standing between Draw 1 and Draw 3.
- Whether she must stay in the lab's sightline: the partner lab decides.

10. Adverse events
- If she feels unwell, the responsible staff member calls the midwife (from 08:00) or a phlebotomist. In an emergency call 000.
- GTT staff give no clinical treatment. Emergency protocol: NOT IN REPO, lawyer to check.

11. Labelling
- Each tube is labelled at the bedside with name, date of birth, draw number and time.
- The phlebotomist checks each label before the tube leaves the collection area.

12. Transport
- Tubes go in the lab-approved transport box. The paper request travels with the sample.
- Pickup time, temperature and timing: the partner lab decides.

13. Departure
- She may leave only after Draw 3. A snack is offered straight away.
- The venue manager checks she has no outstanding flags before she leaves.

14. Results
- The lab sends Path A results to the requesting midwife. GTT does not take over diabetes care. Path B results go to the client's outside practitioner.
- Normal results: a generic text or email, no health detail.
- Abnormal results: the midwife phones. No answer: she leaves a voicemail asking the client to call GTT, then sends an email saying the midwife has tried to make contact regarding results. The email goes only if the client agreed at booking. No result detail anywhere. Lawyer to check.
