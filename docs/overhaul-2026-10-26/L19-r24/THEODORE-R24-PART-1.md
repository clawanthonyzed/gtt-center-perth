## 1. What the two groups do (one line each)

- **GST group:** Holdings, Medical and Lounge act as one business for GST. One member (the representative member) lodges one BAS for all. Sales between members are ignored: no GST, no tax invoice (V, [ATO GST groups](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/gst-excise-and-indirect-taxes/gst/registering-for-gst/gst-groups-and-branches/gst-groups), updated 14 Sep 2026).
- **Tax consolidated group:** Holdings (head company) lodges one income tax return for all three; a loss in one company offsets profit in another (V, [ATO Consolidation](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/corporate-tax-measures-and-assurance/consolidation)).
- **Payroll tax grouping is separate.** WA groups the three companies by law whatever we choose for GST or income tax (V, Part 2).
