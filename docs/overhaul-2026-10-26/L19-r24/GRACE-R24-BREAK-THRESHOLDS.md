| Nurses Award MA000034 (midwives) | 14.1(a) to (c) | In excess of 5 hours | Unpaid 30 to 60 minutes, taken between the 4th and 6th hour where reasonably practicable | On duty during the break: overtime until the break is taken. URLs: [MA000005](https://awards.fairwork.gov.au/MA000005.html), [MA000027](https://awards.fairwork.gov.au/MA000027.html), [MA000034](https://awards.fairwork.gov.au/MA000034.html).

Extra finding (V, MA000005 cl 16.1): hair and beauty staff also get PAID 10 minute rest breaks. A part-time or casual employee on a shift of 4 hours or more but under 7 gets one; on 7 hours or more, two (full-time: two). These count as time worked. Payroll should allow for them in the roster. This is separate from the unpaid meal break.

## 3. The contract rule (LOCKED Round 24)
Put the written 30 minute break agreement into a hair or beauty contract only if that employee's shift is long enough to be entitled to the break (more than 5 hours of work). On the repo shapes that is the morning service staff (on 6 hours on site) and the afternoon casuals. A hair or beauty employee whose shift is 5 hours or less gets no break, so no agreement clause is needed. If the morning shift is later cut to 5 hours or less, remove the clause for that contract. Nurses and phlebotomist contracts need no shortening agreement because their award already allows 30 minutes.
