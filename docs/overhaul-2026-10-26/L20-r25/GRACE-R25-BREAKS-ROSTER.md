## 2. If a service staff member is needed during a break
1. Only if a client or draw cannot be covered any other way. The venue manager asks; the staff member can say yes.
2. The minutes worked are PAID. Code MB-R with the minute called, the minute finished, who asked and why (no client detail).
3. The break restarts in full once the job is done (30 minutes for lunch, 10 for a rest).
4. Pay rate for MB-R under MA000005: NOT IN REPO, payroll to confirm.
5. Two MB-R codes for the same slot in a week means the roster is wrong; change the roster.
Midwife needed urgently on her break: she is not called. The venue manager follows the incident steps (docs/operations-manual.md section 6) and rings emergency services if it is an emergency. Normal appointments are never booked into the break.
