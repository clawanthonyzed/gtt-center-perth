## Answer in one line
GTT may keep a fair, clearly stated part of the lounge amount when a client cancels, misses or is late for her draws inside 48 hours, but never the money for anything not delivered when the reason is illness, a pregnancy problem, clinical advice or GTT's own failure, and it may never say "no refunds".

## Can GTT keep half the lounge amount for a late or missed test (the R20 line)?
**Yes, probably lawful, lawyer to check.** GTT's legitimate interest: a fasting morning slot with paid staff (phlebotomist, service staff) that cannot be resold at short notice. Half the lounge amount (A$81.08 Package 1, A$106.08 Package 2; R, THEODORE-R20 section 6) is well short of the full amount, which helps. Two balancing conditions make it much safer: (1) the same terms give her a full refund or free rebook when GTT or the lab fails, and (2) GTT refunds the kept half if it fills her slot from the waitlist (no double payment for one slot). "Late" must be measured by the clock, not "at our discretion" (ACL s 25 example, V).

**Deposits and "non-refundable":** do not use the word "non-refundable" anywhere. Say what is kept and when. GTT takes full payment at booking (R); no separate deposit is needed.

## Recommended policy table (lawyer to check)
| Situation | GTT may keep | GTT must refund or offer |
|---|---|---|
| Change or cancel 48 hours or more before | Nothing | Full refund of what she paid GTT, or a free move |
| First move of a booking inside 48 hours (any reason) | Nothing | One free move to a new date |
| Cancel inside 48 hours, or a second move | Half the lounge amount (A$81.08 / A$106.08). The consult fee only if the consult took place | The rest. If the slot is filled from the waitlist, the kept half too |
| Late for or misses her first draw (no wiggle room) | Half the lounge amount; the consult fee if the consult took place | The other half of the lounge amount. Rebooking is a new booking |
| Late more than 10 minutes for, or misses, her consult | No consult fee (none delivered) | Rebook the consult before her test date at no fee. If no consult can happen, treat as "cancel inside 48 hours" |
| Unwell, pregnancy complication, hospital, labour, or her doctor, midwife or our midwife advises against the test | Only the consult fee if the consult took place | Free rebook or full refund of everything not delivered, her choice. Short code only, no diagnosis, no certificate |
| GTT, its staff or the lab cannot run the session | Only the consult fee if the consult took place | Free rebook or full refund of everything not delivered, her choice |
| Test stopped on the day for a clinical reason (for example vomiting the drink) | Services already received | Free rebook of the draws |
| A draw or service done without due care and skill | Nothing for the faulty part | Fix it or refund it (ACL) |

The lab fee is the lab's own matter: GTT never holds it (R).

## Exact client-facing wording (booking page and confirmation; lawyer to check)
> **Changes and cancellations.** You can change or cancel free of charge up to 48 hours before your appointment, and we send reminders. Inside 48 hours you can move your booking once at no cost. If you cancel inside 48 hours, move it a second time, or miss or are late for your first blood draw, we keep half of your lounge and service amount (A$81.08 for Package 1, A$106.08 for Package 2) and refund the rest. If we fill your time from the waitlist, we refund that half too. Your consult fee is refunded unless your consult has already taken place.
>
> **If you are unwell or your care changes.** If you are unwell, have a pregnancy complication, are in hospital or in labour, or a doctor or midwife advises you not to have the test, tell us as soon as you can. You only need to say "unwell" or "advised not to". You choose a free rebook or a full refund of anything not yet provided.
>
> **If we cannot go ahead.** If we, our staff or the lab cannot run your session, you choose a free rebook or a full refund of anything not yet provided.
>
> These terms do not limit your rights under the Australian Consumer Law.
