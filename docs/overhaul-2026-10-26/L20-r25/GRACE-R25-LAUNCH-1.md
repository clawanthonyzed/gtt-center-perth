## 1. The verdict

**NO. GTT cannot open by 27 December 2026. Confidence about 95 percent (E).**

One sentence reason: today there is no venue, no partner lab, no registered company and no hired endorsed midwife, and the two longest chains (lab agreement and premises) have not started, because labs wait for a real government reply (AskMBS says usually within 15 working days, so about 16 November, R CATHERINE-R24-PART-2) and no site has been inspected (R ITEMS-TO-CONFIRM ITC-01, BLOCKING).

This is not a dead end. It is a timing problem. The 2 months can be used to finish everything that does not need a building, so the moment a site and a lab land, only the physical chain is left.
