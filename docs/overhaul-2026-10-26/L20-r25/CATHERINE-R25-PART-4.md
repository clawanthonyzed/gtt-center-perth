## ITEM 4A: First aid

**Answer: Anthony's plan (first aid course for all staff) is lawful and above the minimum. The certificate must come from a registered training organisation (RTO). A GTT midwife cannot issue it, but she can co-run the on-site day with the RTO trainer and run in-house practice drills between renewals.**

| Question | Answer | Source |
|---|---|---|
| What the law requires | The business must provide first aid equipment, access to it, access to facilities, and "an adequate number of workers are trained to administer first aid" (WHS (General) Regulations 2022 (WA) r 42, as summarised in the Code) | V WorkSafe WA Code of Practice "First aid in the workplace" (2022), https://worksafe.wa.gov.au/publications/code-practice-first-aid-workplace (PDF 221173_cp_firstaid.pdf, read 2026-10-27) |
| Must a first aider be on site at all times? | No fixed rule. The Code recommends "one first aider for every 50 workers" in low risk workplaces and asks the business to allow for shifts and "large numbers of other people present". GTT has pregnant clients, needles and fainting risk, so **one trained first aider on site whenever GTT is open** is the safe reading | V Code, section 3.5 |
| What training counts | "nationally recognised statement/s of attainment issued by a registered training organisation (RTO) for the nationally endorsed first aid unit of competency Provide First Aid or a course providing equivalent skills" | V Code |
| How often | "Refresher training in CPR should be carried out annually and first aid qualifications should be renewed every three years" | V Code |
| Can a midwife issue the certificate? | No, unless she delivers it on behalf of an RTO that has the unit on its scope. Only RTOs issue nationally recognised statements of attainment | V-partial, ASQA "What is an RTO?" (legacy page, via search, 2026-10-27); lawyer to check |
| Award cost | Hair and Beauty Industry Award 2020 cl 20.3: "An employer must pay a first aid allowance of $14.55 per week to an employee who: (a) has an appropriate current first aid qualification; and (b) is appointed by the employer to perform first aid duty" | V https://awards.fairwork.gov.au/MA000005.html (rate from 1 Jul 2026) |

**Compliant way to do it:**
1. Book an external RTO to deliver Provide First Aid (with CPR) on site for all staff in one session before opening. Course price: NOT IN REPO (get 2 quotes).
2. A GTT midwife co-facilitates the pregnancy parts (fainting at a draw, a client unwell after the glucose drink). No certificate from her.
3. Every year: CPR refresher by the RTO. Between times the midwife may run short practice drills, logged, not certified.
4. **Appoint** named first aiders on the roster (at least one each shift). Only appointed staff under the Hair and Beauty Award get the A$14.55 a week allowance; training everyone does not trigger it (V, cl 20.3). Write in each contract whether the person is appointed.
5. First aid kit, emergency phone list and 000 procedure at the front desk.

## ITEM 4B: Police checks

**Answer: Only midwives are checked by law (through Ahpra registration). For everyone else a National Police Certificate is the employer's choice. It is reasonable for every role here because staff are alone with pregnant clients and see health information, but GTT must not refuse someone because of a spent conviction.**
