## ITEM 5: Why GTT Lounge signs, not GTT Holdings

**Answer: GTT Lounge signs the lease and gives GTT Medical a written licence for the clinical rooms. Holdings stays a clean parent that only owns shares.** (Matches the Financial Center of Truth 4A: Lounge "holds the lease and employs the venue manager", R.)

In plain words:
1. **Ring-fencing.** A lease is the biggest promise GTT makes (years of rent). The company that signs carries it. If Holdings signs, the parent that owns both businesses carries the rent, so a bad year puts the whole group at risk. If Lounge signs, the risk stays in the company that uses most of the floor.
2. **Who uses the premises.** Lounge runs the lounge, the services and the front desk, and employs the venue manager (R). Medical uses the consult room and two collection rooms under a licence from Lounge, paying a market room fee (R, amount NOT IN REPO).
3. **Keep Medical clean.** Medical bills Medicare-linked consults, employs the midwives and phlebotomists (R) and carries the clinical claims risk. If it also held the lease, a clinical claim and a rent dispute would hit the same company. Medical should hold as few big contracts as possible.
4. **GST.** Inside a GST group "the representative member ... can also claim all the GST credits to which each group member is entitled", and "transactions you conduct with other group members are ignored for GST purposes" (V ATO GST groups page, crawl4ai 2026-10-27). So with the Year 1 GST group (locked) the room fee from Medical to Lounge carries no GST, and the rent credits are claimed for the group whoever signs. GST is therefore NOT the main reason. Before the group exists, Lounge (which makes taxable sales) can claim the credits on rent; a Holdings with no sales may not be able to register at all (U, accountant to check). All members are "jointly and separately responsible" for the group's GST (V); an indirect tax sharing agreement can limit that (V).
5. **Landlord guarantees.** A landlord will likely ask for a personal guarantee from the directors, and maybe a bank guarantee. Negotiate: (a) a bank guarantee or bond of a set number of months' rent instead of a personal guarantee; (b) if a personal guarantee is given, cap it (for example 6 to 12 months' rent, E) and end it after 2 years of rent paid on time; (c) release on assignment. Lawyer to check.
6. **The licence.** The lease must allow (a) a licence to a related company without needing fresh landlord consent each time, and (b) a permitted use covering a day spa, beauty services and a medical and pathology collection clinic. The licence states the rooms, hours, room fee, who insures what, and that it ends with the lease.

If a second location comes, each site's Lounge can sign its own lease. Commercial lease lawyer to check, including whether the Commercial Tenancy (Retail Shops) Agreements Act 1985 (WA) applies (U).
