### Who it binds (page numbers are the printed numbers on the pages)
| Part | Binds | Why (quote, page) |
|---|---|---|
| Whole document | The partner lab (an accredited pathology practice) | "Pathology practices that provide Medicare-funded pathology services are required to comply" (p 8) (V) |
| Whole document | Not GTT by law | GTT runs no lab and is not an approved collection centre. Other "healthcare services that provide pathology services should also apply" it as best practice (p 8). "Should", not "must" (V) |
| Standards 1 to 5, 8, 10, 11 (governance, lab supervision, devices, testing, point of care) | Lab only | All written to "the pathology practice" and its laboratories (V) |
| Standard 9 (specimen collection) | The lab, and in practice GTT | "These requirements apply to pathology services responsible for specimen collection" (p 47). GTT Medical's phlebotomists collect the sample, so the lab will pass these rules to GTT through its collection manual and its acceptance terms (E) |
| Standard 7.02 (identification) | The lab, and GTT staff at the chair | At least three patient identifiers on the record and the specimen; the client checks the labels if she can (p 38) (V) |
| Standard 6.02 (consent, out of pocket costs) | The lab | Consent to collection; "transparent financial information including about out of pocket expenses ... prior to specimen collection" (p 35) (V). GTT's booking page already shows the lab fee (R) |
| Glossary, "Specimen collector" | Hiring rule for GTT Medical | "must hold, at a minimum, a Certificate III in Pathology Collection or have equivalent experience and competency" (p 68 to 69) (V) |
