## 3. Rules that shape the sizes
- **NPAAC (pathology collection centres, third edition 2013, Tier 3B)** ([PDF](https://www.safetyandquality.gov.au/sites/default/files/2026-04/tier_3b_requirements_for_medical_pathology_specimen_collection_third_edition_2013.pdf)): gives NO square metre figure. It says collection areas need "adequate space for furniture to enable patients to be seated or to be recumbent ... as well as a work surface" (C1.1(ix)), a secure supply store only staff can open (C1.1(iv)), non-porous floors in collection and storage areas (C1.1(v)), and toilet doors that open outward or can be removed (C1.1(vii)). V. So size comes from AusHFG and the lab's own site inspection (repo: lab inspects before first collection).
- **Accessibility:** doorways are planned at 850 mm clear opening to AS 1428.1 (consultant and parliamentary summaries, secondary; AusHFG's consult room sheet specifies a 900 mm clear door, V). The National Construction Code Part D3 text and AS 1428.1 itself were NOT read (ABCB pages did not load; the standard is paid). U. The architect and building certifier confirm.
