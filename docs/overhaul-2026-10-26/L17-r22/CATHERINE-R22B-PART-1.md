## Verdicts (one line each)

| Job | Verdict |
|---|---|
| 1. Anthony as venue manager | **High risk to his Special Rate Disability Pension (SRDP).** The test is whether he is ABLE to do more than 10 hours a week of remunerative work (MRCA s 199(1)(d), s 209(b)). Unpaid work for his own profit-making company is not treated as voluntary work in DVA policy, and a wage is not needed. A 06:30 to 15:00 roster is about 42.5 hours a week, four times the line. Safe route: owner and director duties only, logged and kept well under 10 hours a week, with a paid venue manager (already in the financials). DVA or lawyer to confirm. |
| 2. Midwife participating status | **Hire endorsed midwives only.** Participating midwife = an "eligible midwife" (HIA s 3), which means endorsed by the Nursing and Midwifery Board (Regs 2018 s 66). She then needs a Medicare provider number for the GTT location (HPOS online or form HW088; HW093 is the wrong form), professional indemnity insurance, and private practice. An employed midwife at a private company can have the consult charged by GTT Medical on an account naming her and her provider number. No collaborative arrangement needed since 1 Nov 2024. |
| 3. Illness and complications rules | **Lawful within limits:** ask for a short statement, never a diagnosis; keep health detail off timesheets and lounge-visible notes; treat pregnancy complications as an exception, not a breach (pregnancy discrimination); a no-exceptions clock rule is an unfair-term risk; a 5 to 10 minute consult grace is lawful if written as a rule, not a whim. |
| 4. Path B notice and receipt | **Lawful with the s 23DNL notice in writing before the draw, to the client AND the lab.** Theodore's Option 3 receipt (honest zero consult line) is lawful; any invented consult or GST-free line would mislead. The lab's private fee for Path B must be confirmed in writing, or "all in" may be false. |
| 5. Privacy: results messages, cards, day list | **Lawful with changes:** ask each client at booking how she may be contacted and whether a message may be left; voicemail and email say only "please call us", never "results" or "midwife"; the day list is health information (it lists clients of a pregnancy test service), so locked, counted and shredded the same day with a log. |

## Job 1. Anthony as administrator and venue manager: the law

### 1.1 The statute (V: MRCA Compilation 77, library copy MRCA-SRDP.md)

| Section | Words | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| s 199(1)(d) eligibility | "the person is unable to undertake remunerative work for more than 10 hours per week, and rehabilitation is unlikely to increase the person's capacity to undertake remunerative work" | V |
| s 209(b) stopping | The Commonwealth "is no longer liable to pay" SRDP if the Commission "is satisfied that ... the person is able to undertake remunerative work for more than 10 hours per week" | V |
| s 197 outline | "A person who works more than 10 hours per week stops receiving the Special Rate Disability Pension" | V |
| s 200(3) | A person no longer paid under s 209 "is taken not to have chosen" SRDP (so he reverts to incapacity payments) | V |
| Definition | "Remunerative work" is NOT defined in the Act (library check: no s 5 entry) | V |
| s 405 | The Commission may give a written notice requiring him to report a specified change; failing to comply is an offence, 10 penalty units, strict liability | V |

**Key point:** the test is about **ability**, not pay and not only actual hours. Actual hours worked are the best evidence of ability.

### 1.2 DVA policy (CLIK, library copies, fetched 2026-10-19)

| Source | Words | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| Policy Instruction No. 4 (historical, last amended 3 Feb 2009) | Remunerative work is "work of a nature capable of attracting remuneration". "Unpaid work for family, friends, or a business enterprise formed for the purpose of making a financial profit is not classified as voluntary work." | S (historical policy) |
| Same instruction | "The receipt of salary or wages, however, is not a prerequisite to determining that an injured member or former member has an ability to earn" | S |
| CLIK 13.06 | "Re-assessment of SRDP should not be actively pursued unless the person has returned to remunerative work for more than 10 hours per week." | S |
| CLIK 13.06.1 | "Where a person obtains employment for more than 10 hours per week their payments will revert to incapacity payments"; if the work "proves to be unsuitable or unsustainable, the person should not be deemed with an ability to earn"; "A new offer for SRDP should be made if the person again meets the SRDP eligibility criteria." | S |
| CLIK 13.06.2 | Ceasing SRDP "will not remove eligibility for the ancillary benefits, such as the 'TPI' embossed Gold Card" | S |
