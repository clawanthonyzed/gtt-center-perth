## 2.1 Plain answers

| Question | Answer | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| What counts as remunerative work? | Any work "of a nature capable of attracting remuneration": work someone would normally be paid to do. Reception, opening up, printing welcome cards and day lists, rostering, timesheets, deciding late and illness cases, supplier calls and admin all qualify, because GTT pays a venue manager to do them (repo venue manager line, A$8,504.76 a month loaded, R per Theodore R22). | S, U |
| Does unpaid work for his own company count? | **Very likely yes.** DVA policy says unpaid work for "a business enterprise formed for the purpose of making a financial profit is not classified as voluntary work", and pay "is not a prerequisite" to an ability to earn. Taking no wage does not protect him. | S |
| Do dividends count? | No. Route B franked dividends are investment income, not work (locked Founder Rule, 2026-10-18). But no CLIK text covers dividends or directors (library gap Q14), so if he works full time unpaid, DVA could ask whether the dividend is really pay for his work. **Lawyer to check.** | U |
| Do director duties count? | Possibly. A director can be paid fees, so director work is "capable of attracting remuneration". Count it in the 10 hours to be safe. | U |
| How are hours counted? | The Act says "more than 10 hours per week" and gives no counting method. Safest reading: every hour of work for the business, on site, at home or on the phone, counted week by week, not averaged. Travel and thinking time: not settled. **DVA to confirm.** | V (words), U (method) |
| Can it be evidence even under 10 hours? | Yes in theory, because the test is ability. In practice CLIK 13.06 says reassessment "should not be actively pursued" unless he returns to work over 10 hours a week. | S |

## 2.2 Risk of an 8.5 hour day, Monday to Friday

| Item | Effect | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| Hours | 06:30 to 15:00 = 8.5 hours a day, about 42.5 a week. Day 2 alone crosses 10 hours. | maths |
| Legal result if DVA learns of it | The Commission can be "satisfied" he is able to work over 10 hours a week (s 209(b)). SRDP stops; he is "taken not to have chosen" it (s 200(3)) and reverts to incapacity payments worked out as normal earnings less what he is able to earn (CLIK 13.06). | V, S |
| Gold Card | Kept: ceasing SRDP does not remove the "TPI" embossed Gold Card (CLIK 13.06.2). | S |
| Reporting | If DVA has given him a s 405 notice listing work as a change to report, not reporting is an offence (10 penalty units) and overpaid money can be recovered as a debt. Whether his notices list work: **check his DVA letters.** | V, U |
| Visibility | A public venue, staff, a roster and a welcome card naming "[venue manager first name]" put his role on paper. Assume DVA could see it. | U |
| Overall | **High risk. Not recommended.** | U |

## 2.3 What he can safely do (all DVA or lawyer to confirm)

| Approvals: publishing approvals (Publishing Shield), hiring decisions | Short, occasional | Count it |
