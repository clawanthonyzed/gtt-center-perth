## 1. The late rule: exact words (use word for word everywhere)
> **"Please arrive at least 10 minutes before your appointment, because late means from your appointment time onwards and we cannot fit a first blood draw in after that."**
Places: booking page (tick box above pay), terms, confirmation, reminders, arrival message, follow-up.
Why no wiggle room for the test: the first draw is the start of a two hour clock. Draw 2 is exactly 60 minutes after Draw 1 and Draw 3 exactly 120 minutes after (R). Draws run in pairs every 25 minutes, and two services must fit between the draws. A late first draw breaks the next pair and the services. No published pathology centre late rule was found (Reed, V), so this is GTT's own rule.
Consult sentence, added only where the consult is booked (Catherine's wording):
> **"For a midwife consult, if you are up to 10 minutes late we will still see you and the consult ends at its normal time; if you are later than that, we will rebook you."**
Why 10 minutes: a 25 minute block less 10 leaves 15, still above the Medicare 10 minute minimum for the short visit, item 82105 (V, Reed). Catherine prefers a fixed number to "if the midwife can finish", because a rule only GTT can judge is a consumer-law risk. The midwife may still rebook free if a shortened consult would not be safe or complete (a clinical reason, not a penalty). A same-morning consult still ends before her draw; the test slot never moves.
