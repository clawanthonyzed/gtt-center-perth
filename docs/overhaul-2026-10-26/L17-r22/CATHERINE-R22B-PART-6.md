## 6.1 The rules that apply

| Rule | Words | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| Privacy Act s 6FA | Health information includes information about a person's health and "other personal information collected to provide, or in providing, a health service" | V (R20 library) |
| APP 6.1 | Use or disclose only for the purpose collected, unless she consents or an exception applies | V (R20 library) |
| APP 11.1, 11.2 | Reasonable steps against "unauthorised access ... or disclosure"; destroy or de-identify when no longer needed | V |
| OAIC APP guidelines 11.45, 11.50 | Destruction methods include "shredding, disintegrating and pulping" (11.45); for hard copy, "disposal through garbage or recycling collection would not ordinarily constitute taking reasonable steps" unless already shredded or pulped (11.50) | V (OAIC chapter 11, fetched 2026-10-26) |

## 6.2 Abnormal results: phone, voicemail, email, SMS

**Fix first: ask at booking (Path A).** One question with options lets every later message rely on her consent (APP 6.1(a)):
> How may our midwife contact you about your results? ☐ Call my mobile ☐ You may leave a voicemail asking me to call back ☐ You may email me ☐ You may text me. Your mobile: [number] (please check it).

| Channel | May say | Must not say | Tag |
|---|---|---|---|
| Phone, answered | Midwife first confirms identity (full name and date of birth) before any detail | Any detail before identity is confirmed | U |
| Voicemail (only if she ticked it) | "This message is for [first name]. This is [midwife first name] from [business name]. Please call us back on [number]. Thank you." | "results", "glucose", "pregnancy", "midwife", "abnormal", "urgent about your test" | U |
| Follow-up email (only if ticked) | Subject: "Please call us". Body: "Hello [first name], [midwife first name] from [business name] tried to call you today and will try again later today. Please call us on [number] when you can. Regards." | "results" (Grace's draft says "about her results": remove it), any test name, any health word in subject or preview | V (APP 11.1), U |
| SMS (only if ticked) | Same as the email body, short | Same | U |
| Normal results | A generic message only if she ticked email or SMS: "Your midwife has reviewed your recent visit and no action is needed. Call [number] with any questions." | Values, diagnosis | U |

**Clinical safety step (midwife's call, not mine):** if she still cannot be reached, the midwife's written escalation (for example a further call next morning, then a letter) belongs in the clinical protocol. Abnormal results cannot wait on a privacy rule; the rule only controls what is said, not whether GTT keeps trying (U).

**Path B:** results go to her outside practitioner, so GTT makes no results call at all.

## 6.4 Printed day list

| Rule | What the venue manager does |
|---|---|
| Content | First name and surname initial, Draw 1 time, staff first names. Nothing else (Grace PART-4 section 6 agreed) |
| Copies | One master plus only the copies staff need. Number each copy |
| Print | Work printer only, collected at once; no home printing, no photos of the list |
| Store | Closed folder in the locked front desk drawer overnight; face down or covered at the desk |
| Destroy | Shred every copy and unused card at the end of the test day, cross-cut shredder or locked shred bin (OAIC 11.45). Never the recycling bin (11.50) |
| Log | One line: date, copies printed, copies shredded, initials. Counts must match |
| Loss | A missing list is a possible data breach: tell Grace the same day for the 30-day assessment (Privacy Act s 26WH, R21) |
| Who | Lounge staff use booking data only, never the clinical record (s 13B limit on sensitive information) |
