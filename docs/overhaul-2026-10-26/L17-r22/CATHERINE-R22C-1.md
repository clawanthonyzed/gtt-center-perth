What it is: a "Tier 2" document. It sets the minimum standards a pathology LAB must meet to be accredited (and so to get Medicare benefits). Lines marked "S" are mandatory; "C" lines that say "must" are also mandatory.

Important limit: this document has NO rule on fasting times, the oral glucose drink, waiting or rest times between draws, or a GTT protocol. Those sit in each lab's own collection manual. The room rules sit in a separate NPAAC document (section 4 below).

## 2. Who is responsible: the lab, not GTT
The lab (its "Approved Pathology Authority") holds the approval, the "designated person" (a medical practitioner, S4.5), the quality manager (S5.2) and the accreditation. GTT provides the room. So most rules below are things the lab will ask GTT to support in the room agreement.

## 3. What matters for GTT (read 2026-10-26, V)

| Topic | Rule (plain English) | Ref |
|---|---|---|
| Client wellbeing | The client's wellbeing and rights come first | S2.1 |
| Information to client | Client must be able to get information about the test process, costs and when results come | C2.1 |
| Open disclosure | Lab must have a policy to talk openly with a client after a safety incident | S2.2 |
| Commercial influence | Lab must manage commercial or financial influences and conflicts of interest (relevant to any GTT and lab money link) | C2.4(i), (ii) |
| Consent | Consent is inferred when the client lets the collection happen; consent "should" include informed financial consent | C2.5(iii), (iv) |
| Privacy | Privacy and confidentiality kept at all times | S2.6 |
| Premises | Premises clearly identified and suitable; enough designated space for safe specimen collection | S7A.1, S7A.2 |
| Collection rooms | Approved Collection Centres must meet the NPAAC collection centre guidelines | C7A.1 |
| Request | Testing only on a documented request naming the client, requester and tests | SA8.4 |
| Direct to consumer | If a lab offers direct-to-consumer testing it must sit in a documented risk framework | CA8.4(iii) |
| Identity | 3 identifiers on the request; 2 (or 3) on the tube; tube labelled in front of the client; collector's identity traceable | CA8.5(ii) to (vii) |
| Records | Lab records include date and time of collection and "clinical status of patient (e.g. fasting), where required" | SA8.6(c), (g) |
| Handling | Specimens traceable at all times; packed and moved so they stay intact | SA8.2, SA8.8 |
| Electronic records | Systems protected from unauthorised access or change | S7B.6 |
| Critical results | Lab needs a critical result policy; if the doctor cannot be reached, every effort to contact the client | S6.2, CC8.3(iv) |
| Results to clients | Lab needs documented procedures before giving results straight to clients | SC8.5 |
| Retention | Records kept under the NPAAC retention requirements | SC8.6 |

## 4. Companion document: collection room rules (already in the library)
"Guidelines for Approved Pathology Collection Centres (Requirements for medical pathology specimen collection)", Third Edition 2013, Tier 3B. Library copy: /opt/openclaw/second-brain/knowledge/legal/npaac/NPAAC-COLLECTION-CENTRES-GUIDELINES-3RD-2013.md (fetched 2026-10-19; I re-read sections 1 to 6 on 2026-10-26). Key points for GTT rooms (V):
- Approval certificate visibly displayed; hours of operation displayed (C1.1(i), (viii)).
- Easily cleanable work surfaces; non-porous floor in collection and storage areas; secure staff-only supply storage (C1.1(iii) to (v)).
- Room big enough for the client to sit or lie down as needed, plus a work surface (C1.1(ix)).
- Toilet doors lockable inside, openable from outside in an emergency (C1.1(vii)).
- Staff wear identification; staff trained in basic first aid; privacy and consent policies (S3.1, S3.3, C3.3(ii)).
- Hand wash or alcohol rub plus gloves; client told about the procedure and given after-care instructions (S5.1 to S5.3).
- Specimens never stored with food, drink or medicines; fridge 2 to 8 degrees if needed (C2.2(ii), C2.1(i)). Note for the lounge: keep tubes away from the kitchen and herbal tea area.
