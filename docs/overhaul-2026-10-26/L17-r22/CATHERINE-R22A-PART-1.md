| 2(a) Fair Work Act s 45 | **Every Nurses Award term is enforceable law.** Up to 60 penalty units (600 if serious), 5 times for a company; the venue manager can be liable too (s 550); with no record GTT must disprove the midwife's claim (s 557C). |

**Key design points (U unless tagged):**
1. One payment screen, two documents: GTT Medical (consult, GST-free) and GTT Lounge (taxable). The checkout receipt says "not a Medicare account"; the Medicare account is emailed only after the consult, dated that day (HIA s 19(6), Regs s 49, L).
2. Path B never gets a Medicare account or a rebate figure, and no draw happens until the s 23DNL notice is signed (HIA s 23DNL, L).
3. A consult under 10 minutes is never billed as 82105; she is rebooked or refunded (MBS 82105 descriptor, L).
4. "Free" is banned in every form (Ahpra guideline 4.2, V), as are "Medicare fee" and rebate figures without "about".
5. New this round: the National Law s 133 penalty (up to A$120,000 for a company, V) now sits behind the wording rules as well as the ACL.
6. Open: Medicare billing requirements for the midwife beyond participating status and a provider number were not read this round. **Lawyer to check.**
