## 2. What s 23DNG actually is
s 23DNG is headed "Revocation of approval". It lets the Minister cancel a lab's approval for a specimen collection centre. s 23DNG(1) grounds include (V, paraphrased):
- (b) the centre does not follow the NPAAC collection centre guidelines;
- (ba) the centre is "not reasonably accessible to the public";
- (bb) the centre "does not operate under reasonable hours";
- (bc) the lab "does not operate the centre as a specimen collection centre";
- (c) breach of the ID number rule (s 23DNBB(3)), the notice rule (s 23DNK), an undertaking, or misuse of the number.

s 23DNG(1A), verbatim (V):
> "Without limiting paragraph (1)(bc), an approved pathology authority does not operate a centre as a specimen collection centre if, in any continuous period of 6 months beginning on the day the approval for the centre is granted under subsection 23DNBA(1), the total amount of benefit payable in respect of pathology services rendered, using pathology specimens collected at the centre, is less than the amount of benefit prescribed by the regulations for the purposes of this subsection."

Plain English: a collection centre that does not generate enough Medicare benefit in a 6-month period, counted from the day it is approved, can be treated as "not really operating" and its approval can be revoked. It is a minimum ACTIVITY test for the lab's centre. It is not a minimum benefit paid to anyone, and it does not set what the client gets back.

## 3. The dollar amount: NOT FOUND (no amount exists yet)
The Act leaves the amount to "the regulations". Checked 2026-10-26 (V):
- Health Insurance Regulations 2018 (F2018L01365), latest registered compilation No. 26 (F2026C00551, in force from 1 July 2026): the only "23DN" hit is the definition of "identification number" (s 23DNBB). No s 23DNG(1A) amount.
- The next change to those Regulations (1 November 2026) comes from the Health Insurance Legislation Amendment (2026 Measures No. 3) Regulations 2026 (F2026L01359). Its text has no "23DNG" and no collection centre amount.
- Round 9 (2026-10-19) found the subsection was inserted by the Regulatory Reform Omnibus Act 2025 and the Revised Explanatory Memorandum said the amount "will be set by regulation". Library file: HIA-23DNG-1A-MINIMUM-BENEFIT.md.
Result: the test exists in the Act but has no number yet, so it cannot bite until a regulation prescribes one. Watch for it.

## 4. Part IIA neighbours that matter (V)
| Section | What it says | GTT meaning |
|---|---|---|
| s 23DN | Minister approves premises as an accredited pathology laboratory, for up to 3 years | The LAB is accredited, not GTT |
| s 23DNBA | Minister may approve a collection centre for an approved pathology authority on premises it owns, leases, sub-leases or "is otherwise entitled to occupy"; tax must be paid first | The lab needs a lease, licence or right to occupy GTT's collection rooms |
| s 23DNBB | Centre gets an ID number; every collection document must show it | Lab paperwork, not GTT's |
| s 23DNK | The lab must keep a notice on prominent display that the centre is approved. Penalty 10 penalty units, strict liability | Leave wall space near the collection rooms |
| s 23DNL | Anyone running an UNAPPROVED collection centre must take reasonable steps to tell the client and the pathologist, before collection, that no Medicare benefit would be payable. Penalty 20 penalty units | This is the "no benefit" warning. If GTT's rooms are ever used before the lab's approval is granted, this warning is compulsory |
| s 23DO(2G) | The lab can ask the Minister to reconsider a revocation within 28 days | Lab's remedy, not GTT's |

## 5. What "minimum benefit" might mean if Anthony meant something else
- If he meant "the least Medicare pays the client": that is the MBS benefit for each item (85 percent of the fee), already locked at lab A$22.05 and consult A$32.95. Not in s 23DNG.
- If he meant "how much work a lab needs at GTT to keep its centre": that is s 23DNG(1A). The amount is not set.
