## 3. Where earlier-day consults go
Any block on any other midwife day, 1 to 28 days before the test, in the morning blocks left over or after 10:30. Test clients get priority on every midwife slot. The midwife roster is Mon to Sat (fortnightly Mon-Wed / Thu-Sat block), so no Sunday consults: Monday tests are covered by the Saturday before.

## 4. After 10:30: normal midwife bookings (Reed R22 Part 3, V)
Perth private midwives list: routine antenatal about 60 minutes, first booking visit up to 90 minutes, postnatal visits. Our 25 minute consult is shorter than all of them and is the Medicare short visit (item 82105, at least 10 minutes). Rules:
1. Test clients have the first call on every block (R). Standalone clients see only blocks nobody with a test booking still needs, released at 18:00 the day before (E).
2. A 60 or 90 minute visit may be sold only into time released that way, in whole blocks, never ahead of a test client.
3. Standalone is upside only and is never counted in any figure (R).
4. The midwife keeps start and end times in every note (Medicare may check, V Reed).

## 5. Hiring condition: participating status (Catherine R22B Part 3)
A participating midwife is an eligible midwife: registered, endorsed by the Nursing and Midwifery Board, with a Medicare provider number for the GTT address (online or form HW088), professional indemnity insurance, in private practice. Hire endorsed midwives only. Check before day one: Ahpra register print-out, insurance certificate, provider number confirmation. No provider number, no Path A consults. The meal break clause is in the contract (the midwife chooses it on the day).
