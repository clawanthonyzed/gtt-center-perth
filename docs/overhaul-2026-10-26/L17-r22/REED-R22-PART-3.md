## What "normal midwife bookings" are, in plain words
- The usual follow-up visit is 60 minutes. The first booking visit is up to 90 minutes. V (two Perth-area providers)
- A 25 minute consult is shorter than anything the private midwives list. It is a quick check, which fits the Medicare short item below. E
- So after 10:30 the midwife can offer: 60 minute visits (routine antenatal), 90 minute first visits, and postnatal visits. E
