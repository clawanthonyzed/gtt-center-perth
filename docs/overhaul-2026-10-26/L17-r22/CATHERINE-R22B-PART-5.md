## 5.1 The law (unchanged from R21, labels updated)

| Rule | Effect on Path B | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| HIA s 16A(5AA) | No benefit for a test on an outside practitioner's request unless the specimen is collected by the client, the requester or the requester's staff, or lab staff at an approved collection centre. GTT is none of these. | L (HIA-SECTION-16A.md) |
| HIA s 23DNL | Whoever operates a collection centre that is not approved must take reasonable steps so that, **before** the specimen is collected, the client AND the lab's approved pathology practitioner "are informed that, if the approved pathology practitioner were to use the specimen in rendering a pathology service, a medicare benefit would not be payable in respect of the pathology service". 20 penalty units. | V (R21) |

**Correction to my R21 wording:** a slip that travels with the sample reaches the lab AFTER the draw. The lab must be told **before**. Fix: a standing written notice to the partner lab, given once before the first Path B draw and kept in the lab agreement, PLUS the slip with each sample. **Lawyer to check** that a standing notice is a "reasonable step".

## 5.4 The lab fee inside "all in"

The A$300 / A$350 all-in price assumes the lab charges A$27.85. On a Path B request no Medicare benefit is payable, so the lab may set a different private fee. If it does, "all in" is a false price claim (ACL s 29(1)(i), V). Fix: get the lab's Path B private fee in writing before launch. Until then say: "A$272.15 to GTT today; the lab bills its own fee, currently A$27.85." NOT IN REPO: the lab's Path B fee.

## 5.6 Tax invoice (GST Act s 29-70, V, library copy)

A tax invoice must be issued by the supplier, in the approved form, and show: the supplier's identity and ABN; the buyer's identity or ABN if the total is A$1,000 or more; what is supplied with quantity and price; how far each supply is taxable; the date issued; the GST on each supply; and it must be clear the document is meant to be a tax invoice.

| Point | View | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| Who issues | The company that supplies. If GTT Medical's phlebotomists do the Path B draw, either Medical supplies it, or the Lounge buys it from Medical inside the group and sells it on. The invoice names the true seller. **Accountant to check.** | U |
| GST | Treat the whole Path B amount as taxable (the safe side). A draw with no Medicare benefit is GST-free only if it fits "medical service" para (b): a service "by or on behalf of a medical practitioner or approved pathology practitioner" generally accepted as necessary (GST Act s 195-1).
