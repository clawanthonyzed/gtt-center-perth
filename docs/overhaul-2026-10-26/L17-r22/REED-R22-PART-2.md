## The law in one paragraph
Australian Consumer Law does not ban cancellation fees. Fees must be told before booking and be fair for the real loss. A defined way to waive fees for genuine emergencies, applied the same way each time, is recommended. Source (article dated 14 Oct 2026, general information not legal advice): https://sprintlaw.com.au/articles/cancellation-fees-appointments-australia/ V
