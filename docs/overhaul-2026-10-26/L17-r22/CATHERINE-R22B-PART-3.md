## 3.1 What "participating midwife" means

| Rule | Words | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| MBS item 82105 | "Short antenatal professional attendance by a participating midwife, lasting at least 10 minutes" | V (R21) |
| HIA s 3 | "participating midwife means: (a) if the Minister has approved a common form of undertaking under section 21A, an eligible midwife in respect of whom there is in force an undertaking given by him or her and accepted by the Minister under section 21B; or (b) otherwise, an eligible midwife" | V |
| HIA s 21(1) | an eligible midwife "(a) is a midwife; and (b) meets the requirements specified in the regulations" | V |
| Regs 2018 s 66 | "the requirement for a person to be an eligible midwife is that the person is endorsed by the Nursing and Midwifery Board of Australia" | V |
| Undertaking | Whether a s 21A undertaking form is in force today: not checked. If one is, she signs it as part of the provider number application. | U |
| MN.13.16 | Benefits only for "clinically relevant services"; one patient per occasion; services subject to Medicare audit and Professional Review | V (R21) |
| MN.13.17 | The attendance may be given in settings that include "a midwifery group practice, a participating midwife practitioner's rooms or a medical practice" | V |

So in plain words: **participating midwife = a registered midwife with the Board's endorsement for scheduled medicines, billing in private practice with a Medicare provider number.**

## 3.2 How a midwife gets there

| Step | Requirement | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Registration | Registered as a midwife with the Nursing and Midwifery Board of Australia (Ahpra). Check the public register. | V (Services Australia billing rules) |
| 2. Endorsement | Endorsement for scheduled medicines for midwives. The Ahpra entry must say she is "qualified to prescribe schedule 2, 3, 4 and 8 medicines and to provide associated services required for midwifery practice". Board fact sheet: needs "three years' full-time clinical practice (5,000 hours) in the past six years"; contexts of practice include "antenatal". The rest of the standard (the approved prescribing course) I did not read: check the standard itself. | V (NMBA fact sheet, Services Australia), U (course) |
| 3. Insurance | "All midwives ... must have appropriate PII arrangements"; private-practice midwives need cover for antenatal care. Services Australia also lists professional indemnity insurance as a billing requirement. | V |
| 4. Private practice | She must "work in a private practice or in a public facility that has an exemption under subsection 19(2)". HW088: a midwife "cannot provide Medicare services as an employee of a public hospital or other government funded entity". A private company like GTT Medical is private practice (U, Services Australia to confirm). | V, U |
| 5. Provider number | "a separate provider number for each location you're practicing at". Apply online in HPOS (needs a PRODA account), or on **form HW088** ("Application for a Medicare provider number and/or prescriber number for a nurse practitioner or midwife"). **HW093 is the allied health form, not for midwives.** | V |
| 6. Collaborative arrangement | Not needed since 1 November 2024 (Department factsheet). | L (catherine-r13.md) |
| 7. Time to issue | Not stated on the pages I read. Plan for weeks, not days, and apply as soon as the GTT address is final. | U |

**If a candidate is not endorsed:** she cannot become participating quickly; endorsement needs 5,000 hours of recent practice plus the course. Compliant alternatives: (1) hire midwives who are already endorsed; (2) an endorsed midwife with a provider number elsewhere only adds the GTT location in HPOS (fastest); (3) contract an endorsed privately practising midwife for sessions; (4) a registered, non-endorsed midwife can support the endorsed midwife but cannot bill 82105 or request the test; (5) a participating nurse practitioner or a GP can also request the test (L, catherine-r13.md).

## 3.3 Can an employed midwife's consult be billed through GTT Medical?

| Point | Answer | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| Who charges | Yes, GTT Medical may charge the client and issue the account. The account must name the midwife and her practice address **or her provider number for the GTT location**, and state that she provided the service (Regs 2018 s 51); patient name, date rendered, amount charged, paid and owing (s 49); the item (s 50). | V (R21) |
| Who claims | The client claims her own benefit after paying (Structure B; HIA s 20(1), L R21). | L |
| Employment contract | Must say GTT Medical sets and keeps the consult fee, the midwife uses her GTT-location provider number only for GTT clients at GTT, and records belong to GTT Medical with her access kept. | U, lawyer to check |
| Medical entrepreneur | Employing a participating midwife makes GTT Medical a "medical entrepreneur" (s 3B(a)) and a "requester" for Part IIBA lab rules. Keep the lab relationship to permitted benefits only. | L (catherine-r13.md, catherine-r15.md) |
