## 1. The flow in one table
| Step | Who | What | Timing (E) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Midwife | Result reaches her. She decides it needs a call. | As soon as it arrives. Lab turnaround and how it reaches the midwife: NOT IN REPO |
| 2 | Midwife | Phones the client's mobile (the number checked at booking). | Same day, inside her shift |

Path B: the results go to her outside doctor or midwife, so GTT makes no results call at all (Catherine, V).

## 2. Ask at booking (Path A) so every message is allowed
Catherine's consent question (APP 6.1(a), V), placed on the Path A details screen:
> How may our midwife contact you about your results? [ ] Call my mobile [ ] You may leave a voicemail asking me to call back [ ] You may email me [ ] You may text me. Your mobile: [number] (please check it).
No tick means no voicemail, email or SMS. The midwife then keeps ringing and the clinical protocol decides the rest.

## 3. Call script (midwife)
1. "Hello, may I speak to [full name]? This is [first name], from [business name]."
2. "Is now a good time to talk in private?" If no: "When can I ring back today?"
3. "Before I go on, can you tell me your full name and date of birth?" No match, no detail.
4. "I am calling about your recent visit. One of your results needs a follow-up, and I would like to explain what happens next." Then the midwife explains in plain words and the next step with her own doctor or midwife (clinical protocol).
5. "Do you have any questions? You can call us on [number] any time we are open."
6. Record the call (section 6).

## 4. Voicemail wording (only if allowed)
> "This message is for [first name]. This is [midwife first name] from [business name]. Please call us back on [number]. Thank you."
Never say: results, glucose, pregnancy, midwife, abnormal, urgent about your test (Catherine, U).

## 5. Written follow-up (only if allowed)
Sender: the business name. Subject: "Please call us". No preview text with health words.
Email (default, Catherine's wording):
> Hello [first name], [midwife first name] from [business name] tried to call you today and will try again later today. Please call us on [number] when you can. Regards.
SMS: "[business name]: please call us on [number] when you can. Regards."
Option for the "results" version you asked for. Your locked text says the midwife tried to make contact about her results. Catherine says to leave "results" out, because the message could be read by someone else. Workaround: add a tick box at booking, "You may say in an email that our midwife tried to reach me about my results". Only if she ticks it, the email may read: "[midwife first name] tried to reach you about your results today and will try again later today. You can call GTT on [number] to speak to the midwife." The subject stays "Please call us" (see question 1).
Normal results (only if she allowed email or text). Subject: "A message from [business name]". Body: "Your midwife has reviewed your recent visit and no action is needed. Call [number] with any questions. Regards." No values, no diagnosis.

## 6. Who logs it
The midwife, in the clinical record, never the venue manager. One line per attempt: date, time, channel (call, voicemail, email, SMS), outcome (reached, no answer, left message), name of the midwife. The front desk logs only "client rang, midwife to call back" with name, number and time, with no reason. The venue manager (Lounge) must not see or hold any result (Privacy Act s 13B, V Catherine). Nothing about a result goes into staff notes, timesheets or the day list.
