## RULE 4. Waitlist and cancellations (LOCKED)
| Rule | Who | When |
|---|---|---|
| A slot that opens inside 48 hours is offered to the list AND made public at the same moment. First to book and pay gets it | Venue manager | The minute the slot opens |
| A slot that opens outside 48 hours goes to the list first, then the booking page after 48 hours | Venue manager | As it opens |
Venue manager steps: (1) Mark the slot free in the computer. (2) Check the time left: inside 48 hours means both lists at once. (3) Send the offer email below to everyone on the matching list. (4) Switch the slot to public on the booking page in the same minute. (5) When one client books and pays, the slot closes and the system emails everyone else "slot taken". (6) Log: date, time, slot, who got it. (7) The client joins the list by ticking a box herself; she can leave at any time.

Two clients at once: the first completed payment wins. The second sees "This time is being booked right now. Try again in a few minutes", is not charged, stays on the list, and gets the "slot taken" email. If a second payment is ever taken by mistake, the venue manager refunds it the same day (proposed, U). Nobody is promised a slot before she pays.
