## 1. Why the word "trust" came up (it is not the plan)

You are right: the plan is three companies. The trust topic came up for one reason only. My Route B memo (THEODORE-R22C-PART-2) **checked that no trust is involved**, because a new Bill would put a 30 percent minimum tax on discretionary trusts.

- The Bills: **Treasury Laws Amendment (Tax Reform No. 3) Bill 2026** and **Income Tax Rates Amendment (Minimum Tax on Discretionary Trusts) Bill 2026** (V, APH, read 2026-10-26). Introduced 14 October 2026; Senate committee reports 13 November 2026; not law yet. Would start for income years from 1 July 2028.
- The tax falls on the **trustee of a discretionary trust**. A company is not a trust (V, Bill ss 101AA, 101AB).
- Holdings is **owned directly by Anthony and his partner** (R, CURRENT-STATE section 000 item 10). Dividends go straight to them as people. No trustee receives the profit, so the trust tax has nothing to land on.
- The one trap: if Holdings shares were ever put into a family trust, the dividends become trust income. Keep the shares in your two names.

## 3. The real issues with three companies (short list)

1. **Payroll tax grouping.** The three are grouped by law: one A$1,000,000 WA threshold, not three. Each company is liable for the whole group's payroll tax. Group payroll tax A$1,344.04 a month (R, Center of Truth; E rate, R15, R16).
2. **GST group.** Plan: form a GST group in year 1, one BAS, no GST on charges between the companies (R, R20 s10.2). Without it, the room fee carries GST that the payer claims back: no cost, cash timing only (U, R16 A2).
3. **Tax consolidation.** Plan: elect in year 1, Holdings lodges one return at 25 percent (R, R20). Without it, Holdings could be taxed at 30 percent if its income is only passive, and a loss in one company cannot shelter profit in another (E, R15 s5.3, R16 A3). As head company of a consolidated group, Holdings enters PAYG tax instalments automatically (V, ato.gov.au, Starting PAYG instalments, 2026-10-27).
4. **Dividends between companies.** Ignored for tax if consolidated; if not, franked, no extra tax (U, R16 A2).
5. **Division 7A.** Owners lending money in is outside it. Watch any money going from a company to an owner (loan, private bill, private use): it becomes a deemed dividend unless handled correctly (V, R22C memo).
6. **Who employs whom.** Plan: Medical employs midwives and phlebotomists; Lounge employs service staff and the venue manager (R, R20). **Open:** DECISION-LOG 2026-10-20 notes that GTT employing the phlebotomists conflicts with Medicare s16A(5AB)(c) and is to be re-decided (R). **Round 24: Anthony locked Medical as the employer**; the s16A(5AB)(c) point stays with Catherine to confirm the compliant set-up.
