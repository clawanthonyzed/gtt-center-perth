## Job 3(a): abnormal results follow-up email and voicemail
Locked rule (R): the midwife phones. No answer: leave a message to call GTT, then send an email saying "the midwife has tried to make contact regarding results", with no result detail in the email or message.

**Verdict: lawful under APP 6 and APP 11, with three conditions. Confidence: high.** (L: Privacy Act 1988 and OAIC APP Guidelines ch 11, library r20 and r22; general knowledge for APP 6.)
| Rule | Why it is fine | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| APP 6 (use and disclosure) | Contacting the client about her own test is the primary purpose the information was collected for. Telling the client herself is not a disclosure to a third party | Use only the phone and email she gave GTT Medical for this purpose |
| APP 11 (reasonable security steps) | The email holds the least possible health information: no result, no word "abnormal", no test value | Ask at booking: "May we leave a voicemail and email you about your results?" with a tick box, and record her answer. If she says no to email, phone only |
| APP 11 | Someone else may read a shared inbox or hear a voicemail. The words "midwife" and "results" do show she had a test with us | Send from the GTT Medical mailbox only (not the Lounge), to her address only, no copies; keep a log of attempts in her record |

Safe email wording (approved meaning, minimal detail):
> Subject: Please call GTT Center Perth
> Hello [first name], the midwife has tried to make contact regarding results. Please call us on [GTT Medical phone number] between [hours]. Regards, GTT Center Perth

Safe voicemail wording:
> "Hello, this is [midwife first name] from GTT Center Perth, calling for [client first name]. Could you please call me back on [number] when you can. Thank you."

No result, no "urgent", no "abnormal", no lab name. The square-bracket items are fields the system fills in. Do not change the wording to "your results are abnormal" in any text, email or message. Normal results by generic text or email (locked) is fine on the same conditions.

## Job 3(b): meal break lock (two lines)
1. Lawful: Nurses Award MA000034 cl 14.1(a) gives a 30 to 60 minute unpaid meal break on shifts over 5 hours, which can be forfeited only on shifts of 6 hours or less, so "rostered for all staff, never worked through, staff choose to stay or leave" complies; cl 14.1(c) pays ordinary time when GTT requires her to stay available, so "stay on call = paid" is right (V, library r21/r22, consolidated 1 August 2026).
2. Fair Work Act s 45 forbids breaching an award term that applies; the lock prevents a breach, and payroll must pay overtime under cl 14.1(b) for any day work stops a break (V, r22/FWA-S45 file). Limit: cl 14 covers the midwives; the break clauses in the other staff's awards (service staff, phlebotomists, venue manager) were not checked this round (U).
