## 1. The change window (Anthony wrote both 1 week and 48 hours)

| | Option 1: free change up to 1 week before | Option 2: free change up to 48 hours before |
|---|---|---|
| Time to refill a freed slot | 5 days or more. Waitlist plus new bookers have time. | 2 days. Waitlist only, mostly. |
| Risk of an empty pair | Lower | Higher |
| Fairness to a pregnant client (illness, work, due date moves) | Harsh. She loses the free change early. | Kinder |
| Consumer law risk | A longer lock-in is easier to call unfair (Catherine R20, ACL s 23, V; her view on this length: not given) | Lower (U) |
| Fee inside the window (R20 recommended rule) | Keep half the lounge amount: A$81.07 (Package 1), A$106.07 (Package 2) | Same |
| Consult | A moved test keeps its consult if the new test is within 28 days after it; otherwise a new consult (A$130) | Same |

Recommendation: **Option 2, 48 hours**, with a reminder at 72 hours and an automatic waitlist (below) to refill. Anthony decides. (R20 had 24 hours. This replaces it if he agrees.)

## 2. Waitlist and cancellation list

| Rule | Draft |
|---|---|
| Who can join | Any client, free, no deposit, for a day or a time she chose. She may add a "test needed by" date (optional). |
| Offered first | 1. Clients who joined that day or time, in order of joining. 2. If none, clients on the list for nearby days. 3. Then the public booking page. |
| Tie-break | Same minute of joining: earlier "test needed by" date; then a documented random draw. |
| How long to accept | More than 24 hours to the test: 3 hours. 6 to 24 hours: 1 hour. Under 6 hours: 20 minutes (E). The slot is held for her only. No answer: the next client is offered it. |
| One bundle | A freed test pair and a midwife block move together. If a client cancels, her undelivered consult block is released at the same moment and offered with her test pair. A same-morning block is offered only with a pair that allows it. If the new client already has a valid consult (within 28 days before the new test), only the test pair is offered. |
| Midwife slot alone | A freed block goes first to a waitlisted test client, then to a standalone client (GRACE-R21-MIDWIFE-SLOTS.md). |
| Delivered consult | Stays earned (see section 5). |
| Charge | She pays only the GTT amount at acceptance. The lab bills her later. |

## 3. Illness rule (least proof)

| Step | Draft |
|---|---|
| Client says she is unwell, has a pregnancy concern, is in hospital or in labour | She ticks one box on the change page. She is NOT asked for a diagnosis or a certificate. |
| First time | Free move, no proof. |
| Second time within the same booking | Ask for a short note from her doctor or midwife that says she could not attend. No diagnosis. |
| Staff may never ask | What is wrong, test results, or her records. |
| Where it is kept | Only the tick and the date, in her booking (not in the staff notes). |

Why least proof: health information is sensitive under the Privacy Act, and APP 3 limits collecting it to what is necessary (Catherine R20, V). What a business may lawfully ask and what a consumer-law term can require: Catherine is checking limits (U). Until she answers, use this draft.
