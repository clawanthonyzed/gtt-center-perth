What price keeps profit equal? About A$298 all-in for Package 1 if the roster flexes, about A$330 if it is fixed (E, derived). Package 2 is A$50 more.

## 4. Consult listed as 30 minutes vs 25 minute blocks

| | 25 minute blocks | 30 minute listing, midwife really needs 30 |
|---|---|---|
| Time for 18 consults | 450 min = the 7.5 paid hours exactly | 540 min = 9 hours |
| First / last appointment | 08:15 / ends 15:45 | 08:15 / ends 17:15 (the roster stops at 16:00) |
| Consults that fit in 7.5 paid hours | 18 | 15 |
| Money | none | needs about 54 paid hours a week, not 45: A$2,550.14 less before tax, A$1,912.61 less after (net A$18,061.37) |

Confirmed result: if the midwife's real time stays at a 25 minute block, listing the appointment as 30 minutes costs GTT nothing. The extra 10 minutes of paperwork then has to happen before the consult, when the client arrives 10 minutes early (the late rule already requires that). If the midwife truly needs 30 minutes, GTT pays for it. No standalone income is counted either way. Recommendation: list it as 30 minutes for the client ("arrive early, 20 minutes with the midwife, then done"), schedule 25 minute blocks, and keep paperwork in the client's early arrival and the midwife's 5 spare minutes. You decide.
