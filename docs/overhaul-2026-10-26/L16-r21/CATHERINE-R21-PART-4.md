## Job 4. Secure computer record replacing the paper folder

**Verdict: lawful and safer than the weekly folder (R20 section 4). These are the expectations, in plain English.**

| Expectation | What GTT does | Law | Tag |
|---|---|---|---|
| Collect only what is needed | Referral copy, booking times, clinical notes. No extra fields "just in case". | APP 3.2, 3.3 | V |
| Protect it | "Reasonable steps" against misuse, loss and unauthorised access, and those steps "include technical and organisational measures". | APP 11.1, 11.3 | V |
| Who can see it | Role-based logins. GTT Medical staff (midwives, phlebotomists) see referrals and notes. Lounge staff see name, time and "consult booked: yes/no" only. Each person has their own login; no shared passwords. | APP 11.1; s 13B limit on sharing between companies (R20) | V, U |
| Audit log | The system records who opened, printed or changed a record, and when. Someone checks it monthly. | APP 11.3 measure | U |
| Strong sign-in | Two-step sign-in for every user; lock screens when away. | APP 11.3 measure | U |
| Backups | Automatic, encrypted, tested by a restore every quarter. | APP 11.1 (loss) | U |
| Retention | Clinical record: at least 7 years from the last entry (adult), common practice; insurers sometimes ask longer for pregnancy records. **Lawyer and the indemnity insurer to confirm.** Spare printouts: shred the day after the test. The lab keeps the original request for 2 years (HIA s 23DK(1), V in R20). | APP 11.2 | V, U |
| Destroy when done | Delete or de-identify when no longer needed and no law requires keeping it. | APP 11.2 | V |
| Australian hosting | No law I read forces a private provider to store in Australia. If data goes to an overseas recipient, GTT must take reasonable steps so the recipient does not breach the APPs (APP 8.1), and may be liable for its acts (s 16C). **Recommendation: choose a provider that keeps data in Australia and says so in the contract.** | APP 8.1 | V, U |
| Breach plan | If GTT suspects a breach: assess within 30 days (s 26WH(2)). If there are reasonable grounds to believe there was unauthorised access or disclosure likely to cause "serious harm" (s 26WE(2)), give the Privacy Commissioner a statement (s 26WK) and tell affected clients (s 26WL). Health data makes serious harm likely. | Part IIIC | V |
| Scanning requests | Follow Medicare's Electronic Transactions Act notice on scanning pathology requests. | L (npaac/NPAAC-RETENTION-LAB-RECORDS-9TH-2022.md) | L |
| WA law | No WA law found for private providers' health records. **Lawyer to check.** | | U |

**Draft privacy notice line (add to R20 section 4.4 wording):**
> We keep your pathology request and health notes in a secure electronic record held in Australia. Only our midwives and phlebotomists can open it, every access is logged, and copies are encrypted. Our lounge staff see only your name and booking times. If a data breach is likely to cause you serious harm, we will tell you and the Privacy Commissioner.

**Draft staff rule (operations manual):**
> Open a client record only for her care today or her booking. Never share logins. Printouts go back to the locked tray and are shredded the next day. Report any lost device, wrong email or unexpected access to the venue privacy contact at once.

## Job 5. Midwife meal breaks (Nurses Award 2020, MA000034)

Source: Fair Work Ombudsman award text, "consolidated modern award incorporates all amendments up to and including 1 August 2026 (PR812118)", read 2026-10-26 (V).

| Clause | Text (quoted) |
|---|---|
| 4.1(b) coverage | covers "employers who employ a nurse/midwife, principally engaged in nursing/midwifery duties"; Sch A: "nursing care also includes care provided by midwives" |
| 13.1(a) | Day worker ordinary hours "between 6.00 am and 6.00 pm, Monday to Friday" |
| 13.1(d) | Shift length "a maximum of 10 hours exclusive of meal breaks" |
| 14.1(a) | "An employee who works in excess of 5 hours will be entitled to an unpaid meal break of 30 to 60 minutes. Such meal break will be taken between the 4th and the 6th hour after beginning work, where reasonably practicable." Shifts of 6 hours or less may forfeit it by agreement |
| 14.1(b) | "Where an employee is required to be on duty during a meal break, the employee will be paid overtime for all time worked until the meal break is taken." |
| 14.1(c) | "Where an employee is required by the employer to remain available during a meal break, but is free from duty, the employee will be paid at ordinary rates for a 30 minute meal break." |
| 14.2 | "a paid 10 minute tea break in each 4 hours worked"; tea breaks "count as time worked" |
| 19.1(a)(i) | Overtime Monday to Saturday: 150% for the first 2 hours, 200% after |
| 21.1 | Saturday ordinary hours: 150% |

**How it applies to an 08:00 to 16:00 on-site shift**

| Point | Answer | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| Is a 30-minute unpaid break lawful? | **Yes.** She works over 5 hours, so she is entitled to a 30 to 60 minute unpaid break (cl 14.1(a)). | V |
| Paid hours | 8.0 on site less 0.5 unpaid = **7.5 paid hours**, matching Theodore R20. | V, R |
| When | Between the 4th and 6th hour: **12:00 to 14:00** for an 08:00 start, where reasonably practicable. Block it in the booking system. | V |
| If she must stay reachable | If GTT requires her to stay available (for example to respond to a client who feels faint), the break is paid at ordinary rates (cl 14.1(c)): 8.0 paid hours. | V |
| If she works through it | Overtime until the break is taken (cl 14.1(b)). | V |
| Tea breaks | Two paid 10-minute tea breaks are part of the 7.5 hours (cl 14.2), so they also need gaps in her bookings. | V |
| Making the break truly unpaid | Roster says she is free from duty and may leave the premises; phlebotomists and first aid cover handle the floor; no consults, no results calls. | U |
| Enforcement | Not paying an award entitlement breaches the Fair Work Act (s 45, not re-read this round). | U |

**Capacity flag (legal, not financial).** On-site time 08:15 to 16:00 is 465 minutes. Less the 30-minute meal break and two 10-minute tea breaks leaves **415 minutes** for consults and results calls. 18 consults at 20 minutes (360) fit. At 25-minute blocks (450) or 30-minute appointments (540) they **do not fit** without breaks being skipped, which turns them into paid or overtime time. See Question 5.

**Draft roster line:**
> Meal break: 30 minutes, unpaid, between 12:00 and 14:00. You are free from duty and may leave the building. Tea breaks: two paid 10-minute breaks, one before and one after the meal break.
