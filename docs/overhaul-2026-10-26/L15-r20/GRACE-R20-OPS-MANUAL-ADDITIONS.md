## 3. Booking order and consult timing

| Step | Rule |
|---|---|
| 1 | Client books the test first. |
| 2 | She then books the consult (or states she has a referral). |
| 3 | Consult is 1 to 28 days before the test, or on the morning of the test before her first blood sample. |
| 4 | Same-morning consults only for the pairs 08:40, 09:05, 09:30, 09:55 and 10:20, one client per pair, in the block shown in GRACE-R20-ROSTER-BOOKING.md section 3. |
| 5 | Clients booked 07:00 to 08:15 (8 a morning) have their consult on an earlier day. |
| 6 | A consult booked for once the blood has been drawn is never allowed. No request, no draw. |
| 7 | A same-morning client must stay fasted for the consult. Warning on the booking page and every reminder. |
| 8 | If the midwife decides no test is needed, the test is released and the booking terms apply. |
