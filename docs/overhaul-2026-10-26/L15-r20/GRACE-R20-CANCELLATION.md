Author: Grace (Operations Manager, GTT Center Perth). Date: 2026-10-26 (Round 20). **DRAFT for Anthony. I do not decide. Catherine to confirm the legal wording before any of this goes on a booking page.** Nothing was sent.

Inputs: Anthony's 50 percent idea (Round 20 decision 7) and Reed's benchmark in REED-R20.md (read in full on 2026-10-26, section 2). Money uses locked numbers only: lounge and service A$162.15 (Package 1) and A$212.15 (Package 2), consult A$130 (GST-free), lab fee A$27.85 (paid to the lab, never GTT money), GST is one eleventh of the lounge amount.

## 1. Anthony's idea, in plain words

If a client attends her consult but misses her test completely or arrives late, she gets 50 percent of the lounge amount back. When she rebooks she must pay the full lounge amount again. The consult is kept by GTT because the midwife did it.

Anthony's question: is 50 percent too generous, and what do other businesses do?

Reed found no published rule for a missed glucose test at PathWest or WDP (labs are paid per test done). A law firm article he read says a fee must be clear at booking and tied to the real loss. I also saw an unverified summary saying 24 hours notice with 50 percent for late cancel and 100 percent for no-show is a common spa pattern. Neither is a legal ruling.

Answer for Anthony: **50 percent is not too generous. It is at the middle of the market.** Reed agrees. What is missing in Anthony's idea is a notice window, a medical exception and a rule for lateness. Recommended rule below adds them.

Working notes:
- Recommended P1: refund 162.15 / 2 = 81.075, rounded to A$81.08 back (GTT keeps A$81.07). Cash collected: 162.15 - 81.08 + 162.15 = 243.22. Extra to the client: 243.22 - 162.15 = 81.07. P2: 212.15 / 2 = 106.075, back A$106.08, keep A$106.07, cash 212.15 - 106.08 + 212.15 = 318.22.
- Alternative B P1: 162.15 - 50.00 = 112.15 credit; she pays 162.15 on rebooking, uses 112.15 credit, pays 50.00. Cash collected 162.15 + 50.00 = 212.15. P2 credit 162.15, collected 212.15 + 50.00 = 262.15. The A$50 is Reed's example from a clinic, NOT a cost figure for GTT; the real cost of an empty slot is NOT IN REPO beyond the ex-GST value above.
- The 50 percent outcome is the same as a 50 percent no-show fee with the other half carried to the rebooking. The only difference is cash flow and fees.
- Card fee on a repeat charge (locked model 1.7 percent plus A$0.30): charging the full A$162.15 again costs about A$3.06 (162.15 x 0.017 + 0.30). A credit-and-top-up (client tops up A$81.07) costs about A$1.68 (81.07 x 0.017 + 0.30). Whether the card provider returns fees on a refund is NOT VERIFIED. Credit-and-top-up is cheaper if it is allowed; Anthony asked for a refund, so the table shows a refund.
- GST on a retained fee: whether GST applies to the amount kept is an accountant question (NOT VERIFIED). Figures above show the cash kept, with the ex-GST view where marked.
- If she does not rebook: under the recommended rule GTT keeps A$130 + A$81.07 = A$211.07 (P1) and A$130 + A$106.07 = A$236.07 (P2). Under Alternative A for a no-show GTT keeps A$292.15 (P1) or A$342.15 (P2).
- If her rebooked test falls more than 28 days after the consult, a new consult is needed (A$130). Inside 28 days the existing referral is used.
- The client's Medicare consult rebate (A$32.95) is not affected because the consult took place.
- Medicare rebate for the lab: if she does not have the test, no lab bill and no rebate; she owes the lab nothing.

## 4. The rules table for the booking terms (recommended version)

| Situation | What happens | Money |
|---|---|---|
| Client changes her test with 24 hours notice or more | Free move to another test slot. The lounge amount moves with her. If the new test is more than 28 days after the consult, she books a new consult. | No fee |
| Client changes her test with less than 24 hours notice | Treated as a late change | 50 percent of the lounge amount kept; she pays the full lounge amount again to rebook (section 3) |
| Client attends the consult, then misses the test completely | As above | 50 percent back, full lounge amount again to rebook |
| Client attends the consult, then arrives late for the test | She is late if she arrives after the time in her confirmation (for the 07:00 pair, 06:50). The locked rule is no allowance for late arrivals. | 50 percent back, full lounge amount again to rebook (grace time is question 1) |
| Client cancels the consult with 24 hours notice or more | Free move or free cancel. The test slot stays held until 18:00 the day before the test, then is released. | No fee |
| Client cancels the consult with less than 24 hours notice, or misses it | Consult block is lost. | NOT DECIDED. Suggestion: 50 percent of the consult (A$65.00), the rest refunded or moved (question 2). |
| Midwife decides no request is needed | Test slot released | No lounge fee. Consult kept because it was delivered. Refund of the lounge payment in full. |
| Client is unwell or has a pregnancy problem or an emergency | One free rebook, with a short message from her doctor or midwife if asked. Work, travel and forgetting do not count. | No fee for the first time |
| Client arrives without a referral (see GRACE-R20-PAPER-SAFETY-NET.md section 6) | Same-morning consult if a block is free; otherwise she rebooks | Rebooking fee as late change |
| Second miss | She pays the lounge amount up front to rebook | Full lounge amount before the new slot is held |
| GTT or the partner lab cancels or cannot do the test | Free rebook or full refund of GTT's part | No fee, client chooses |
| Lab fee (A$27.85) | Paid to the lab only. GTT never refunds or holds it. | Not GTT money |

## 5. Recommendation and two alternatives

| | Rule | Why |
