## 1. Executive summary

### 1.1 The business in one sentence

GTT Center Perth is a private, pregnancy-only centre in Perth where an expecting mother does her glucose tolerance test (the sugar-drink blood test for diabetes in pregnancy) in a luxury lounge, with a midwife consult and two spa services, instead of sitting in a waiting room for two hours.

### 1.2 Why it can work

Almost every pregnant woman in Australia is asked to do this test. It means fasting overnight, drinking a very sweet glucose drink, and giving three blood samples over about two hours, mostly sitting still. The test itself does not change. GTT Center Perth changes the two hours around it. Think of an airport lounge: the flight is the same, but the wait is completely different.

### 1.3 How it makes money

The client pays one private, all-inclusive price. She then claims her own Medicare rebate. The business never handles the lab's money.

### 1.4 The honest headline

On the core business alone, net profit is A$19,973.98 a month. That is A$26.02 short of the founders' A$20,000 target. About A$34.70 more profit before tax a month would close the gap.

Three things not counted in that headline would each clear the target:

| Upside (not in the headline) | Extra net profit a month | Label |
|---|---|---|
| Standalone private pregnancy consults, low case (5 a week) | A$2,022.96 | Estimate, take-up assumed |
| Card provider at 1.6 percent instead of the cautious 1.7 percent plus A$0.30 | about A$123 | Estimate, provider not chosen |
| Interest on client prepayments (first 12 months, after tax, at the verified 4.60 percent cash rate) | A$3,204.92 in the first 12 months | Estimate, top of range |

The plan uses cautious numbers on purpose. The model charges the higher card fee, plans for every client to buy the cheaper package, and leaves standalone consults and interest out of the headline.

### 1.5 What happens next

Nothing has been signed, registered or paid. The first steps cost nothing: written questions to the government bodies that set the Medicare and pathology rules, then approaches to all four candidate pathology labs at once. No company is registered, no lease is signed and nobody is hired until those answers are in and the founders approve (Gate 1, section 13).

## 3. The problem and the opportunity

### 3.1 Who we serve

Pregnant women in Perth who need the pregnancy glucose tolerance test (Medicare item 66548). The whole centre is for expecting mothers only. Any pregnant person may book.

### 3.2 What the test involves

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1 | The client fasts overnight. Water only, for the hours the partner lab sets |
| 2 | First blood sample, fasting |
| 3 | She drinks a 75 g glucose drink |
| 4 | Second blood sample at 60 minutes |
| 5 | Third blood sample at 120 minutes |
| 6 | Only then can she eat |

### 3.3 The problem

At a normal collection centre the client is asked to stay in the centre and remain seated until the test is finished. That is about two hours, fasting, often tired, and sometimes feeling sick after the glucose drink. Nothing is wrong with the test. The wait is the problem.

How clients feel about this wait has not been measured. There is no survey yet. The pain point is the founders' view, built on the conditions above.

### 3.4 The opportunity

| What the client wants | What GTT Center Perth offers |
|---|---|
| Not to be bored, hungry and uncomfortable | A luxury lounge and two spa services fitted between the blood draws |
| A clinician who explains the test | A 20-minute midwife consult before the test |
| No surprise bills | The full price, including the lab's fee, shown at checkout |
| Clear results | The midwife reviews the results and contacts her, and refers her on if needed |
| A simple booking | Test and consult booked together online |

A second, smaller opportunity sits inside the same staff: the midwives can also sell standalone private pregnancy consults to other expecting mothers in any spare consult time.

## 4. The service and the client journey

### 4.1 The two packages

| | Package 1 | Package 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Spa services | 2 services of 30 minutes | 2 services of up to 45 minutes |
| Midwife consult, 20 minutes | Included | Included |
| Lounge access and approved drinks | Included | Included |
| Blood test and collection | Billed by the lab | Billed by the lab |
| Full price shown at checkout | A$320.00 | A$370.00 |

Morning services are massage, beauty treatments, nails and hair. Services fit between draws and never interrupt a draw. The plan assumes every client buys Package 1, the cautious case.

Food and drink rule: water only until the last blood sample, then herbal tea and breakfast are waiting.

### 4.2 The lounge

The setting is a luxury day spa and maternal sanctuary, not a clinic and not a hospital. The space will hold two collection rooms with one chair each, one consult room, an open lounge, partitioned treatment bays and a refreshment counter. A registered architect will design the space. The floor area is not yet confirmed.

### 4.3 The client journey

| Stage | When | What happens | Money |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Book the test | Online, any time | She picks Package 1 or 2, then her test day and time | None yet |
| 2. Choose the referral path | Same booking | "I need a referral" (package plus consult, shown first and recommended) or "I have a referral" (see note below) | None yet |
| 3. Book the consult | Same booking | She picks a consult 1 to 28 days before the test, or on the morning of the test before her first sample where a slot exists | None yet |
| 4. Checkout | Same booking | She sees the full price including the lab fee, pays only the GTT amount, and sees a note on her expected Medicare rebates | A$292.15 (Package 1) to GTT |
| 5. Reminders | Before each visit | Fasting rule, arrival time, what to bring | None |
| 6. Midwife consult | Earlier day or test morning | 20-minute assessment. If the midwife decides the test is right for her, she writes and signs the pathology request (referral) | Already paid |
| 7. Test morning | Arrive 10 minutes before the first appointment (06:50 for the 07:00 pair) | Three blood samples over two hours, two spa services between them, then breakfast | Already paid |
| 8. Lab bill | After the test | The partner lab bills her directly | A$27.85, client to lab |
| 9. Results | After the lab reports | The midwife reviews the results and contacts her. A positive result is referred to her doctor, obstetrician or hospital service | None in the package |
| 10. Medicare claim | After she has paid each account | She claims her own rebates | About A$55.00 back to her |

No request on file means no blood draw. The request must exist before the first sample is taken.

Same-morning consults. A client may book her consult on the morning of the test, before the test. She must stay fasted for the consult, and the booking page and every reminder say so. The midwife starts at 08:00, so a same-morning consult is only possible for the later test times (section 7.3).

Note on "I have a referral". The founders want a choice for clients who already hold a referral from their own doctor or midwife, with a strong recommendation to use the GTT midwife. Legal review has flagged that, under the Medicare collection rules, a blood sample collected at GTT Center Perth on an outside referral may not attract a Medicare benefit (section 9.2). This option is held as an open decision until a health lawyer confirms the position (section 15, item 4).

### 7.4 Standalone consults as upside

Spare consult time depends on how long each consult block really takes.

| Planning basis | Spare consult slots a week |
|---|---|
| 20-minute slots with no turnover time | about 24 (4 a day) |
| 25-minute blocks with every test client using the GTT midwife | 0 on a full week |

Spare slots also appear whenever a morning is under 18 clients, which will be the case during the early months. Test clients always come first. Unsold consult blocks are released for standalone bookings the evening before. Standalone income is upside only and is not in the headline profit.

### 7.5 Booking rules

1. The client books the test first, then the consult.
2. A consult is 1 to 28 days before the test, or on the test morning in an eligible slot.
3. The same-morning option is never shown for the 07:00 to 08:15 pairs.
4. A consult after the first blood sample is never offered.
5. The midwife marks "request issued" at the end of the consult. The phlebotomist checks it before the first draw.
6. If the midwife decides the test is not right for the client, the test slot is released and the lounge payment is refunded in full.
7. A test moved more than 28 days after the consult needs a new consult.

### 7.6 Referral safety net

The founders have chosen a paper back-up: a weekly folder with one pocket for each test day, where the midwife files a copy of each referral for the day of the test, in case a client forgets hers. Legal review rates this as the weakest privacy point in the plan and recommends a scanned copy in the secure clinical record, printed on the test morning, as the safer equivalent (section 9.4). The choice is an open decision (section 15, item 7).

### 8.2 Why the clinical staff sit in the Medical company

Medicare pays for a test collected by an employee of the same business that employs the requesting midwife. So the phlebotomists must be employees of the Medical company, never Lounge staff and never contractors.

### 8.3 Rostering to bookings

Staff are rostered to match the day's bookings, so nobody is paid to wait.

| Clients booked that morning | Phlebotomists | Service staff |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | 2 |
| 2 to 9 | 1 | 4 |
| 10 | 2 | 6 |
| 11 to 18 | 2 | 8 |

A named second collector is on call for mornings of 9 or fewer.

### 8.4 Midwife requirements

Each midwife must hold current registration with the national health practitioner regulator, professional indemnity insurance, and a Medicare provider number for the venue (which can take up to 28 days to issue). Pay is flat. No pay, bonus or target is ever linked to the number of tests requested.

## 9. Legal, regulatory and privacy

This section is a plain summary of the legal review completed so far. It is not legal advice. Every point below must be checked by a health lawyer before launch, and tax points by an accountant.

### 9.1 Medicare and pathology rules

| Rule | Plain meaning |
|---|---|
| A participating midwife may request the pregnancy glucose test | The GTT midwife can write the referral |
| The written request must exist before the sample is collected | Consult first, then the test. There is no minimum waiting time, so a same-morning consult is lawful if it ends before the first sample |
| The law sets no expiry on a pathology request | The 28-day booking rule sits safely inside the law. The lab is asked to confirm in writing that it accepts requests of that age |
| Collectors must be employees of the requesting midwife's employer | Phlebotomists are Medical company employees |
| No kickbacks between a lab and the business that requests tests | Nothing of value passes from the lab to any GTT company. The lab billing the client itself is the lowest-risk option. A lab payment terminal in the collection room should be avoided |
| The request must not tie the client to a named lab without a clinical reason | The partner lab is described as the lab GTT Center Perth uses |
| The rebate belongs to the client | She claims it herself after paying each account in full |
| Real clinical care and records | A genuine assessment and notes for every client |

### 9.2 Flags raised by the legal review

| Flag | Issue | Recommended workaround |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A sample collected at GTT Center Perth on a referral from an outside doctor may not attract a Medicare benefit, because the collectors are not employed by that doctor and the room is not an approved collection centre | Every client tested at the centre has a GTT midwife consult and a GTT request. A client with an outside referral brings it to the consult as background. Only one test is done |
| 2 | The lab fee and the lab rebate may rest on different collection items | Use the word "about" on every rebate figure, get the lab's fee in writing before launch, and update the booking page once the Medicare item helpline answers |
| 3 | Midwives start at 08:00 but draws start at 07:00 | The booking engine offers a same-morning consult only when it ends before that client's first sample |

### 9.3 Consumer law: pricing and cancellations

| Point | Plain meaning |
|---|---|
| Single price rule | When part of a price is shown, the full price must be shown at least as prominently. The full A$320 (or A$370) is the headline at checkout |
| No misleading prices or rebate claims | Rebates are shown as estimates and never as a GTT discount. Never "A$55 back on your consult" |
| New drip-pricing rules from 1 July 2027 | Any extra charge must sit clearly next to the base price. The planned checkout already does this |
| Cancellation fees are tested for fairness | A missed-test fee must be clear, shown at booking, matched by the business's own duties, and linked to a real loss |
| Lateness must be defined by the clock | For example, "not checked in by the arrival time on your confirmation", never "at our discretion" |
| The business's duty must mirror the client's | If GTT Center Perth or the lab cannot run the test, she chooses a full refund of the lounge amount or a free rebooking |
| Exceptions | Illness, a pregnancy complication, hospital admission or labour, or a clinical decision not to test, all lead to a free rebooking or a full lounge refund |
| Consult fee | May be kept when the consult took place. A second consult is charged only when a new one is genuinely needed |

### 9.4 Privacy

| Point | Plain meaning |
|---|---|
| The Privacy Act 1988 applies to every health service provider, whatever its size | Privacy policy, collection notice, security and data breach reporting are required |
| A referral is health information, which is sensitive | It needs consent or a health-service exception to collect, and may be used only for the purpose collected |
| Related companies may not freely share sensitive information | Only Medical company staff (midwives and phlebotomists) see referrals and health details. Lounge staff see names and times only |
| The lab keeps the original request | The lab must keep the written request for 2 years |
| Paper register | Lawful only with tight controls (locked cabinet, Medical staff only, copies destroyed after the test). A lost weekly folder could expose up to 108 clients. A scanned copy in the clinical record is the recommended replacement |
| Booking details passed to the lab | The request and sample are covered by a clear notice. Extra details for the lab's billing should have the client's express consent |
| How long to keep clinical records | No set period was found for a private midwife's records. To be confirmed by a lawyer and the indemnity insurer |

### 9.5 Other obligations

| Area | What applies | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Workers compensation | Compulsory in Western Australia for each employing company | Established |
| Work health and safety | Safe workplace, first aid and a needlestick procedure | Established |
| Fair Work awards | Award classes and rates for each role, including the 06:30 start and the midwife meal break | Not yet confirmed |
| WA health facility licence | A consult room and collection rooms are probably not a licensable private hospital (no overnight stay, no anaesthetic) | Written view to be sought from WA Health |
| Accessibility | Disability discrimination law and premises standards apply to the fit-out | Building surveyor to check |
| Advertising health services | No misleading claims and no testimonials about the midwife | Established |

## 10. Structure and tax

### 10.1 Three companies

| Company | Job | Why separate |
|---|---|---|
| Holdings company | Owns the other two companies and receives their profits. Owned directly by the two founders, who are its directors | Keeps retained profit away from claims against the operating companies |
| Medical company | Employs the midwives and phlebotomists and bills the consult | Meets the Medicare collection rule and keeps clinical risk in one place |
| Lounge company | Holds the lease, sells the lounge and spa services, employs the service staff and the venue manager | Ring-fences the lease, the largest liability |

The Lounge company grants the Medical company a written licence to use the consult and collection rooms for a flat market fee. The fee is never linked to Medicare benefits or to the number of tests. Inside the group this fee has no effect on total profit.

### 10.2 Tax

## 16. Glossary

| Term | Plain meaning |
|---|---|

| Partner lab | The pathology company that tests the samples and bills the client |
| Kickback rules | Laws stopping labs giving anything of value to businesses that send them tests |
| GST | A 10 percent tax on most sales, collected for the government |
| GST-free | A sale with no GST on it, such as a health consult |
| GST group | Related companies treated as one for GST |
| Excluding GST | A price with the GST taken out |
| Company tax | Tax a company pays on its profit, 25 percent for a base rate entity |
| Tax consolidation | A group of companies lodging one income tax return |
| Payroll tax | A Western Australian tax on total wages above a threshold |
| Franked dividend | Profit paid to shareholders, carrying a credit for company tax already paid |
| Shareholder loan | Money the owners lend the company, repaid before dividends |
| On-cost | Costs on top of wages, such as super, workers compensation and leave |
| Break-even | The point where income exactly covers costs |
| Sensitivity | How much profit moves when one input changes |
