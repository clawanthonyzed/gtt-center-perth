## 1b. Standard Clarifying Question, All 3 Partners: Self-Collection / Lab-Processing-Only Model (added 2026-08-28)

**Genuine gap found and closed 2026-08-28, not just a PathWest fix.** GTT Center Perth's actual proposed model is: GTT employs its own phlebotomists and runs its own collection room(s); a pathology partner's role is limited to lab testing and reporting results back to the client's own referring doctor, the partner does not supply or operate collection staff at all. This is a materially different, and more standard, ask than "the partner supplies/operates the collection itself," and it had not been stated plainly to any of the 3 partners before PathWest's 2026-08-28 reply exposed the gap: her answer read as though she assumed the larger ask (PathWest supplying/operating collection) and declined that, without confirming whether the actual, narrower ask (self-collection, lab-processing-only) was ever understood or addressed.

**This question must now be asked of all 3 partners, not treated as PathWest-specific:** (1) does "collection staff"/"collection service" in any reply mean phlebotomists specifically, sample-transport/courier staff, or both; (2) is there a model where GTT collects with its own staff and simply delivers samples for testing and reporting, with no partner staff on-site at all; (3) if a partner mentions needing a doctor-staffed clinic or GP involvement, what would that actually involve, and is it specifically required to unlock the self-collection/lab-only model, or a separate, different arrangement. See `docs/architecture/WDP-PARTNERSHIP-ACTIVATION-PLAN.md` §2 for the corresponding new Model D added to the phlebotomist-employment-model comparison, alongside the existing Model A/B/C (who employs the phlebotomist), since this is a different axis (whether the partner needs any role in the collection process/accreditation at all, versus purely receiving and testing already-collected samples), not a restatement of the existing employment-model question.
