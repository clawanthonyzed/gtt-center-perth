## Draft

**Subject:** New venture — discretionary trust, mixed-supply GST, seeking initial engagement

> Hi [Accountant/Firm name],
>
> I'm setting up a new Perth wellness venue, trading through an existing discretionary trust with a corporate trustee. I'm looking for an accountant with experience in trust taxation and mixed-supply GST for an initial engagement, and wanted to check fit before going further.
>
> A brief outline of what I'd need help with:
>
> - Confirming whether the existing trust structure should trade this business directly, or whether an operating company under the trust makes more sense given the position we're in
> - Advice on how staff at this venue should be remunerated (salary vs. distribution) — this affects payroll setup, so I'd like to resolve it early
> - GST treatment for a mixed-supply business — part of the revenue is GST-free (a licensed pathology service) and part is standard-rated (wellness services, room rental) within the same transaction, so I need advice on the apportionment method
> - GST registration and BAS lodgement frequency recommendations for a new business with a slow initial ramp
> - Default superannuation fund selection for future employees
> - Confirming WA payroll tax threshold implications for a business of this expected size
>
> The venture is pre-launch — no venue secured yet, no staff hired yet — so this would be an early-stage engagement to get the structure right before any of that happens.
>
> Could you let me know your availability for an initial call or meeting, and your fee for an initial structure review?
>
> Regards,
> Anthony Zed
