## Source Tier System Used Throughout This Directory

- **Tier 1** — government/regulator/official (ACSQHC, WA Health, ABS, AHPRA, Federal Register of Legislation, training.gov.au)
- **Tier 2** — professional/industry association, trade press, established industry publications
- **Tier 3** — real operator accounts (news coverage of a named real business, direct correspondence)
- **Tier 4** — forums, aggregator/SEO content, unverified — used only where explicitly labelled, never as the sole basis for a finding
