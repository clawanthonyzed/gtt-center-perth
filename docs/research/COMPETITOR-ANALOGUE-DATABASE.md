## 2. OPERATIONAL ANALOGUES (Perth-specific: pregnancy-focused day spas, same target customer, different occasion/model)

These are not GTT-wait competitors, but they compete for the same customer's pregnancy wellness spending, and are the closest real evidence of demand, positioning, and durability for a pregnancy-focused premium wellness concept in this exact city.

| SOOTHE Massage & DaySpa | Nedlands | General day spa: remedial/relaxation massage, couples massage, facials, head spa, infrared sauna, foot care | Appears operating (search-indexed current) | Tier 4 (own website, soothespaperth.com) | 2026-09-25 |

**What this means for GTT Center Perth (evidence-based observations, not a decision):**

1. **Yummy Mummy Perth's closure is a genuine, real data point that a pregnancy-EXCLUSIVE day spa concept has not durably survived in this exact city before** — this is not proof GTT Center Perth would fail (the model is materially different: mandatory clinical event as the anchor, not discretionary pampering spend; multi-service concurrent model, not single-client), but it is real evidence that pregnancy-only positioning alone is not automatically durable in the Perth market, and should be weighed against the assumption (implicit throughout this venture's marketing documents) that "no WA competitor" equals low market risk. **No source found explains why Yummy Mummy Perth actually closed** — do not infer a specific cause; it is flagged as an open, unresolved question, not treated as validated failure intelligence (see `OWNER-OPERATOR-FAILURE-INTELLIGENCE.md` for the distinction between confirmed-cause and unconfirmed-cause closures).
2. **The Subiaco/Nedlands catchment (near King Edward Memorial Hospital) already has established general-purpose day spas (Greenhaus, SOOTHE) with an existing local client base for massage/facial/beauty services** — these are not GTT-wait competitors, but they are evidence the immediate catchment already sustains multiple day-spa businesses, a positive read for local demand density, not a negative one.
3. **No competitor anywhere in the Perth market currently combines a clinical anchor appointment with a multi-service concurrent wellness experience** — this is the genuinely durable differentiator, more durable than "pregnancy-only" positioning by itself (which Yummy Mummy Perth's closure suggests is not, on its own, sufficient).
