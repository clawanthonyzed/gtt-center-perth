## 2. OPERATIONAL ANALOGUES (Perth-specific: pregnancy-focused day spas, same target customer, different occasion/model)

These are not GTT-wait competitors, but they compete for the same customer's pregnancy wellness spending, and are the closest real evidence of demand, positioning, and durability for a pregnancy-focused premium wellness concept in this exact city.

| SOOTHE Massage & DaySpa | Nedlands | General day spa: remedial/relaxation massage, couples massage, facials, head spa, infrared sauna, foot care | Appears operating (search-indexed current) | Tier 4 (own website, soothespaperth.com) | 2026-09-25 |

**What this means for GTT Center Perth (evidence-based observations, not a decision):**

1. **Yummy Mummy Perth's closure is a genuine, real data point that a pregnancy-EXCLUSIVE day spa concept has not durably survived in this exact city before** — this is not proof GTT Center Perth would fail (the model is materially different: mandatory clinical event as the anchor, not discretionary pampering spend; multi-service concurrent model, not single-client), but it is real evidence that pregnancy-only positioning alone is not automatically durable in the Perth market, and should be weighed against the assumption (implicit throughout this venture's marketing documents) that "no WA competitor" equals low market risk. **No source found explains why Yummy Mummy Perth actually closed** — do not infer a specific cause; it is flagged as an open, unresolved question, not treated as validated failure intelligence (see `OWNER-OPERATOR-FAILURE-INTELLIGENCE.md` for the distinction between confirmed-cause and unconfirmed-cause closures).
2. **The Subiaco/Nedlands catchment (near King Edward Memorial Hospital) already has established general-purpose day spas (Greenhaus, SOOTHE) with an existing local client base for massage/facial/beauty services** — these are not GTT-wait competitors, but they are evidence the immediate catchment already sustains multiple day-spa businesses, a positive read for local demand density, not a negative one.
3. **No competitor anywhere in the Perth market currently combines a clinical anchor appointment with a multi-service concurrent wellness experience** — this is the genuinely durable differentiator, more durable than "pregnancy-only" positioning by itself (which Yummy Mummy Perth's closure suggests is not, on its own, sufficient).

## 2b. REAL AUSTRALIAN BUSINESS-FOR-SALE ECONOMICS (Added Round 2, 2026-09-25 — Not a Competitor List, a Financial-Forensics Source)

Mined directly from real, current Australian business-for-sale listings — this is not competitor intelligence in the usual sense, it is real financial data on comparable single-site service businesses that generic industry articles never disclose. Full detail and analysis in `docs/research/CASE-STUDY-DATABASE.md` §5-6 and `docs/research/FINANCIAL-FORENSICS-RECONSTRUCTION.md`. Headline finding: a real, current (2026), established (20+ year) South Perth day spa/beauty/skin business lists turnover ~A$5,500/week (~A$286,000/year), rent ~A$1,334/week (~24% of turnover), asking price A$85,000 plus stock — and 8 further real national hair/beauty salon listings sourced in the same session show single-service salons consistently trading at $55,000-$200,000 regardless of years established, a materially lower resale multiple than the broader "day spa" sector's own cited 1.4-3.5x SDE range. Source: Benchmark Business Sales & Valuations, businessforsale.com.au, direct fetch, Tier 3, 2026-09-25.

| Any Lab Test Now | USA | Direct-to-consumer retail lab-draw franchise, walk-in/appointment, largely doctor-order-free | Real proof point that a standalone blood-collection storefront can be commercially viable as its own unit — different payer system, not a transferable number | `docs/research/PATHOLOGY-OPERATIONS-DEEP-DIVE.md` §3, Tier 2/3 | 2026-09-25 |

| PamperSuite (Minnesota Women's Care) | USA | Spa co-located inside/adjacent to a real OBGYN practice, pregnancy-safe treatments | Real precedent for combining obstetric medical care with wellness services, though structurally different (co-located ongoing practice, not a single test event) | `docs/research/CASE-STUDY-DATABASE.md` Case 10, Tier 4 (own site only) | 2026-09-25 |
| Private phlebotomy/blood-test clinics (Harley Street/London) | UK | Direct-pay blood tests, £29-420 per panel plus a flat £50 phlebotomy fee per visit regardless of number of tests ordered in that visit | Different payer system (no NHS-equivalent bulk-billing pathway used), but shows a real market precedent for pricing the collection/phlebotomy act itself as a distinct, flat fee separate from the test panel price — a pricing-structure idea, not a number to import | Search results, multiple UK clinic pricing pages, Tier 3/4 | 2026-09-25 |

**Honest limitation stated plainly:** no international example was found combining a *mandatory* pregnancy screening test with a premium wellness experience the way MIWM and GTT Center Perth do — the IV-lounge and PamperSuite examples are the closest adjacent patterns (clinical procedure + premium environment; obstetric care + wellness), not exact analogues. This supports, rather than undermines, the claim that GTT Center Perth's specific combination is genuinely novel internationally, not just nationally.
