## 1. Do the steps have to be in order? (the rule in plain words)

**No. Only the tag on each step matters, not its number.**

1. A step tagged **CAN START NOW** has nothing in front of it. Start it any day, in any order, side by side with others.
2. A step tagged **NEEDS STEP N FIRST** must wait until step N is ticked. Step N is ticked when its "Done when" line is true.
3. You do not have to finish a phase before starting the next phase. Phases only group the steps.
4. Two hard gates sit across the plan. **Gate G1 (step 17):** no company registration, lease or hiring until Anthony says go. **Government before labs:** the four lab emails (step 22) wait for the government replies.
5. Steps marked **Anthony, personally** are the ones only you can do: identity, signatures, money, accounts, or a fact only you hold. Everything else is drafted or run by an agent or an outside professional.
6. Each step's status below starts as "[ ]". Tick it by changing it to "[x]" when its "Done when" is true. Steps already done are pre-ticked.
