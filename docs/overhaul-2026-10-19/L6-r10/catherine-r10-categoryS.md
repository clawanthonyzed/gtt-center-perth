# Catherine R10: Can GTT run its own lab? (plain-English summary)

Date: 2026-10-20 (AWST). Full analysis: CATEGORY-S-GTT-BLUEPRINT.md (same folder, one level up). Analysis only, not legal advice. Nothing sent.

## The short answer
On the wording of the law: **yes**. GTT, or a sister company, can become an approved pathology owner (Approved Pathology Authority) with its own small "specialised" lab (Category S) that does only the pregnancy glucose tolerance test. GTT would hold the lease, employ the collectors, run the experience and claim Medicare. Nobody has publicly done a glucose-only lab like this, and three questions need written answers before money is spent.

## What changed since Round 9
| Point | Round 9 said | Now verified |
|---|---|---|
| Government application fees | A$750 for a specialised lab | **A$0.** Abolished from 1 July 2025 by the Health Insurance (Pathology) (Fees) (Repeal) Act 2025 |
| Collection room tax | A$2,000 per 2 years | Still A$2,000. No exemption for a specialised lab |
| Can a specialised lab claim item 66548? | Not checked | **Yes.** 66548 is a chemistry test with no lab-type limit. Collection fee 73928 (A$5.95) also stays |

## Who GTT must have
| Person | Rule |
|---|---|
| Specialist doctor in charge (endocrinologist best fit, obstetrician possible). Must NOT be a pathologist | "Full time" role, but the law never defines "full time" |
| Senior lab scientist (5 years plus a fellowship or a PhD) on site every hour the lab is open | Part-time works because duty covers opening hours only. Cover for leave is needed |
| A second scientist for relief and to act as quality manager | Required by national lab standards |
| Collectors (Certificate III) | Must be employed by the lab owner, so GTT employs them itself. This is the legal way to keep them |

| 1 | GTT-owned specialised lab | Yes, if NATA accepts the doctor and the hours |
| 2 | Same, with a specialist practice contracted in as a joint venture | Yes. Health lawyer needed on referral rules |
| 3 | Buy an existing lab-owning company | Yes, if a seller exists |
| 4 | Glucose-only general lab under a chemical pathologist | Yes, but costliest and lower collection fee |
| 5 | Outside lab sub-leases only the blood room; GTT keeps lease, lounge, brand, hires | No (sub-lease), but fastest and legal. Can run first, then switch |
Barred: GTT owning collection rooms with no lab of its own (Health Insurance Act s 23DA(1)); GTT-employed collectors working for an outside lab (s 16A(5AB)(c)).
