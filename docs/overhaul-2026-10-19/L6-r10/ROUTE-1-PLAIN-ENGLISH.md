# Route 1 in plain English

Grace, 2026-10-20. Draft for Anthony. Not legal advice. Sources: CURRENT-STATE, DECISION-LOG, Catherine Round 9.

**Route 1:** a pathology lab (WDP or Clinipath) runs the Medicare-approved blood collection centre inside GTT. The lab employs the collectors. GTT finds them. The lounge is declared a shared area.

## 1. The paper
- GTT signs the main lease with the landlord. The landlord must agree in writing.
- GTT signs a second paper with the lab for the two collection rooms and a share of the lounge, at market rent. Catherine says Medicare's form needs a sub-lease. Whether a licence is enough: not found. Ask the Department.
- Anthony insists this paper does NOT cover: exclusive use of the lounge, the treatment rooms, the booking list, or the therapists. Those stay GTT's.

## 2. Wages
- GTT recruits. The lab hires, trains and pays the collectors. Medicare only pays when the lab's own employee collects.
- Today the model has GTT paying each collector A$6,682.28 a month. If the lab charges nothing, GTT saves that: A$13,364.52 for two.
- Net at 18 a morning: +A$48,550.92 a month, up from +A$35,186.40. Break-even falls to 6.235 clients.
- If the lab wants a fee for the collectors: not in repo.
- Lounge rent from the lab: amount not in repo.

## 3. Medicare
The lab bills Medicare and keeps the money. Test (item 66548): fee A$19.90, benefit A$16.95. Collection (item 73928): fee A$5.95, benefit A$5.10. GTT is not paid by Medicare.

## 4. The client's morning (first pair, 25-minute steps)
| Time | Client | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Before 07:00 | Arrives fasting, checked in | GTT host |
| 07:00 | First blood draw (5 min) | Lab collector |
| 07:05 | 45-minute treatment | GTT therapist |
| 07:25 and 07:50 | Next pairs arrive and draw | Host, collector |
| 08:00 | 1-hour draw | Collector |
| 08:05 | Second 45-minute treatment | GTT therapist |
| 09:00 | 2-hour draw, then she goes home | Collector, host |
| 10:20 | Last pair of the day has its first draw | Collector |

Two chairs, nine pairs, 18 clients. Glucose drink timing: not in repo, the lab sets it. Resting between steps in the lounge: host watches, collector responds.

## 5. What the client pays
A$0 if the lab bulk bills. A$3.80 if the lab bills the full fee. Booking page must say: "You need a doctor request. Medicare covers the test. You pay A$0 or A$3.80, and the lab decides which. The lab's staff take your blood. Treatments are extra." Lab choice is not yet made.

## 6. Control
| GTT controls | Lab controls |
|---|---|
| Main lease, lounge look and rules | Medicare approval and billing |
| Bookings, 07:00 start, prices | Collectors' pay and discipline |
| Therapists, host, treatments | Clinical safety, the 2 hours |
| Recruiting collectors | Test handling and results |

## 7. Three risks
1. The Department or lab refuses the lounge as shared. Fix: ask both before signing; keep today's model until yes.
2. Centre must be reasonably open to the public. Fix: published hours, public booking, small public waiting spot.
3. Lab short-staffs or changes terms. Fix: contract fixes 07:00, a named backup collector, and notice before changes.

## North star: GTT employs the team, GTT controls the lease
- Met: GTT employs therapists and host, holds the main lease, recruits collectors, runs bookings.
- Not met: the lab, not GTT, employs collectors and holds clinical control of its rooms.
- Nearest compliant step: write GTT's recruiting, roster and veto rights into the contract. Keep Route 3 as the 2-year option.
