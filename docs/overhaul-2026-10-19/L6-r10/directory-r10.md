Key facts
- St John of God Pathology no longer exists. It was sold to Clinical Labs on 10 Oct 2016.
- Parent of Clinipath is Sonic Healthcare. Parent of WDP is Healius Limited. Parent of ACL is Australian Clinical Labs Limited.
- No NATA lab scope names glucose tolerance testing. GTT is a collection centre service.
- Only WDP and Clinipath publish a route to open a new collection centre.
- A secondary source says APA, APP and APL fees ended 1 July 2025. U. Re-check before quoting fees.
