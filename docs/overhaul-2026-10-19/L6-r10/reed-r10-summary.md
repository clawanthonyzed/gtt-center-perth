# Reed R10 summary: can GTT buy or franchise a pathology collection room?

Compiled 2026-10-20. Research only, nobody contacted. Full file: reed-r10-franchise-and-partners.md (same folder).

## Short answers
1. Franchise? No evidence of one. No Australian lab network was found offering to sell or franchise a collection centre (searched, nothing found).
2. Closest offer: "Licensed Collection Centre in your practice". WDP, Laverty, Dorevitch and QML use the same line; PathWest says it places collection "within your practice" (76 licensed points in WA). No prices or terms are published. (Verified on their sites.)
3. Buy one? Not possible. The Medicare approval belongs to the lab company, for the site. GTT could only own one by becoming an approved lab owner itself (12 to 24 months, specialist doctor needed). (Verified, legal library.)
4. Set one up inside GTT? Yes. The lab sub-leases the room from GTT and applies for the approval. This is the existing Rank 1 plan.

## How the usual deal works
- Host holds the lease, lab sub-leases or licenses a small room (often under 50 sq m).
- Lab pays rent at market value. More than 20 per cent above market is a breach (secondary source).
- Lab employs the collector. Medicare only pays if the lab's own employee collects (verified statute).
- Lab bills Medicare. The host never does.
- No per-collection fee or volume-linked payment to a host (verified statute).
- The payment rules are in Part IIBA (sections 23DZZIA to 23DZZIU). Sections 23DNK and 23DNL are about signage and unapproved centres, not payments.
- Key safeguard: no GTT owner or connected person should be a doctor, midwife or nurse practitioner who requests tests. Please confirm; I have not assumed.

## WA examples of labs in someone else's premises
- Clinipath inside Carousel Family Medical Centre, Cannington (search summary).
- WDP inside Cannington Medical and Dental Centre, and in a serviced office at 300 Murray Street (WDP page read).
- ACL at a Forrestfield medical centre and inside St John of God Subiaco Hospital (search summary).
- No WA pharmacy-hosted example found. No wellness or luxury venue example found either.
- GTT's own file: WDP's "venue-rental clinic" model has WDP keeping the collector's employment; no rent figure has arrived.

## Three structures to propose (ranked)
1. Lab sub-leases the room and lounge from GTT at valuer's market rent. GTT recruits and nominates collectors, lab is sole employer. Exclusive 07:00 to 10:30, appointment only, GTT brand leads.
2. WDP-style session licence. Lab supplies and employs collectors. GTT keeps a veto on named people. Fallback only.
3. Two-lab panel: one primary lab, a second for a defined slot or backup. Strongest bargaining, most complex.

## Ask list (judgement on likely answers)
- GTT-recruited collectors: Clinipath and ACL likely yes to nominations; WDP unlikely at first; PathWest unlikely.
- Exclusive early window and appointment-only: likely yes at WDP, Clinipath, ACL.
- Brand rights, service targets, 90-day exit, cover collector within 24 hours, data split: standard asks, yes in principle.
- Rent: ask for a valuer's figure, never tied to volume.
