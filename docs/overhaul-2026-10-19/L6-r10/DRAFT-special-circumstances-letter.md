**Request for written guidance before an approved collection centre application: GTT Center Perth, Western Australia**

GTT Center Perth Pty Ltd is establishing WA's first premium waiting experience for pregnant women undergoing the oral glucose tolerance test (MBS item 66548). The test asks a fasting pregnant woman to stay for two hours between three blood draws. Our aim is simple: make that two hours comfortable, private and calm in a dedicated sanctuary setting, so that more women complete the test that screens for gestational diabetes.

We are not a pathology provider and we do not request pathology tests. We intend to partner with an approved pathology authority that owns an eligible laboratory. The authority would hold its own approved collection centre in our premises and do all analysis. Our model is:

- GTT Center Perth will hold the head lease of the venue (not yet secured) and run the lounge and the guest experience.
- The authority occupies a dedicated collection room under a signed agreement with us, at nil or a flat fee, with no payment linked to tests or requests.
- Collectors are recruited with our help but are employed solely by the authority, which directs all collection and clinical care.
- Bookings start at 07:00, a new pair of clients every 25 minutes, with the last first draw at 10:20 and final draws by about 12:20, six days a week. Two collection chairs; at most 18 clients a morning.

Before our partner lodges form HW035, we would be grateful for a dated written reply to these questions:

1. **Occupancy and shared lounge.** Will the delegate accept an agreement between the authority and GTT Center Perth as head tenant (with the owner's written consent) as the authority's entitlement to occupy under section 23DNBA(1)? Which box at question 11 of form HW035 applies? May the lounge be shown on the floor plan as a "common area used by the ACC and one or more other occupants", so that clients waiting between draws remain within the approved centre, and must an unpaid shared area be counted in the total area?
2. **Reasonably accessible.** How does the delegate apply "reasonably accessible to the public" in section 23DNG(1)(ba)? Is an appointment-based centre, open to any member of the public with a valid request and listed publicly, treated as reasonably accessible?
3. **Employee.** We understand sections 16A(5AA)(d) and 16A(5AB)(c) require collectors to be employees of the authority. Can GTT Center Perth recruit and nominate candidates, with the authority as sole employer, without affecting approval or Medicare benefits?
4. **Category S.** Would a laboratory performing only glucose tolerance testing, under a specialist medical practitioner as designated person, be considered for Category S, and could it then hold a centre under section 7(4) of the Approvals for Eligible Collection Centres Principles 2020?
5. **Operations.** Does any rule prevent GTT Center Perth from setting booking times, the guest experience and premises standards by contract, while the authority keeps full control of collection, specimens and clinical safety?
6. **Hours.** Are published morning hours from 07:00, with collections ending by about 12:30, six days a week, treated as "reasonable hours" under section 23DNG(1)(bb) for a centre built around a fasting test?

We also ask: if any item of the Guidelines for Approved Pathology Collection Centres cannot practically be met in a lounge setting, what would the delegate need from the authority to accept that item as not reasonably practicable, as contemplated by section 8(2)(d)(i) of the Principles? Is there any other discretion or pre-lodgement process we should know about?

We will design the venue to whatever standard you set. We would welcome a short call with our intended partner if that would help.
