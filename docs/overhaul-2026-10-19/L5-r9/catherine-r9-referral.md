# Catherine R9: referrals, telehealth, client billing, collector staffing (plain summary)

2026-10-19. Analysis only, not legal advice. Full research: /opt/openclaw/second-brain/knowledge/legal/REFERRAL-TELEHEALTH-BILLING-MECHANICS.md. Nothing sent externally.

## 1. Is a referral valid at GTT?

| Question | Answer | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Must the request name GTT or a collection centre? | **No.** The law lists what a request needs: requester name and address or provider number, patient name and address, the test, the date. No venue | Health Insurance Regulations 2018 ss 33 to 35 (VERIFIED, legislation.gov.au) |
| Must it say "GTT"? | It must describe the test clearly enough to identify the item. Ask clinicians to write "OGTT pregnancy" so it maps to item 66548, not 66542 | Regs s 35(a) |
| Is an open request good at any approved collection centre? | **Yes.** Doctors may name a lab only on clinical grounds and must then write the grounds. Branded lab forms must say she can go to any provider | HIA s 16A(3A); Regs s 36; Reg 69(2) |
| Who can request 66548? | GPs and any doctor; **participating midwives (66548 is named in their list)**; participating nurse practitioners | HIA s 16A(1); Regs ss 30, 31; MBS note PN.1.1 (VERIFIED) |
| Catch | Midwives can request 66548 but **not** 66542 | Regs s 30 |

## 2. Patient relationship, telehealth and a clinician network

| Clinician | Telehealth for a new client? | Source |
|---|---|---|
| GP | Only if seen face to face in the last 12 months, or she is MyMedicare-registered with that practice. **No pregnancy exemption** | AskMBS advisory, 1 Nov 2025 (VERIFIED) |
| Nurse practitioner | Same 12-month rule, no pregnancy exemption | Same; MBS note MN.0.1 |
| **Participating midwife** | **No 12-month rule found.** Midwife video and phone antenatal items (91211, 91218) only need to be safe, clinically appropriate and out of hospital | MBS note MN.13.15 (VERIFIED). Confirm with AskMBS |

**Can GTT partner with independent bulk-billing clinicians? Yes, if they stay truly independent.**

The big trap: if GTT **employs, contracts or directs** a clinician, GTT itself becomes a "requester" (s 23DZZIE(1)(b), (c)). Then lab collectors placed at GTT's premises can become a banned benefit (s 23DZZIF(7)(b)). That would break the lab deal. Penalty up to 6,000 penalty units for a company.

New flag: if GTT holds strong control over lab collectors, GTT might count as a "provider" (s 23DZZIE(3)(d)). A provider must not give clinicians free rooms or perks.

**Safe network recipe**
1. Clinicians are independent: their own ABN, their own provider number, their own records and their own bulk-billing choice.
2. They rent a room from GTT at independently valued market rent. No discounts, no per-referral fees.
3. They decide on clinical grounds and can use any lab.
4. GTT shows a patient-choice statement and accepts any valid request from anyone.
5. Never mix a GTT-employed clinician model with the lab collection centre model.
6. Medical Board code 10.12.8: "Not offering inducements or entering into arrangements that could be perceived to provide inducements" (VERIFIED).

## 3. If the lab bills the client

| | 66548 | 73928 | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Schedule fee | A$19.90 | A$5.95 | **A$25.85** |
| Medicare pays back (85%, rounded up to 5 cents) | A$16.95 | A$5.10 | **A$22.05** |
| Her gap | A$2.95 | A$0.85 | **A$3.80** |

- **How she claims:** on the spot at the lab after paying, or the Medicare online account or myGov app (usually paid within 7 days), or by mail (usually 21 days), or at a service centre (Services Australia, VERIFIED).
- **Safety net 2026:** after A$594.40 of gaps in the year, Medicare pays 100% of the schedule fee, so her gap becomes A$0. Only payments made in full before claiming count (VERIFIED).
- **Above schedule fee:** no cap found. A lab billing privately may charge more, and she pays the extra.
- **Bulk-billing:** she signs an assignment form. The lab takes A$22.05 "in full payment", so she pays A$0 (s 20A; Regs s 65C).
- **Collection at a room that is not approved:** no Medicare benefit at all, and nothing can be bulk-billed. GTT must never do this (s 16A(5AA); s 23DNL).
- **Consumer law:** ACCC says businesses must "clearly disclose ... at the start of a purchasing process the types of fees that will apply". Never say "free" if a gap can apply (ACL s 18). Doctors must tell patients their fees in time (Medical Board code 4.5.3).

**DRAFT wording (needs Anthony's approval, Publishing Shield)**
1. Your glucose test is done by [Lab name] in its approved collection room inside GTT.
2. You need a pathology request from your GP, obstetrician, midwife or nurse practitioner. Any lab's form is fine.
3. You can take your request to any pathology provider. Choosing GTT is your choice.
4. [If bulk-billed] [Lab name] bulk-bills the test. You pay A$0 for the test.
5. [If billed privately] [Lab name] charges A$25.85. Medicare pays back A$22.05. Your cost is A$3.80. We tell you before you book.
6. The lounge package is a separate GTT charge. It does not include the test and has no Medicare rebate.
7. Questions about the test fee? Ask us before you book.

## 4. Collector staffing

| Option | Works for Medicare? |
|---|---|
| Lab employs GTT-recruited collectors, GTT holds contract levers | **Yes (best)** |
| Dual employment | Doubtful |
| Labour hire (GTT or an agency employs) | **No**: the collector must be the lab's employee (s 16A(5AB)(c)). WA has no labour hire licence scheme (secondary sources) |
| Contractor collector | No |

- **Qualification:** HLT37525 Certificate III in Pathology (Pathology Collection), with at least 35 hours of work placement. training.gov.au says "No licensing, legislative or certification requirements apply" (VERIFIED). There is no personal licence.
- **Volume pitch:** 473.94 clients a month x A$22.05 = **A$10,450.38 a month, A$125,404.52 a year** in Medicare revenue to the lab. The lab's own costs: NOT FOUND.
- **Levers:** guaranteed booked volume in a fixed morning window, requests checked before arrival, rooms fitted to the lab's spec at market rent, GTT recruits and trains in hospitality, low no-shows, a 2-year term matching the approval. **Never offer** per-test payments, rebates or free rent.
- **Fallback (BATNA):** (1) another lab: Clinipath, WDP, ACL, PathWest; (2) private billing with the A$3.80 gap disclosed; (3) GTT's own Category S lab (long term); (4) practitioner-led model without an approved centre (loses item 73928; health lawyer first).
