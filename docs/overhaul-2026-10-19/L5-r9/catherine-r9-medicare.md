## The big win

**The "must stay in our waiting room" rule is not law.** It is the lab's own policy (WDP, PathWest).
- The national collection centre rules (NPAAC Guidelines) have no waiting-room rule at all.
- The official application form (HW035) lets a collection centre include **"each common area used by the ACC and one or more other occupants"**, sized in square metres.
- So GTT's lounge can be put on the lab's floor plan and sub-lease as a **shared common area**. A client relaxing in the lounge is then legally "in the collection centre".

This keeps the lounge, keeps the 07:00 model, and keeps bulk billing (A$0 to the client) through the lab.

## 2. Lab categories in plain English

| Category | Plain meaning | Boss | On site | Can own a collection centre? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| GX | Big general lab | Pathologist | At least 1 full-time pathologist | Yes |
| GY | Satellite of a GX lab | GX's pathologist | Pathologist for some tests | Yes |
| B | Branch, can be a point-of-care-only lab in a lab network | Parent lab's pathologist | Scientist manager | No |
| M | Small lab for one medical practice's own patients | A doctor | Trained operator | No |
| S | Specialist lab, limited tests, one patient group | Full-time specialist doctor (not a pathologist) | **Clinical scientist on site all opening hours** | Yes, for its own tests only |

**Could GTT run a glucose-only lab?**
- **Category S** fits the words: an obstetrician-led lab doing GTTs for pregnant women. Big costs: full-time specialist, on-site scientist, NATA. 12 to 24 months (estimate).
- **Fingerstick tests by GTT staff: no.** There is no Medicare item for it, and it is not a recognised way to diagnose gestational diabetes.

The lounge share must be charged at **market rent** (form declaration). The floor plan is drawn by GTT's architect, not an agent.

## 4. Public access trap

- The Minister **may** cancel a centre "not reasonably accessible to the public" or without "reasonable hours" (s 23DNG(1)(ba), (bb)).
- These words are **not defined**. No guidance found.
- **No rule found that forces walk-ins all day.**

**Safe workarounds**

| # | Workaround |
|---|---|
| W1 | Anyone with a valid request can book; centre listed publicly |
| W2 | Street door or corridor straight to the collection room, plus a small public waiting spot |
| W3 | Fixed published hours, for example 07:00 to 11:00, Monday to Saturday |
| W4 | Lab also collects other tests for walk-ins in those hours |
| W5 | Lounge used only by GTT bookings; public use the public spot |
