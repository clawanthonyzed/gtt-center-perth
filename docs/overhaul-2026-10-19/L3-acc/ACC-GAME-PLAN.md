## E1 How GTT's rooms become an approved collection centre (ACC)

GTT cannot hold the approval. The lab (an approved pathology authority, APA) applies for GTT's rooms. The Minister's delegate grants it.

| # | Step | Who does it | Document | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lab is eligible: APA that runs and owns a category G (GX or GY) or S lab | Lab | Existing APA number | [LIB: HIA-SECTION-16A.md, Principles s 7(2), (4)]; [LIB: SERVICES-AUSTRALIA-ACC-PROCESS.md, s 2] |
| 2 | Lab gets a legal right to the rooms: a **sub-lease**, not a licence | GTT grants, lab signs | Sub-lease at independently valued market rent | s 23DA(1) "owned, leased or sub-leased"; s 23DNBA(1) [LIB: HIA-SECTION-16A.md] |
| 3 | Floor plan: trading name, address, room size in sqm, room and shared areas coloured, shared rented area. Must equal the lease area | GTT's architect draws; lab lodges | Floor plan | [LIB: SERVICES-AUSTRALIA-ACC-PROCESS.md, s 2] |
| 4 | Equipment on site for collecting and preparing specimens | GTT fits out to the lab's written spec; lab signs off | Lab specification | Principles s 9(2) |
| 5 | Trained staff, employed or controlled by the lab | Lab | Staff list or roster | Principles s 9(3) |
| 6 | Undertaking to follow the NPAAC Collection Centre Guidelines, and report any breach within 24 hours | Lab | Written undertaking | Principles s 8(2)(d) |
| 7 | NPAAC items: approval certificate shown, cleanable surfaces, secure storage, floor coverings, hours displayed, specimen storage away from food, staff ID, attire, privacy and consent policies, first aid | GTT builds; lab runs | Lab's NPAAC checklist | [LIB: npaac/NPAAC-COLLECTION-CENTRES-GUIDELINES, S1.1, S2.2, S3.1 to S3.3] |
| 8 | Pay the A$2,000 tax (not needed only if co-located with a GX or GY lab) | Lab | EFT receipt | s 23DNBA(2); [LIB: SERVICES-AUSTRALIA-ACC-PROCESS.md, s 2] |
| 9 | Lodge the ACC application form | Lab | Application plus all above | Principles s 8(1) |
| 10 | Decision and written notice; centre number issued | Delegate | Approval letter | Principles s 10; s 23DNBB |
| 11 | Approval runs **2 years**, then renew | Lab | Renewal | Principles s 11(1) |
| 12 | If refused: ask for reconsideration | Lab | Request | s 23DO(2DA), cited in Principles s 10(b) |

**Timelines:** no fixed processing time. "Processing times vary and there's no guarantee that late applications can be processed within the desired timeframe." Lease changes must reach Services Australia within 14 days [LIB: SERVICES-AUSTRALIA-ACC-PROCESS.md, s 2].

**Keeping it:** revocable if not "reasonably accessible to the public", without "reasonable hours", or below a minimum benefit in any 6 months (amount NOT FOUND) [LIB: s 23DNG(1)(ba), (bb), (1A)].

**"Will the lab allow it, or does Services Australia decide?" Both.** The lab must choose to apply (nobody else can), then the delegate grants or refuses. So the lab deal comes first.

## E3 Staffing and control: the central dilemma

**The rule that decides it:** at an ACC, Medicare pays only if the specimen is collected by "an employee of an approved pathology authority that is the proprietor of the laboratory" [LIB: HIA-SECTION-16A.md, s 16A(5AA)(d)(ii), (5AB)(c)].

| Mechanism | Rating | Why |
|---|---|---|
| (a) Lab employs GTT-recruited staff, GTT holds contractual levers | **Safe** | Lab is the employer, so s 16A(5AB)(c) is met |
| (b) Dual or joint employment | **Doubtful** | "Employee" undefined [NOT FOUND]. Arguable only with separate contracts and separate hours |
| (c) Secondment or labour hire (GTT or an agency is employer) | **Fails for Medicare** | Not the lab's employee. Approval possible, benefit not |
| (d) Principles s 9(3)(b)(ii) "engaged directly or indirectly ... under a contract that gives the APA power to generally direct or control" | **Approval only** | s 16A(5AB)(c) says "employee" only |
| (e) Personal collector licence or ID | **Does not exist** | See below |
| (f)(iii) Independent practitioner engages GTT to collect | **Arguable, high risk** | See below |
| (f)(iv) GTT engages the practitioner, GTT staff collect | **Arguable, textually cleaner** | See below |

**(a) Control levers GTT can lawfully hold** (lab stays employer and keeps the power to "generally direct or control", Principles s 9(3)(b)):
1. Named dedicated collector pool for GTT's rooms only.
2. GTT recruits and nominates; the lab hires on its own contract.
3. GTT may request removal; the lab acts reasonably and promptly.
4. GTT service standards adopted as the lab's site policy. NPAAC lets attire follow "the APA's policies" (S3.2), so the lab can adopt GTT's uniform style with lab ID (S3.1).
5. Rostering protocol: fixed 07:00 to 10:30 cadence.
6. KPIs: draw on time at 60 and 120 minutes, client feedback.
7. Hospitality training by GTT on top of the lab's clinical training.
8. Replacement within a set time if a collector is absent.
9. Step-in: the lab must supply a relief collector.
10. Termination if KPIs fail.

Keep out: GTT setting pay, disciplining, or directing clinical work (drifts toward (b) or (c)).

**(e) Personal accreditation:** none exists. The ACC number is per centre (s 23DNBB). NPAAC sets ID, attire, policies and first aid (S3.1 to S3.3) and lab assessment of "competency of staff" (Requirements, App. A item 2) [LIB: npaac]. No qualification is named; industry norm is Certificate III HLT37525 [Grace file]. Standing comes from the lab's sign-off, not a licence.

**(f) The practitioner route, tested**
- Text: Medicare pays without an ACC if the specimen is collected "on behalf of the treating practitioner, by an employee of, or by a person engaged under a contract for services by or on behalf of, the treating practitioner" (s 16A(5AA)(c)(iii)). Or, if the practitioner is employed or engaged by a medical entrepreneur, collection may be "by another employee of that medical entrepreneur" (c)(iv) [LIB: HIA-SECTION-16A.md].
- (iii) with GTT's company as contractor: "person" includes a company [GK: Acts Interpretation Act s 2C], but whether a GTT employee's draw is collection "by" GTT is untested. **Doubtful.**
- (iv) is cleaner: GTT engaging the practitioner makes GTT a medical entrepreneur (s 3B), so its own employees may collect.
- She must be that practitioner's patient (s 16A(1)); a lab may be named only on clinical grounds (s 16A(3A)).
- **Part IIBA catches GTT either way:** a "requester" under (iv) (s 23DZZIE(1)(b)); "connected" under (iii) (s 23DZZIJ(1)(j)). Lab staff or equipment at GTT is excluded (s 23DZZIF(7)(b)); free tubes or courier need market consideration (s 23DZZIF(6)). Penalty up to 6,000 penalty units for a company (s 23DZZIL).
- Money: the lab bulk-bills the test only (s 20A). **Item 73928 is lost** (needs "an approved collection centre") [LIB: MBS file]. GTT pays its own collectors.
- Lounge package covering collection: no rule found either way [NOT FOUND]. Health lawyer.
- **New risk, s 23DNL:** an unapproved collection centre must tell the client and lab that "a medicare benefit would not be payable", which clashes with (5AA)(c). Health lawyer.
- Network needs: independent practitioners, genuine consults, no per-referral pay, their own choice of lab. **Mixing with (a) is doubtful** (s 23DZZIF(7)(b)).

**Best "GTT keeps control" package:**
1. Primary: (a), with all ten levers in a Collector Services Schedule to the sub-lease.
2. Parallel long-term: GTT's own Category S lab (E4). It is the only route where GTT's own employees collect at an ACC with Medicare paying.
3. Keep (f)(iv) as a documented contingency, never mixed with (a).

## E4 Barrier to entry: GTT as its own APA and lab

**Statutory conditions** [LIB: APA-BARRIER-TO-ENTRY.md]
1. APA: an undertaking accepted by the Minister, up to 12 months, then renew (s 23DF(1), 23DG).
2. Fit and proper person test, which looks through owners and controllers (s 23DF(6), (7)).
3. Lab approval: names services, category and a period of up to 3 years (s 23DN(2A)). Needs a current assessment report from NATA or another approved body (Principles s 11(2)) [WEB: NATA ISO 15189, search result only].
4. Medicare-eligible categories are only **GX, GY and S** (F2026L00272 s 6(1)). "Category G" does not exist in the current instrument.
5. A designated person directs and supervises (s 18). The Supervision Requirements, 8th edition 2025, are NOT in the library.
6. A practitioner who performs the tests needs an approved pathology practitioner (APP) undertaking (s 23DC; s 16A(2)).
7. APA documents: proprietor, directors, shareholders, fit and proper details (F2018L01381 Sch 1).

**Fees [WEB estimate, not verified]:** GX A$2,500; M and S A$750. APA fee may have been removed (unverified). NATA cost NOT FOUND. ACC tax A$2,000 per centre.

**Category S, from the text only:** under "full-time direction, control and supervision" of "a medical practitioner with specialised scope of practice" who is "not a pathologist". It does "a limited range of pathology testing ... for a target patient population or of a specialised nature", restricted to that person's scope (s 17(1) item 5).
- An obstetrician-led, glucose-only lab for pregnant women appears to fit the words. Department view needed.
- A Category S APA may then apply for ACCs that collect "only specimens for the kind of pathology services" its lab is approved for (Principles s 7(4)).
- GTT's own employees would then be "employee[s] of an approved pathology authority that is the proprietor" (s 16A(5AB)(c)), so full control and Medicare both work.

**Verdict:** possible but slow and costly. Nearest practical route: the lab partner holds the approval; GTT holds the sub-lease.

**Staged path if GTT ever wants its own:** (1) lab partner model first; (2) recruit an obstetrician as designated person and APP; (3) build a glucose lab, NATA ISO 15189 assessment, QA program; (4) lodge the APA undertaking and Category S application; (5) lodge ACC applications for GTT rooms (Principles s 7(4)); (6) exit the partner when its sub-lease ends.

## E5 Referral journey and independent network

1. She can take her request anywhere. Branded forms must say "the request may be taken to a pathology provider of the person's choice" [LIB: HIA-PATHOLOGY-REQUESTS..., reg 69(2)]. A practitioner may name a lab only on clinical grounds (s 16A(3A)).
2. GTT **may** list independent bulk-billing GPs, midwives and nurse practitioners, and accept any valid request.
3. GTT **may not** pay per referral, give practitioners perks linked to requests (s 23DZZIF(7)(a), (8)), or steer requests to one lab.
4. "Patient" is undefined [NOT FOUND]. In practice it means a genuine consult, a clinical record, and the practitioner judging the test "reasonably necessary" (s 16A(12)(b)).
5. Telehealth: GP and nurse practitioner telehealth needs a face-to-face visit in the last 12 months or MyMedicare registration. There is no pregnancy exemption [WEB: AskMBS advisory, cited R7 D6]. Midwife telehealth rules: [NOT FOUND].

**Journey:** request from her own practitioner, book and upload, 07:00 lab collector checks it and she signs the bulk-bill form, draws in the lab's rooms, wait in the lounge, results to her doctor.
