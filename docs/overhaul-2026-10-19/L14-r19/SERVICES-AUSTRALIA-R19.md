## 1. Short answer

**Services Australia publishes no public email inbox for pathology approvals or for general Medicare provider enquiries.** I searched harder than in R18 (official pages, official forms, a Department of Health document, a stealth browser pass on the server because plain fetches timed out). Two email addresses do exist in official documents, but neither is published as a general enquiry inbox. The best official route is the Department-first route Anthony already approved (email 1 and 2 ask the Department to forward) plus a posted letter.

## 3. Best route, in order

1. **Keep email 1 and 2 (Department of Health, verified inboxes).** The Department's Pathology Collection Centres Compliance Team and AskMBS are the Department's own policy contacts, and Services Australia administers the approvals under the Department's rules. Both emails already ask to be forwarded. Verified inboxes, so no bounce risk.
2. **Add a posted letter to Services Australia Pathology Approvals, GPO Box 9822, Sydney NSW 2000** (candidate C, verified). Same text as the universal email, printed and signed "Anthony". This gives the written record Anthony wants and costs a stamp.
3. **Optional extra recipient on email 3: candidate A** (pathology.registration@). It is the only address any official document ties to "enquiries about pathology applications". Risk: stale, may bounce. If it bounces, nothing is lost; the letter in step 2 still stands. Anthony decides.
4. **Do not use candidate B** unless A bounces and Anthony wants to try: it is the practitioner-undertaking inbox of the Director, Medicare Providers, Pathology and Diagnostic Imaging Section. The right team, the wrong use.
5. Later, once an ABN and a PRODA account exist: HPOS messaging (candidate F). Phone lines (1300 721 546, 1300 302 122) are not used because Anthony said emails only.
