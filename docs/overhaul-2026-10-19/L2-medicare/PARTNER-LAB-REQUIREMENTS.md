## 1. Universal baseline (applies to every lab, as summarised in the repo)

Source: docs/architecture/PARTNER-ACCREDITATION-STANDARDS-COMPARISON.md s1 and s5 (NPAAC Tier 3B Third Edition 2013, read in full by the repo on 2026-09-08). [REPO: that file]
- Surfaces non-porous and cleanable; secure supply storage; space for patients to sit or recline plus a staff work surface; specimens never stored with food, drink or pharmaceuticals; approval certificate displayed.
- Staff: identification, attire per the lab's policy, policies on privacy, confidentiality and consent, basic first aid trained. No named qualification in NPAAC; each lab sets its own competency standard. Industry norm is Certificate III in Pathology (HLT37215, superseded 21 Dec 2025 by HLT37525).
- L-medicare adds (repo finding, for Catherine to confirm): only the lab can apply for and hold the collection centre approval; the lab files the occupation agreement and floor plan; the lab gives an undertaking to follow NPAAC so every lab will inspect and sign off the room; Medicare pays only where the collector is the pathologist or the lab's employee; a specimen fridge inside the room is supported if secure and for specimens only. [REPO: MEDICARE-FEASIBILITY-REPORT.md s2 and headline box]

How these are usually structured (from the sources, not general knowledge): the host supplies space, the lab runs the collection, and the lab's own employees staff it (the Laverty example is explicit; the others do not say). Healius network: 2,063 approved collection centres out of 2,158 pathology sites [WEB: https://www.healius.com.au/our-brands/ via search summary].

Rent and regulation (commentary only, NOT interpreted, Catherine to assess): public law-firm and media commentary says rents for co-located collection centres are expected not to differ substantially from market value (read by the AMA as within 20 percent), new leases for co-located centres were to be lodged with the Department of Health from 1 July 2018, and a Federal Court penalty of A$1.65 million was imposed on a Healius subsidiary in August 2023 over above-market rents. [WEB: https://hwlebsworth.com.au/pathology-collection-centres-responding-to-doh-please-explain-letters-about-rent-2/ ; https://jws.com.au/what-we-think/medicares-first-penalty-against-a-pathology-provider-for-paying-above-market-rents ; https://www.ama.com.au/gp-network-news/compliance-check-pathology-approved-collection-centres-continues]. These sources date from 2016 to 2023 and may be out of date. Practical consequence for the negotiation: any rent in either direction needs an independent market valuation. The repo already says this. ## 5. One-page negotiation fact sheet: zero-fee placement (internal, NOT a message to any lab)

What GTT brings
- A fixed, repeatable clinical load: 18 expecting mothers a morning, Monday to Saturday (about 474 a month), one two-hour test each, booked in advance. [REPO: CURRENT-STATE.md; GST-SPLIT-MODEL.md]
- Predictable timing: first booking 07:00, 25-minute pair cadence, last draw finishing by about 10:30, matching WDP's 10:30 guidance and PathWest's preference for early starts. Hard 60 and 120 minute draw marks are built into the schedule. [REPO: CURRENT-STATE.md; scenario-c-sync-timetables.md; PATHOLOGY-PARTNER-TABLE.md]
- Gross Medicare schedule value to the lab of about A$12,251 a month (A$147,016 a year) at 18 a morning, before the lab's costs. [DERIVED, see s4]
- Rooms built to the lab's own specification: two 19.0 sqm private rooms, one chair each, basin, fridge, bench, recliner, wheelchair approach, power and data; specification agreed in writing before fit-out. [REPO: F4-COLLECTION-ROOM-SIZE.md]
- Clients stay seated and on site, supervised by GTT hosts through the wait, with a venue manager responsible for timing and wellbeing. [REPO: wdp-reply-carole-2026-08-07.md; wdp-reply-carole-2026-08-03.md]
- Referral base: midwives and obstetricians, not GPs on site (WDP has said doctors on site are not a concern). [REPO: VERIFICATION-TRACKER.md 29d]
- Afternoon: rooms free from about 10:30 for the lab's own public patients; volume is the lab's to judge, no GTT figure exists.
- One end-of-day courier pickup, grey top tubes. [REPO: PATHOLOGY-PARTNER-TABLE.md]

What GTT asks (all in writing, same questions to each lab)
1. The lab holds the collection centre approval for the rooms on an occupation agreement and places its own employed collectors, at no placement fee to GTT. Confirm whether two collectors are required. [REPO: L-medicare s1 workaround 1; s4 questions]
2. Bulk-billing confirmed in writing with no patient charge, and the lab's private fee stated if bulk-billing is ever unavailable. Fee in A$; "no fee quoted yet" until received. [REPO: PATHOLOGY-FEE-NEGOTIATION-POINTS.md asks 1 and 2]
3. Free tubes, bags, forms, labels, sharps; glucose drink position (PathWest does not supply it). [REPO: pathwest outreach s4e-vi]
4. Who supplies the label printer, tablet, LIS link and temperature logging; courier cost and cut-off terms.
5. Any room rent between lab and GTT at independently valued market rent, either direction, with term, exit and exclusivity stated. No money linked to doctor request volumes. Catherine to review before any term is accepted. [REPO: PATHOLOGY-FEE-NEGOTIATION-POINTS.md "Points to avoid"]
6. Written room specification and sign-off process before fit-out; medical waste covered; insurance certificates for the collector and client during the wait.
7. How the lab wants the client supervised during the wait (sightline or quick response).

Fallbacks (from L-medicare, for Anthony's decision): hybrid with lab collectors doing the draws and GTT hosts doing everything else; client pays the lab privately (about A$25.85 at schedule fees); GTT absorbs it (about A$12,251 a month, profit down from A$35,186.40 to A$22,935.05). [REPO: MEDICARE-FEASIBILITY-REPORT.md]

Do not claim: that GTT holds or applies for any Medicare approval; that "employed or engaged" is enough for Medicare to pay (it is for centre approval only, per the repo); any lab fee not yet quoted. [REPO: L-medicare FOR-DOCUMENTS-AGENT.md "Do not say"]
