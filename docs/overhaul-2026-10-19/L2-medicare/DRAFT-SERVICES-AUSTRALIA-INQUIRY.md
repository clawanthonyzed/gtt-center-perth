Dear Delegate of the Minister,

**Inquiry: approved collection centre within a maternal wellness venue, Perth WA**

I am writing for GTT Center Perth, a planned day spa and maternal sanctuary in Perth. It will host a two-hour oral glucose tolerance test programme for pregnant women. GTT Center Perth will not hold any approval. The model is:

- a fitted collection suite within our premises, sub-leased at market rent to an approved pathology authority (our partner lab);
- the lab would hold the approval under section 23DNBA and run the centre with its own staff;
- clients booked 07:00 to 10:30 each morning, with the public served in the afternoon.

We would be grateful for guidance on these points:

1. What conditions you usually apply under section 23DNBA and the 2020 Approval Principles for a centre in rooms sub-leased from a non-medical operator.
2. Whether a centre used mainly by booked clients of the operator from 07:00 to 10:30, and open to the general public in the afternoon, meets section 23DNG(1)(ba), (bb) and (bc). If not, what hours or access would.
3. The amount prescribed for section 23DNG(1A), and the instrument that sets it.
4. Under Principles section 9(3), whether collectors whom we recruit and train, but who are employed by the lab under the lab's own employment contracts, are acceptable staff.
5. The meaning of "employee" in section 16A(5AB)(c). Does it cover a secondee, a labour-hire worker, or a person jointly employed by the lab and the venue?
6. Whether any special arrangement exists for specialised maternal collection facilities.

Thank you for your help.

Regards,
Anthony Zed
GTT Center Perth

**First, three questions to each lab (PathWest, WDP, Clinipath)**
1. Will you hold an approved collection centre approval for two rooms sub-leased from GTT at independently valued market rent, open to your public patients in the afternoon?
2. Will you employ the collectors directly (we can recruit candidates for you), and bulk-bill every eligible client in writing?
3. What, if anything, would you charge GTT, and what supervision rule applies during the two-hour wait in our lounge?
