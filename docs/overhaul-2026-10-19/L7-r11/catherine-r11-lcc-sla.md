## 1. How "Licensed Collection Centres" work in WA

| Question | Answer | Proof level |
|---|---|---|
| What is it called? | Labs market a "Licensed Collection Centre in your practice". In law it is an ACC held by the lab, on a lease, sub-lease or licence from the host | Verified |
| Do WDP, Clinipath, PathWest or ACL publish terms? | No. No fee, rent, staffing or equipment schedule is published | Verified (searched) |
| Who pays whom? | Normally the lab pays the host rent. Rent and licence fees are treated the same | Department guidance |
| Rent rule | Must be within 20% of market value, and never linked to tests or referrals, if the host is or is linked to a test-ordering doctor or midwife | Regulations 76 and 77, verified |
| Does it apply to GTT now? | No, GTT orders no tests. It applies the day GTT employs or is related to a doctor, midwife or nurse practitioner who orders tests | Library |
| Perth rent figure? | None found. Benchmarks are not published. Valuer needed | Not found |
| Department checks | Compares rent with nearby collection rooms in the same postcode; both too high and too low rent can breach | Verified |

## 2. Fixing the staff-control problem

The lab must employ and direct the collectors. GTT controls the result, the premises and who is placed on site, never the person's daily work.

| GTT lever | Strength | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| Written guest-experience standards the lab must adopt | Strong | Nothing clinical |
| GTT-designed uniform adopted as the lab's own policy, lab name badge | Strong | Pregnancy and religion-friendly design |
| House rules in GTT areas | Strong | Must not stop safety complaints |
| Remove a person from the site (lab replaces them) | Strong | Written conduct reasons only; never pregnancy, age, race, union |
| Approve who is placed at the site | Medium | No hiring veto; lab hires |
| Staffing to GTT's booking forecast | Strong | GTT never rosters named people |
| Replacement times, punctuality, guest score KPIs | Strong | Lab sets clinical timing |
| Mystery shopper on hospitality only | Medium | No secret audio recording |
| Fixed, capped service credits | Strong | Never per test |
| GTT staff step in to collect | Barred (no Medicare) | Use a standby lab with its own approval |

Why the limits: if GTT directs collectors in practice, a court can treat GTT as an employer (Fair Work Act s 15AA), GTT becomes a "provider" under the anti-kickback rules, and labour hire pay orders become arguable (s 306E; low risk while GTT has under 15 staff and no enterprise agreement). Safety duties are shared and cannot be contracted out (WA safety Act ss 16, 46, 272). Unions may enter GTT premises where lab members work (Fair Work Act s 481).

## 3. If the lab walks away

| Document | Must include |
|---|---|
| Head lease | Wide permitted use; pre-approved sub-letting to any lab; landlord signs Medicare paperwork on request; break or rent relief if no lab approval; related-company transfer |
| Lab agreement | 24-month lock-in; 6-month exit notice; keep collecting until the new lab is approved; cancel approval and hand over documents in 7 days; room stays with GTT; collectors free to join the next lab; fixed early-exit payment |
