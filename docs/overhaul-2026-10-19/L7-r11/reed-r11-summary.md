# Reed R11 summary: are WA fertility clinics the right partners for the GTT lounge?

2026-10-20. Research only; nobody contacted. "GTT Center Perth" is an unregistered working title. Full file: reed-r11-fertility.md (same folder).
V = verified with URL (in full file). J = judgement. U = unverified.

## Short answer
Fertility clinics are a useful audience, not the main referrer. The glucose test is at 24 to 28 weeks. Monash's own early pregnancy service stops monitoring at 16 weeks (V). The request comes from the doctor or midwife running the pregnancy, so obstetricians come first. (J)

## Key facts
- WA has 8 licensed private fertility practices (V, WA Health list reviewed 24 Jul 2026).
- Three groups cover most: Monash IVF (West Leederville ex PIVET; 80% of Fertility North agreed Dec 2023, completed Mar 2024; Ultrasound North bought Apr 2026), Virtus (IVFAustralia, Adora), City Fertility (ex Fertility Specialists of WA).
- Monash runs a private early pregnancy service (6 to 16 weeks) and names a "significant gap in care" after fertility discharge (V).
- Fertility labs are NATA category S (specialised): Fertility North, Genea Hollywood, IVFAustralia, PIVET (V). None can be the Medicare GTT collection point.
- Two City Fertility doctors are also obstetricians who deliver at St John of God Subiaco (V).
- No clinic publishes a partnership or preferred-provider page. Monash has a GP portal with an "educational session" request (V).
- Joondalup Private lost two private obstetricians to SJOG Subiaco in 2026 (search summary).

## Legal guardrails (V statute text in library, Part IIBA)
- No payment, gift, free service, staff, equipment, discount, revenue share or commission to anyone who requests tests. Penalty up to 6,000 penalty units for a company.
- Market-value room rent is allowed only for space used for nothing else under the arrangement.
- Never tie any benefit to volume. Never station collectors at a requester's premises.
- A clinic may say patients can choose any approved centre (wording for Catherine to confirm).
- Realistic: information sheet, patient-education talk at GTT's cost, neutral listing, lab-reviewed content.
- Not realistic: co-location in a clinic, co-branding or endorsement, preferred-provider deals.

## Appeal (V context only)
Clinics already sell privacy and care (Monash "patients come first"; Oasis "boutique feel and privacy"). No survey shows fertility patients want a luxury test. Do not claim it.
