Dear Delegate,

**Request for general guidance before any collection centre application: Specialised Maternal Wellness and Diagnostic Sanctuary, Perth**

I write personally, as a founder and private healthcare investor, before I commit capital. I am planning a Specialised Maternal Wellness and Diagnostic Sanctuary (project working title: GTT Center Perth) in Perth, Western Australia. No company has been formed and no premises are held. A company will be formed once the approval pathway is confirmed, and the site will be confirmed with the owner once that pathway is clear.

The service is for pregnant women only, for the oral glucose tolerance test (MBS item 66548). A fasting woman stays about two hours between three blood draws. The aim is to make that wait private, calm and comfortable, so more women complete gestational diabetes screening.

I am not a pathology provider and will not request pathology tests. The intended model:

- An approved pathology authority that owns an eligible laboratory holds its own approved collection centre in a dedicated room within the venue and does all collection and analysis.
- The venue operator runs the lounge and guest experience only. Any occupancy charge would be nil or a flat amount, never linked to the number or value of tests or requests.
- Appointments from 07:00, a new pair of clients every 25 minutes across two collection chairs, up to 18 clients a morning, six days a week. The last first draw is about 10:20 and final draws finish about 12:20.

I would be grateful for a written reply to these questions.

1. **Collection room inside a wellness venue.** What conditions apply under section 23DNBA of the Health Insurance Act 1973 and the Health Insurance (Approvals for Eligible Collection Centres) Principles 2020 to a collection room inside a maternal wellness venue run by someone other than the pathology authority? May the lounge where clients wait between draws be shown on the floor plan as a common area shared by the collection centre and the venue, and what occupancy document would you expect from the authority?

2. **Access and hours.** How is "reasonably accessible to the public" in section 23DNG(1)(ba) applied to an appointment-based morning service, open to anyone with a valid request and publicly listed? Would the morning hours above be "reasonable hours" under section 23DNG(1)(bb) for a centre built around a fasting test?

3. **Meaning of employee.** Sections 16A(5AA)(d) and 16A(5AB)(c) require the collector to be an employee of the approved pathology authority that owns the laboratory. May the venue operator recruit and nominate candidates, and set customer service standards by contract, while the authority remains sole employer and keeps full control of collection and clinical care, without affecting approval or Medicare benefits?

4. **Specialised laboratory.** Could a specialised (Category S) laboratory performing only the glucose tolerance test be considered, and could it then hold a collection centre under section 7(4) of the Principles 2020? Is there a pre-lodgement process for this route or for question 1?

I understand any guidance is general and that only a decision on a lodged application binds. Your view now would let me build to your standard from the start.

I will telephone Pathology Approvals on 1300 721 546 within seven days to arrange how you would like to reply.
