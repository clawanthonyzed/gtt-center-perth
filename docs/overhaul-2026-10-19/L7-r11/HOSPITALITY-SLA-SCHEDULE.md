# Schedule: Hospitality and Presentation Standards for lab collectors working in the lounge

Grace, 2026-10-20. DRAFT for Anthony. Not legal advice. Nothing sent. Not published (Publishing Shield).

**Working title.** "GTT Center Perth" is an unregistered working title. In the contract, use the registered entity name once it exists. This schedule says "GTT" and "the Lab".

**Sources.** docs/CURRENT-STATE.md (section 00), docs/strategy/BRAND-EXPERIENCE-FOUNDATION.md (tone, avoid list), docs/experience/CUSTOMER-JOURNEY.md, docs/gtt-clinical-protocol.md, docs/architecture/STAFF-PROFILES.md, L5-r9/catherine-r9-referral.md, L6-r10/ROUTE-1-PLAIN-ENGLISH.md. No new figures are introduced. Anything not in the repo says "not in repo".

**Legal status of every standard below.** Catherine's R11 file (/opt/openclaw/second-brain/knowledge/legal/r11/catherine-r11-lcc-sla.md) did not exist when this was written. Every row marked **CTC** = "Catherine to confirm" that it is lawful to require of another company's employees. Why it matters: Catherine R9 warns that if GTT holds strong control over lab collectors, GTT might count as a "provider" (Health Insurance Act s 23DZZIE(3)(d)), and a free-room arrangement could become a banned benefit (s 23DZZIF(7)(b)). So this schedule is written as **premises and service-style conditions the Lab agrees to**, never as GTT directing the collector's clinical work or employment.

## 0. Who does what (context)
Route 1: the Lab employs, trains, pays and disciplines collectors. GTT recruits and nominates, and runs the lounge, host, therapists and bookings. The Lab decides clinical matters, including the 2 hours of testing and the glucose drink timing (not in repo, the Lab sets it).

## 1. Client journey: where the collector appears

Tone (repo, section 15): calm, warm, reassuring, quietly luxurious. Plain English, active voice. Avoid: "patient", "reference number", "results", "provider", "bespoke", "journey", "self-care", "mummy/mumma", exclamation marks, forced warmth.

Wording below is the **standard to meet**. Collectors say it in their own voice but keep the meaning and the avoid list.

| # | Touchpoint | Standard | Wording to meet the tone | Who owns the fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Greeting at the chair | Stand, step forward, eye contact, smile. Use her first name from the host handover. No clipboard model at the chair | "Good morning, [first name]. I am [first name], I will look after your blood tests today." | GTT sets, Lab trains. CTC |
| 2 | Name use | First name only, unless she asks otherwise. Never "patient", "next", a number or "the pregnant lady". Say her name on handover to the host | "[First name], you are all set. [Host name] will take you to [therapist name]." | GTT sets. CTC |
| 3 | Identity checks | Required by the Lab's own clinical rules. Do them quietly, at the chair, never across a room | "Just so I have it right, could you tell me your full name and date of birth?" | Lab decides what is checked, GTT decides where and how loudly |
| 4 | Privacy | Draws happen in the closed collection room (layout is an architect matter, no floor plans here). Door closed during draws. Paperwork face down. No client details spoken in the lounge | "I will close the door so it is just us." | Joint. CTC |
| 5 | Explaining the draws | Say it once, plainly, at the first draw: draw now, one hour, two hours | "Three small draws today: now, at one hour and at two hours. Each takes about five minutes. Your card shows the times." (5-minute draw and the 60/120 marks are in the repo. The Lab confirms the clinical content.) | Lab clinical content, GTT wording. CTC |
| 6 | Acknowledging the needle | One calm line before it happens. Not over- or under-played | "You will feel a small pinch. Tell me anything you need and I will stop and wait." | GTT sets |
| 7 | Drink handling | Drink as supplied by the Lab (repo: commercially prepared, room temperature 20 minutes before). Hand it over in a plain glass or cup, never from a clinical tray. Say what it is and how long she has (repo protocol: 5 minutes, Lab confirms). Note the finish time. Never urge her to hurry | "This is the glucose drink. It is sweet and some people find it strong. Take it at a steady pace over the next five minutes." | Lab decides drink and timing, GTT decides presentation. CTC |
| 8 | If she feels unwell or cannot finish | Sit her, follow Lab clinical protocol, call the host quietly. If she vomits the drink, do not reattempt (repo protocol); tell the host | "Stay seated, there is no rush. I am going to ask [host name] to sit with you." | Lab clinical, host handles comfort |
| 9 | Comfort | Blanket, pillow and a warm room provided by GTT. Collector offers them without being asked | "Would you like a pillow or a blanket while we wait?" | GTT provides, Lab uses |
| 10 | Timing | Draws start on the scheduled mark. If late, tell the host first, then tell her plainly. Never leave her waiting unexplained | "I am running a few minutes behind. Thank you for your patience." | Joint. Clinical marks are Lab-owned |
| 11 | Handover | After each draw, hand her to the host by name with the next time. After the 2-hour draw, say how her doctor gets the results (the Lab states this) | "That is your last draw, [first name]. [Host name] will see you out. Your doctor will receive the results from [Lab]." | Joint |
| 12 | Goodbye | Thank her by name. No selling by the collector (the host raises the 10% post-test treatment offer once, briefly) | "Thank you for coming in, [first name]. Take care." | GTT sets |

| Signage | No hospital-style signage in the lounge: no "PATHOLOGY" banners, hazard stickers or posters. Any sign required by law or the Lab sits inside the collection room, discreet. Exterior is under-signed (repo) | Legal signs stay. The Lab decides what is mandatory. CTC |
| Equipment storage | Trolleys, trays, labels, tubes, printers and the specimen fridge stay inside the collection room, out of sight. Nothing clinical crosses the lounge in view. Specimens leave in a closed, unmarked carrier by the staff route | Needs architect and Lab spec. Not in repo: fridge spec, printer location |
| Sharps and waste | Sharps bin and clinical waste inside the collection room only, disposed of at the point of use. Collected by the Lab's contractor outside client hours or by the staff route. Never carried through the lounge | The Lab owns waste duty and cover (waste cover not in repo). GTT sets only where and when |
| Phones and food | Phones off at the chair. No eating or drinking in view of fasting clients | Joint. CTC |

## 8. Fertility-clinic client pathway: logistics note

For fertility clinics and IVF centres wanting to tell a client who has become pregnant about GTT. DRAFT. Anthony approves before anything goes to a clinic. Catherine to confirm wording against the inducement rules.

### 8.1 How an expecting mother books
| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1 | Her treating GP, obstetrician, midwife or nurse practitioner decides she needs the pregnancy glucose test and gives her a pathology request. Midwives and nurse practitioners can request item 66548 (Catherine R9). GTT does not advise on who should request |
| 2 | The request must be **open** (no lab named) unless the doctor names a lab on clinical grounds and writes the grounds. GTT accepts any valid request. Ideal wording on it: "OGTT pregnancy", so it maps to item 66548 |
| 3 | Timing: offered to pregnant women at 24 to 28 weeks (repo, ADIPS/FIGO standard). **Her doctor decides when she goes.** GTT follows the request and the Lab |
| 4 | She books online or by phone, 2 to 4 weeks ahead (repo). The booking page says: you need a doctor request, Medicare covers the test, you pay A$0 or A$3.80 depending on the Lab, treatments are extra. The Lab is not yet chosen |
| 5 | She receives fasting instructions at 48 and 24 hours before: 10 to 12 hours fasting, water only (repo, PathWest 2024 guidance, the Lab confirms). She brings the request and her Medicare card |
| 6 | On the morning she arrives before her time and is greeted by name |

### 8.2 What GTT staff may and may not say about choosing a centre
| May say | May not say |
|---|---|
| "You can take your request to any pathology provider. Choosing us is your choice." | "You should come here", "we are the best", "better than other labs" |
| What the morning involves, the timeline, the lounge, what is extra | Anything implying a doctor or clinic must send her to GTT |
| Fee facts as the Lab provides them | "Free" or "no cost", or promise bulk-billing before the Lab confirms |
| "Your doctor can tell you if there is a medical reason to use a particular lab." | Clinical advice, test interpretation, diet or diabetes advice |
| "I will check with the Lab." | A rebate figure beyond the Lab's own statement |
| Hand over a leaflet that carries the patient-choice statement | Pay, gift or reward any clinic or clinician for referrals, or give rooms, perks or discounts (inducement rules, Medical Board code 10.12.8) |

Rules for the fertility clinic relationship:
- GTT supplies information only. No fee, gift, perk or discount to a clinic or its staff.
- GTT does not employ, contract or direct any clinician (Catherine R9, s 23DZZIE).
- A clinician who rents space from GTT pays independently valued market rent, with no per-referral fee.
- Every client chooses freely, and the booking page says so.
- GTT does not ask a clinic for her treatment history. The test date comes from her request.

Not in repo: names of fertility clinics to approach, any agreed referral channel, and any clinic's wish to place its own lab. The directory lists Fertility North, Genea, IVFAustralia and PIVET as specialised labs that do not do this test.
