### About GTT Center Perth

GTT Center Perth is a concept currently in the planning/feasibility stage for Western Australia's first dedicated Glucose Tolerance Test (GTT) wellness venue, pairing clinical-grade blood collection with premium pregnancy wellness services during the mandatory 2–3 hour GTT wait.

*Note for whoever posts this ad: "GTT Center Perth" is currently a working/placeholder name — check `brand-guide.md` for current naming status before external use.*

---

### Role Overview

The Receptionist is the first and last point of contact for every client — opening the venue, greeting patients, coordinating the GTT booking flow with the phlebotomy team, managing the Fresha calendar, and handling end-of-visit checkout. This role reports to the Venue Manager.

---

### Key Responsibilities

- Open venue (7:15am): unlock, coffee/tea prep, lounge setup, check Fresha for the day's bookings
- Greet every patient by name on arrival; confirm fasting status, collect referral form and Medicare card
- Introduce patient to phlebotomist for T=0 collection immediately on arrival
- Manage Fresha calendar: same-day changes, waitlist notifications
- Answer phone and email enquiries
- Escort patients to treatment rooms for each service
- Process end-of-visit payments (Fresha checkout)
- Send post-visit SMS/email follow-up; collect Google review requests
- Close venue: tidy lounge, restock snacks, lock up, EOD Fresha report to the Venue Manager
