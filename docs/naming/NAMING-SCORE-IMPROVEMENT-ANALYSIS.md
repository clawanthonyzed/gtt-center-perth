# Naming Score Improvement Analysis

**Date:** 2026-08-16 | **Purpose:** Answers I3 directly — how could the existing SOLENA/ELOWEN/ELOWYN percentages improve, are there stronger already-investigated names, are there higher-scoring uninvestigated names. This evaluates the existing naming work (`docs/naming/NAMING-FINAL-COMPARISON.md`, `docs/naming/NAME-INVESTIGATION-REPORT.md`) — it does not start a new naming batch, propose new names, or reopen the shortlist.

---

## 1. How the Current Percentages (SOLENA 71.6% / ELOWEN 68.8% / ELOWYN 65.9%) Could Improve

Each name's score is currently held down by one specific, identifiable, real-world factor — not a vague "could be better." Here's what would move each number, and by roughly how much, based on the matrix's own weighting (`NAMING-FINAL-COMPARISON.md` §3):

**SOLENA — the biggest single lever in the whole comparison.** SOLENA scores its worst mark of the entire 17-criterion matrix on "Trademark position" (4/10, the second-highest-weighted criterion at weight 9) because of the live AU 2202790 Class 3 registration. If a trademark attorney's opinion (see `docs/naming/TRADEMARK-ATTORNEY-DECISION-BRIEF.md`) concluded that mark does **not** meaningfully obstruct Class 44 (services) use — a genuinely plausible outcome, since the classes are different and this business isn't operating in Class 3 — SOLENA's trademark-position score could reasonably move from 4 up toward 7-8 (roughly where ELOWEN sits, reflecting a real-but-lower-severity conflict rather than a disqualifying one). That single change would add approximately 3.6-4.5 weighted points (9 × 0.4-0.5) to SOLENA's raw total, pushing its percentage from 71.6% toward roughly **75-76%** — a meaningful, not marginal, move, and it doesn't require anything except the attorney question actually being answered.

**ELOWEN — a smaller, more bounded lever.** ELOWEN's own weakest score is also "Trademark position" (6/10) — the unregistered Elowen Counselling (Eaton, WA) conflict, plus the recent Merck KGaA device mark. Since this is a business-use/passing-off risk rather than a registered mark in a relevant class, an attorney opinion that concludes low practical risk could move this to 8-9, adding roughly 1.8-2.7 weighted points — moving ELOWEN from 68.8% toward roughly **70.5-71.5%**. A real improvement, but smaller than SOLENA's potential swing, because ELOWEN's conflict was already scored more favourably (6, not 4) going in.

**ELOWYN — capped, not improvable by new information.** ELOWYN's weak point (referral clarity 3/10, spelling ease 4/10, combined weight 14 — the two lowest scores in its whole row) is a **structural property of the spelling itself**, confirmed independently by two separate design passes. No attorney opinion, no further research, and no branding treatment changes how many times "E-L-O-W-Y-N" needs to be spelled out on a phone call. This is the one score in the whole matrix that cannot realistically improve without changing the word itself — which would mean abandoning ELOWYN as a candidate, not improving it.

**Bottom line: SOLENA has the most upside available, tied directly to the one open question already identified (Class 44 registrability) — this is exactly why the trademark attorney question (`TRADEMARK-ATTORNEY-DECISION-BRIEF.md`) is the single highest-leverage naming action available, more so than any further comparison work.**

## 2. Do Stronger Already-Investigated Names Exist?

**Yes — one, and it's already in the repository's own research, not a new finding.** `docs/naming/NAME-INVESTIGATION-REPORT.md` investigated seven candidates in total (ELOWEN, SOLENNE, SOLENA, ELAVEN, ELOWYN, ELAWEN, ELOVEN), not just the three finalists. **SOLENNE has the highest raw creative/brand-fit score of all seven (8.5/10, vs. 8.3-8.4 for ELOWEN/SOLENA/ELOWYN)** — per that report's own §12 scoring table.

**Why it isn't a finalist, and why that's a legitimate exclusion, not an oversight:** SOLENNE collides with a real, active Australian business — **Solenne Pty Ltd (Adelaide, SA)**, a "lifestyle medicine" wellness clinic that already holds both the `.com.au` and `.au` domains and trades in genuinely adjacent territory (women's health, diabetes, wellness) — closer positioning overlap than any of the three current finalists' own conflicts. `NAME-INVESTIGATION-REPORT.md` §7 flags this explicitly as "close enough in positioning to GTT's own women's-wellness-plus-health framing to warrant genuine caution."

**Verdict: SOLENNE is a real, stronger-scoring name on brand fit alone, correctly excluded from the finalist shortlist for a substantive commercial-conflict reason, not overlooked or under-researched.** It remains recorded in the existing report as "a secondary reference to SOLENA, not a primary pick" — worth knowing about if SOLENA's own Class 3 question resolves unfavourably and a genuine alternative in the same naming territory is needed, but not a reason to reopen the current three-way shortlist today.

The other three non-finalist candidates (ELAVEN, ELAWEN, ELOVEN) all score meaningfully lower (5.8-6.5/10) — ELAVEN and ELAWEN read as "constructed vowel-swaps that lost the specific quality... the founder actually responded to" (per the source report's own words), and ELOVEN carries a genuine, disqualifying conflict with an existing Australian hair-care brand (Eleven Australia) directly adjacent to one of this business's own planned services. None of these three represents an overlooked stronger option.

## 3. Do Higher-Scoring Uninvestigated Names Exist?

**Not evaluable, and not something this analysis attempts to answer by generating new names.** Per the explicit instruction governing this analysis (evaluate existing work, do not start a new naming batch), no new candidate names were generated or searched for this document. `NAME-INVESTIGATION-REPORT.md` itself addresses this exact question directly in its own §18: *"Is there evidentiary basis for generating a new naming territory? No. Two of the seven frozen candidates are commercially clean with excellent brand fit."* That finding stands — nothing in this pass contradicts it or reopens the question.

## 4. Summary Answer

- The current percentages are not fixed facts — SOLENA's could realistically move to ~75-76% if the trademark attorney question resolves favourably; ELOWEN's could move to ~70.5-71.5% similarly. ELOWYN's is structurally capped by its spelling/referral cost, not by unresolved information.
- Yes, a stronger-scoring already-investigated name exists (SOLENNE, 8.5/10 raw creative score) — correctly excluded for a real Adelaide business conflict, kept on record as a secondary reference, not a current finalist.
- No new, uninvestigated names were generated or evaluated here, consistent with the existing report's own finding that there's no evidentiary basis to reopen the naming search.
