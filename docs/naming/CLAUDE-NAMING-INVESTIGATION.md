- feminine, soft, elegant, refined, modern, warm, aspirational

## Business context
GTT Center Perth is a pre-launch premium pregnancy Glucose Tolerance Test experience. The concept combines on-site pathology collection with premium wellness/beauty services during the GTT waiting period, with a standalone PM wellness venue serving women beyond GTT patients. Use the repository's current README and business-plan documentation as the source of truth for business context.

## Research objective
Determine which shortlisted names are realistically viable for an Australian launch, prioritising Perth/WA and Australia. This is pre-clearance research, not a legal opinion and not yet a trademark filing.

## Investigate every candidate
### 1. Australian trade marks
- IP Australia Australian Trade Mark Search (ATMS)
- exact matches
- plural/spacing/spelling variations
- phonetic/sound-alike variants
- visually similar or confusingly similar marks where relevant
- live registrations
- pending applications
- expired/cancelled marks where strategically relevant
- owner
- status
- filing/priority dates
- relevant goods/services classes

### 2. Relevant classes
Determine likely classes from the actual GTT business model rather than assuming a generic beauty class. Consider healthcare, beauty/personal care, wellness, education/events, retail and any other services genuinely contemplated by the business plan. Explain why each class matters.

Do NOT eliminate a name merely because an unrelated registration exists in a different class. Conversely, do NOT assume a different class makes a name automatically safe: assess commercial similarity and potential confusion.

### 3. Australian business use
Search ASIC/ABN and web presence for:
- exact names
- close variants
- Perth/WA businesses
- Australia-wide businesses

Prioritise collisions in:
- women's health
- pregnancy/maternity
- fertility
- healthcare
- beauty
- skincare
- massage
- hair
- nails
- aesthetics
- wellness
- allied health
- medical/spa experiences

### 4. Digital identity
Check, where possible:
- .com.au
- .au
- .com
- Instagram
- Facebook
- TikTok
- meaningful existing Google/search presence

Record whether the issue is ownership, active use, dormant use, or merely a search result.

### 5. Competitive/brand collision
Assess whether a reasonable Australian customer could confuse GTT Center Perth with an existing brand. Separate:
- legal/trademark risk
- commercial/brand collision
- domain/social inconvenience
- SEO/search collision
- strategic brand-fit issue

Do not treat every unrelated use as a blocker.

### 6. Linguistic/practical test
For each name assess:
- likely Australian pronunciation
- spelling-on-first-hearing
- likelihood customers ask how to spell it
- whether it can be said naturally in phrases such as "I'm going to Elowen" or "booked at Elowen"
- whether the variant creates ambiguity

### 7. Strategic brand fit
Score each against the founder's stated desired character. Elowen is the benchmark, not an automatic winner.
