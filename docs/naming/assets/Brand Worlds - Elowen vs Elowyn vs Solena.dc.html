Brand strategy + identity exploration · Phase 1

Three brand worlds
for one premium house

Working name: GTT Center Perth
Finalists: Elowen · Elowyn · Solena
Purpose: make the name choice obvious
The strategic problem

The business has a pathology engine and a beauty soul. Every competitor in the category has branded the engine — GTT, lounge, clinic, test. That is the whole opportunity: the house that gets named after the engine can never grow past it.

So the master brand is built as a premium women's wellness house first, and the GTT is expressed as one of its experiences — a named morning, not a category. The morning is the wedge. The house is the asset.

What all three share
A wordmark-led identity, not a symbol-led one.
Warm neutral grounds, one committed accent.
Editorial layout: generous margins, small type, few words.
Photography of light, hands, materials and faces — never bellies, never equipment.
Service names that could belong to a beauty house, not a pathology form.
How the three differ
ELOWEN
Considered. A quiet house of expertise — Cornish-English restraint, gallery calm, tactile.
ELOWYN
Maison. Sharp fashion typography, cool composure, the Y as the brand's signature.
SOLENA
Sunlit. Mediterranean warmth, plaster and clay, the most product-and-retail-ready of the three.

ELOWEN

EL-oh-wen
The considered house
Interpretation

A real name with a real history, so the brand inherits credibility instead of asking for it. Elowen sounds like somewhere that has always been there — an independent house, not a launch.

The elm etymology stays buried. What we take from the word is its evenness: three soft syllables, no hard edges, nothing decorative. That evenness becomes the design principle — restraint as the luxury signal.

Both sides of the business

Morning: she reads "a house for the hours you didn't choose" and understands instantly that the brand is on her side of the appointment.

Afternoon: nothing in the identity references pregnancy, so a 29-year-old booking brows sees a wellness house. Elowen's risk is the opposite one — it is so quiet it needs strong photography to feel desirable rather than merely tasteful.

Scalability

Scales as a house of addresses — Elowen Subiaco, Elowen Claremont — which is the most credible multi-venue architecture of the three. Membership reads naturally (The Elowen Year). Retail is the weak axis: "Elowen" on a serum bottle is plausible but not compelling; it wants to sell towels, robes and candles more than skincare.
02

ELOWYN

EL-oh-win
The maison
Interpretation

The Y changes everything. It makes the word constructed rather than inherited — and a constructed word is a fashion word. Elowyn doesn't want to be an old English house; it wants to be a maison founded last decade with couture discipline.

So we design it cooler and sharper than Elowen: high-contrast didone, near-black graphite, plum ink, one blush. The Y descender becomes the only piece of ornament the brand ever uses.

Both sides of the business

What limits it is spoken word-of-mouth, which is the primary acquisition channel for this business: a name that must be spelled out loses referral velocity, and referral velocity is the whole growth model.
03

SOLENA

soh-LEE-na
Sunlit
Interpretation

Sol is right there at the front of the word, and it does the work of a whole strategy: warmth, light, the Mediterranean afternoon. Solena is the only one of the three that sounds like it has weather.

That makes it the most immediately likeable and the most obviously beauty-industry — which is both its strength and its exposure. It is warm enough to carry an anxious morning, and commercial enough to put on a bottle. It is also the most familiar-sounding, so distinctiveness must be manufactured by the design rather than supplied by the name.

The recommendation

As lead designer
Build ELOWEN. Keep SOLENA as the product brand it wants to be. Drop ELOWYN.

This agrees with your instinct, and I want to be clear about why, because agreeing with the founder is the easiest mistake in this exercise.

Elowen wins on the one dimension the business cannot buy later: it sounds like a place that already exists. Everything about this venture is asking a woman to trust an unfamiliar operator with a real medical appointment. A name with lineage does quiet credibility work at the exact moment she is deciding whether this is legitimate or gimmicky. Solena, by contrast, sounds like a brand — attractive, but a brand that had to be created. In a category where two skin spas already carry the word, "sounds like a beauty brand" is closer to a liability than an asset.

Elowen also survives the harder of the two audience tests. The morning customer is the anxious one, and Elowen's restraint reads as competence. The afternoon customer meets a house with taste and no maternity residue whatsoever. And it scales the way this business will actually scale — by address, not by product line.

The honest cost: Elowen is the hardest of the three to execute. Quiet luxury with a weak photography budget is just plain. If you choose it, the photography and the physical materials are not the last line item — they are the identity.

ELOWEN

For: an inherited name with genuine lineage; the only candidate that already sounds like an established house; strongest at the anxious morning; scales by address.

Against: an exact-name WA business and the occupied .com.au mean permanent search-sharing; and the aesthetic is unforgiving of a cheap fit-out.

ELOWYN

For: the cleanest research picture, the sharpest typography, an ownable monogram, and the best afternoon-only brand of the three.

Against: it must be spelled out forever in a referral-driven business, and the -wyn spelling reads as a modern baby name rather than a European house. It buys legal comfort with brand equity.

SOLENA

For: warmest, easiest to say, best venue, best packaging runway, and the strongest single line of copy in this document — "The Long Morning".

Against: two existing Australian skin-and-beauty businesses and a Class 3 cosmetics mark sit in precisely the territory it is best at, and the word itself is the least distinctive of the three.

If you overrule me

Choose Solena, not Elowyn. There is a real strategic case for it: if you expect the afternoon business and a future product line to out-earn the GTT mornings within five years, warmth and retail-readiness matter more than inherited credibility, and Solena wins on both. Elowyn is the only one of the three I would argue against outright.

What this phase deliberately hasn't done

No final logo, palette or type system is locked — the wordmarks here are treatments, not artwork, and the fonts are stand-ins chosen to demonstrate a register. Prices are illustrative. Pick the name and the next phase builds the full identity properly from this direction.
