## Legal Wording Note (Read Before Editing This Ad)

Anthony wants this ad to appeal to return-to-work mums and people wanting flexible hours. **The ad must not say "mums," "mums only," "mothers," or similarly target parental/family status directly** — under the Fair Work Act and Australian anti-discrimination law (sex/family responsibilities are protected attributes), an ad that explicitly targets or excludes based on parental status risks indirect discrimination exposure, even where the intent is inclusive rather than exclusive. **Correct approach used throughout this ad:** describe the *conditions* (flexible hours, predictable rotating roster, part-time-friendly) rather than the *candidate's personal/family status*. This reaches the same audience (people balancing caregiving or other commitments who want predictable, flexible hours) without naming a protected characteristic. Do not water this down when editing — if in doubt, describe the job, not the jobholder.

### About GTT Center Perth

GTT Center Perth is a concept currently in the planning/feasibility stage for Western Australia's first dedicated Glucose Tolerance Test (GTT) wellness venue. In the mornings, our service staff support pregnant clients during their mandatory GTT wait; in the afternoons, the same combined team delivers standalone massage, hair, nail, and beauty services to the wider community.

*Note for whoever posts this ad: "GTT Center Perth" is currently a working/placeholder name — check `brand-guide.md` for current naming status before external use.*

---

### Role Overview

You deliver your specialty service — massage, hair, nail, or beauty — to whichever client population your rostered shift serves that week: GTT clients during their morning wait, or standalone walk-in/booked clients in the afternoon. **You're rostered into one shift block per week (AM or PM), rotating over time** — you always know your pattern in advance, this isn't day-to-day on-call work.

**We actively prefer candidates cross-qualified across 2 or more specialties** (e.g. Nail Technology + Hairdressing, or Massage Therapy + Beauty Therapy) — this broadens the service range you can offer and gives you and the venue more rostering flexibility, including covering colleagues' leave. Single-specialty candidates are very welcome too — multi-qualification is a genuine plus, not a requirement.

**Ideal for flexible working arrangements.** Our rotating weekly roster suits people looking for predictable, part-time-friendly hours — including those returning to the workforce after a break or balancing work with other commitments. We're happy to discuss hours, days, and roster preferences at interview.

---

### Key Responsibilities (by line — applies whichever shift you're rostered into that week)

**Massage Therapist** — Deliver 60-min massage treatments (pregnancy-safe technique for AM/GTT clients; general therapeutic for PM standalone clients); set up/pack down treatment room; maintain client intake and consent records.

**Hairdresser** — Full hairdressing menu (cut, colour, style); maintain station hygiene and product inventory.

**Nail Technician** — Manicure and pedicure services (45–60 min); maintain nail station ventilation compliance and pregnancy-safe product inventory for AM clients; sterilise/sanitise tools to AS 4187 standards.

**Beauty Therapist / Brows** — Brow shaping, waxing, tinting, facials; PPD-free tint products where servicing pregnant (AM) clients.

**Shared (all lines, both shift blocks)** — Process bookings/checkout via Fresha; participate in monthly staff meeting; flag any client-facing concerns to the Venue Manager.

### Essential Requirements

| Line | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Massage | Cert IV Massage Therapy (HLT52015) minimum; pregnancy massage specialisation a plus |
| Hairdresser | Cert III Hairdressing (SHB30416) minimum, current registration |
| Nail Technician | Cert III Nail Technology (SHB30115) |
| Beauty Therapist | Cert III Beauty Therapy (SHB30115), waxing/brow proficiency |
| **Preferred (all lines)** | Qualification in a 2nd complementary specialty — Massage+Beauty or Hair+Nail |
| All lines | Genuine availability across the rotating weekly AM/PM roster; current First Aid (HLTAID011) preferred; National Police Clearance + Working with Vulnerable People check (WA); own professional kit preferred |

- A role that can grow with your qualifications — the more service lines you're qualified in, the more rostering flexibility available to you
- Clear path to part-time conversion for lines/hours proving out regular demand
