### About GTT Center Perth

GTT Center Perth is a concept currently in the planning/feasibility stage for Western Australia's first dedicated Glucose Tolerance Test (GTT) wellness venue. In the mornings we host pregnant women during their mandatory GTT wait; in the afternoons, these 4 PM roles open our treatment rooms to walk-in/booked clients from the wider community — standalone massage, hair, nail, and beauty services, not linked to any clinical appointment.

*Note for whoever posts this ad: "GTT Center Perth" is currently a working/placeholder name — check `brand-guide.md` for current naming status before external use.*

You will work in a small, purpose-driven team, cross-shift qualified with our AM treatment staff, all directly employed by GTT Center Perth — not subtenants or room-renters.

---

### Role Overview (applies to all 4 lines)

Each PM Casual Treatment Practitioner delivers standalone individual services (not packages) in their specialty — massage, hair, nail, or beauty — during the 12:00pm–6:00pm PM shift. You are not tied to the GTT clinical operation; PM clients are drawn from the general community and GTT clients who choose to extend their visit. Cross-training with the AM team on the same service line is encouraged and may open relief/AM shift opportunities over time (see `staff-plan.md` §7B Relief/Backup Staffing).

---

### Key Responsibilities (by line)

**Massage Therapist (PM)**
- Deliver 60-min massage treatments (pregnancy-safe technique where the client is pregnant; general therapeutic massage otherwise)
- Set up/pack down treatment room before/after each client
- Maintain client intake and consent records

**Hairdresser (PM)**
- Full hairdressing menu (cut, colour, style) — positions the venue above a standard day spa
- Maintain station hygiene and product inventory

**Nail Technician (PM)**
- Manicure and pedicure services (45–60 min)
- Maintain nail station ventilation compliance (WorkSafe WA local exhaust ventilation) and pregnancy-safe product inventory where relevant
- Sterilise/sanitise tools to AS 4187 standards

**Beauty Therapist / Brows (PM)**
- Brow shaping, waxing, tinting, facials
- PPD-free tint products where servicing pregnant clients (patch test knowledge)

**Shared (all 4 lines)**
- Process bookings/checkout via Fresha
- Participate in monthly staff meeting
- Flag any client-facing concerns to the Venue Manager

### Essential Requirements

| Line | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Massage | Cert IV Massage Therapy (HLT52015) minimum; pregnancy massage specialisation preferred given client mix |
| Hairdresser | Cert III Hairdressing (SHB30416) minimum, current registration |
| Nail Technician | Cert III Nail Technology (SHB30115) |
| Beauty Therapist | Cert III Beauty Therapy (SHB30115), waxing/brow proficiency |
| All lines | Current First Aid (HLTAID011) preferred; National Police Clearance + Working with Vulnerable People check (WA) — required given client population includes pregnant women; own professional kit preferred |
