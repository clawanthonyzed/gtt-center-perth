## 1. Context in five lines

- GTT Center Perth is a wellness venue for pregnant women having the glucose tolerance test, with employed beauty and wellness staff and a pathology partner collecting blood. It has not yet opened.
- Existing entities: YETI Holding Trust (discretionary; holds 24/40 Reynolds Way, Withers, and long-term shares and assets; corporate trustee YETI Tipi Holdings PTY LTD); YETI Operational Trust (exists; trustee, deed and beneficiaries not seen); Cudan Studio Pty Ltd (standalone, not in either trust, may close).
- Anthony receives the Special Rate disability pension and holds a Gold Card. Our reading of the legislation: no income test; an 8-hour work test applies (10 hours if the payment is an MRCA SRDP). He must not work in the business beyond a small logged amount.
- Anthony's partner holds no role in GTT or in any entity.
- Recommended structure (for you to confirm or change): a new Pty Ltd trading entity, shares held by a trust with a different corporate trustee from the Holding Trust, independent director, employed Venue Manager, no guarantees from the Holding Trust, YETI Tipi Holdings or Anthony.

## 2. Documents to give you

Both trust deeds and any amendments; trustee company constitutions and ASIC extracts for each corporate trustee; trust tax returns and financial statements for the last two years; the register of appointors or guardians; the Cudan Studio Pty Ltd lease and any guarantee given; any insurance policies now in force; this folder's three analyses.

## 6. Items that depend on DVA

1. Whether Anthony's pension is paid under VEA s24 or as an MRCA SRDP. 2. Whether a nominal directorship counts as work. 3. Any notification duty. 4. What happens to the rate and Gold Card if the work test were failed.

## 7. Timing

Settle structure before: the lease is signed, the pathology agreement is signed, staff are hired, insurance is bound and the first booking is taken, so nothing needs to be assigned or restarted. Structural advice is on the critical path ahead of any signing.
