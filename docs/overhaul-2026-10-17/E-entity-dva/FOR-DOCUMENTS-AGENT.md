## 2. Business plan: paragraph for the "Legal structure" section

GTT Center Perth will operate through a purpose-built Australian proprietary company. The company will hold the premises lease, the pathology partner agreement, employment contracts, insurance policies, GST and payroll registrations, and the business bank account. Its shares will be held by a family trust whose trustee is a separate corporate entity from the trustee of the family's property trust, so that a claim against the venture cannot reach the family's property. The company will be managed by an employed Venue Manager and overseen by an independent director. The founder will take no operational role. The company will not rely on any other business of the founder or his associates. The structure will be confirmed by the company's accountant and solicitor before any lease or contract is signed.
