## 1. Domains

### How it was checked
- .com.au and .au: direct query to the official auDA WHOIS service (whois.auda.org.au, port 43) for each name. "Domain not found" means not registered today. The auDA website was not needed (the earlier work hit a website rate limit; the direct service did not).
- .com, .net, .org, .health: RDAP (the registry lookup standard) via rdap.org and the registry's own RDAP server from the IANA list. A 404 means not registered. The earlier script /tmp/rdap_check.py was reused for a first pass and extended.
- .co: not reliably checked (its registry is not on the standard RDAP list and the direct WHOIS host did not resolve from the server). Treat .co as TO CONFIRM by a registrar search.
- What is on each taken domain: the home page was fetched from the server with a normal browser header.

### The .au point
ELOWEN.au, BELMIRA.au and BELMIRA.com.au are unregistered today. The auDA priority period for .au (held for existing .com.au holders) ran 24 March to 20 September 2022 and has closed; unclaimed names were released to the public on 3 October 2022 (source: auDA .au direct pages quoted in search results, not opened in full; TO CONFIRM on auda.org.au before relying). Whether we can register a .com.au or .au depends on auDA's "close and substantial connection" rule, which normally needs a registered business name or trade mark connected to the name. TO CONFIRM with a registrar. Nothing was registered.

## 4. ASIC (companies and business names)
- ASIC Connect (connectonline.asic.gov.au) is a scripted form. The page loaded, but the automated search returned "Please enter name or number" even after typing the name, so no ASIC search results were retrieved by machine. This is a stated gap, not a clean result.
- The ASIC and ATO maintenance notice on register.business.gov.au reads: from 8:00 pm Friday 16 October to 11:59 pm Saturday 17 October 2026 AEDT, "retrieving search results for company and business name registrations may be affected." Today falls inside that window, so an ASIC search today may be unreliable even by hand.
- What is verified instead: ABN Lookup lists company names and registered business names (the register above). It is a partial cover of the ASIC business names register, but it does not replace an ASIC name check; how complete it is for business names is TO CONFIRM.
- Manual steps for Anthony or the attorney (about 5 minutes per name, after 11:59 pm Saturday 17 October AEDT): go to https://connectonline.asic.gov.au/RegistrySearch/faces/landing/SearchRegisters.jspx , choose "Organisation and Business Names", type the name; then run "Check Name Availability", which reports whether a name is identical or too similar to an existing company or business name. Do this for ELOWEN, ELOWYN and the leading candidate. No ASIC result is claimed in this paper.
