# Trade mark evidence: ELOWEN, ELOWYN, SOLENA, BELMIRA (IP Australia)

Searched live on 2026-10-17 (AWST). Nothing was filed, registered or reserved. This is NOT a legal trade mark clearance. A registered trade marks attorney must run the formal search and give an opinion before any money is spent on signage, fit-out, domains or filing.

## How it was searched
- Register: Australian Trade Mark Search, https://search.ipaustralia.gov.au/trademarks . The page is script-driven, so it was read through the stealth browser on the server (the quick-search result pages returned readable results; no block).
- Method: quick search on the word and on spelling variants, plus wildcard searches (the register accepts * in the quick search). Goods and services were read from each mark's own record page for every mark that matters.
- What this search cannot do: it matches letters, not sounds. IP Australia's own examiners and any opponent also compare how marks sound and what they mean, and compare the goods and services (a mark in Class 20 can still conflict with a service in Class 44 if the goods and services are related). The advanced search on the site has additional options (such as sounds-like) that could not be driven automatically. TO CONFIRM by the attorney.
- Classes of interest: 44 (medical, health, beauty and wellness services, includes massage, hairdressing, beauty salon, spa), 3 (cosmetics), 5 (health products), 35 (retail), 41 (education and wellness classes), 42 (science and research), 45 (personal services). None of these classes is a gap for us; the table covers all hits whatever the class.

## ELOWYN: every hit found

Searches run: elowyn, elowyn*, *lowyn, *elowyn*, ellowyn, elowynn, elowy*, eloyn.

| Result | Detail |
|---|---|
| Direct hits for ELOWYN in any class | 0 |
| ELOWYN with any suffix or prefix | 0 |
| Spelling variants tried (Ellowyn, Elowynn, Eloyn) | 0 |

Reading: the register has no ELOWYN mark in any class. This reproduces the August 2026 finding. But two things reduce the value of that clean result:
1. ELOWYN sounds identical to ELOWEN. Anyone comparing by ear (the usual test) would treat the ELOWEN marks above as the same sound. So the ELOWEN marks are a risk for ELOWYN too, to the same degree.
2. The name ELOWYN is in live commercial use by ELOWYN PTY LTD (accounting, QLD) and by Elowyn Health (a US telehealth wellness business with the elowyn.health domain). Under Australian law a business can have rights from use even without a registered mark; whether that matters across different industries is a question for the attorney.

## What was NOT done and how to finish
- No phonetic ("sounds like") register search, no design search, no search of pending applications by owner name beyond what the quick search returned. Manual step: the attorney runs these on the full register and on WIPO Global Brand Database for international registrations that could extend to Australia.
- No search of unregistered ("common law") use beyond ABN Lookup and a light web search. That is how Elowen Counselling (WA) would be found, and it is the most relevant exact-name user for ELOWEN.
- Overseas: the search tool used does not show UK, US or EU registers. See BRAND-COMPARISON.md section on overseas use; trade mark registers abroad were not searched.
