## What is included

1. Division 38 s 38-1 (what the Division is about).
2. Subdivision 38-B (Health) in full: ss 38-7 (medical services), 38-10 (other health services, with the full table of 21 items), 38-15, 38-20, 38-25, 38-30, 38-35, 38-38, 38-40, 38-45, 38-47, 38-50, 38-55, 38-60.
3. Definitions relied on, from s 195-1: "medical practitioner", "medical service", "approved pathology practitioner", "hospital treatment", "professional service", "recognised professional".
4. GST Regulations 2019, reg 38-7.01 (prescribed provision for s 38-7(2)(a)).
5. Health Insurance Act 1973: s 3(1) definitions of "pathology service", "pathology services table", "professional service" (opening paragraphs), "approved collection centre"; and s 16A in full (Medicare benefits for pathology services), because it controls who may collect a specimen when a Medicare benefit is claimed.
6. Context sections used by the split analysis: GST Act ss 9-5, 9-15, 9-30, 9-80, 29-70 (tax invoices) and Division 165 (anti-avoidance) ss 165-1 to 165-15, 165-40 to 165-55.

## What could not be fetched or is not included

- The legislation.gov.au API `documents(...)/$value` endpoint returned HTTP 404 for the PDF volumes; the site's own download links (above) worked and were used instead.
- Not included: the Federal Register explanatory material for the 1999 enactment, and case law on "recognised professional" or "nursing". No text for "nursing" is defined anywhere in the Act: the word appears only as item 10 of the s 38-10(1) table (a search of both volumes of the compilation found no other occurrence), so what counts as a nursing service is decided by ordinary meaning, the "recognised professional" definition and ATO guidance.
- The Health Insurance (General Medical Services Table) Regulations 2021 clause 1.2.9 referred to by reg 38-7.01 was not fetched.
- Subdivision 38-B items not relevant to GTT (aged care, disability, drugs) are included for completeness but not analysed.

## Division 38 s 38-1
Division 38 GST-free supplies
Section 38-1
38-1 What this Division is about
                This Division sets out the supplies that are GST-free. If a supply is
                GST-free, then:
                •    no GST is payable on the supply;
                •    an entitlement to an input tax credit for anything acquired or
                     imported to make the supply is not affected.
                For the basic rules about supplies that are GST-free, see sections 9-30 and 9-80.
