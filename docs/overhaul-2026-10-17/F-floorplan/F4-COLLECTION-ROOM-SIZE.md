## Decision

The repo gives a range of 18 to 20 sqm per room (the 9 sqm and 10 to 12 sqm figures are partner or guideline floors, not the target). Rule applied: range, so pick the middle. **Both collection rooms are standardised at 19.0 sqm (4.75 m x 4.0 m).**

- This replaces the v1 drawing of 17.6 sqm (below the 18 target) and 19.4 sqm.
- 19.0 sqm sits inside the repo range, well above the partner's floor (9 to 10 sqm) and the AusHFG enclosed room (12 sqm), and it holds Chair A or B, stool, recovery recliner (about 1.6 sqm), bench with under-counter fridge and documentation space, handwash basin and a clear wheelchair approach.
- Flagged, not resolved: the repo says the recliner allowance of about 2 to 3 sqm is "not yet folded into" the 18 sqm. Reading 18 plus 2 to 3 would give 20 to 21 sqm. At the 19.0 sqm drawn, the recliner is inside the room. Pathology partner confirmation: TO CONFIRM. If 20 sqm is demanded, the plan grows by 2.0 sqm in total (288.6 to 290.6 sqm), just above the top of the 257 to 290 sqm search range, so the architect would trim circulation to stay inside it.
