## Slide 18: Floor Plan and Design

**Title:** Floor Plan and Design

**Image (relative path from outputs/PRESENTATION/):** `floor-plan-concept.png` (vector version `floor-plan-concept.svg`; identical copies `floor-plan-v2.png` and `floor-plan-v2.svg`). A plan-only crop for tight slides is `floor-plan-concept-plan.png`. The full interactive page is `floor-plan-v2.html`.

**Caption under the image (small):** Concept layout, 18.5 m x 15.6 m (288.6 sqm). Concept only, not for construction; subject to site survey, architect and NCC compliance.

**Bullets (6):**

1. Two equal blood collection rooms of 19 sqm, each with its own specimen fridge, handwash basin, recovery recliner and documentation bench (or one large room divided by a clinical privacy curtain).
2. Every room opens onto a corridor or the lounge. Staff reach the staff room without passing the service and linen area.
3. A separate service door and staff-only corridor carry clinical waste and soiled linen away without crossing any client area.
4. Hair and nail areas sized at 5 to 6 sqm per station, giving a larger open luxury lounge beside the Private Refreshment Counter, which is kept apart from specimens and clinical waste.
5. Massage and beauty bays with partition walls, privacy curtains and a handwash basin in every bay; one accessible and one standard toilet shared by clients and staff.
6. Used to brief the architect, obtain fit-out quotes and source furniture and fixtures, including direct from China where compliant.

**Optional speaker note:** The plan is a concept for quoting and sourcing. Final layout, toilet count, building approval and compliance are confirmed by an architect and building surveyor once the site is chosen. The collection room size follows the pathology partner range of 18 to 20 sqm and is subject to the partner's confirmation.

**Do not state** drawn room areas as final. If you quote an area, use "about 289 sqm concept shell" and "collection rooms of about 19 sqm".

## Card C3 (plain English)

**Title:** 📐 Concept floor plan, version 2, and China supplier sourcing strategy

**Text:**

📐 **Floor plan version 2 is ready.** It fixes the problems found in version 1. One clean drawing at 18.5 m x 15.6 m (about 289 sqm, inside the 257 to 290 sqm range we are searching for):

- 🚪 **Every room can be reached.** Each room opens onto a corridor or the lounge, and no room is reached only by walking through another.
- 🩸 **Two equal blood collection rooms**, 19 sqm each (the middle of the 18 to 20 sqm range), each with its own specimen fridge under the bench, a handwash basin, a recovery recliner and a documentation bench. The separate fridge room is gone. The one large room with a curtain (Option B) is shown as an inset.
- 💇 **Hair and nails cut back to realistic sizes** (about 24 and 22 sqm, 5 to 6 sqm per station). The space went mostly to the open luxury lounge, now about 45 sqm.
- 🧑‍⚕️ **Staff no longer walk through the service and linen area** to reach the staff room. The staff room has its own entry and doors to both corridors. Clinical waste leaves by a separate service door along a staff-only corridor.
- 🚽 **Staff toilet removed.** One accessible and one standard toilet are shared by clients and staff.
- 🚫 Clinical waste and specimens stay well apart from the refreshment counter, as decided.

📋 **Shopping list by room** (FF&E schedule) updated: two specimen fridges and two recovery recliners (one in each room), no fridge room, no staff toilet. No budget total changed.

🔎 **How it was checked:** a computer check of the plan data confirms every room is reachable from the main entry, that the staff room and the lounge reach the collection rooms without crossing the dirty zone, and that both collection rooms are exactly the same size. The sizes follow the venue room schedule, the pathology partner brief and public hospital planning guidelines.

🇨🇳 **China sourcing strategy:** unchanged. What is sensible to import direct (lounge furniture, decor, partitions, curtains, cabinets, signage, massage and beauty beds, pedicure chairs), and what must be bought in Australia or proven first: blood-collection chairs (therapeutic goods register), electrical items (RCM mark), taps and basins (WaterMark), wall and ceiling materials (fire rules).

⚠️ **Things to know**
- It is a concept only. An architect and building surveyor must confirm it for the real site.
- The pathology partner must confirm the 19 sqm collection room size. If it asks for 20 sqm or more, the plan grows by about 2 sqm.
- The toilet count (one accessible, one standard, shared) needs the architect and building surveyor to confirm under the National Construction Code.
- A second specimen fridge, second recovery recliner and possibly a second centrifuge are quantity changes that are not yet priced in the financial model.
- Nobody has been contacted. Nothing ordered or paid.

👉 **Needs your decision:** approve the "sensible to import" list, and choose how to buy (our recommendation: an Australian-based sourcing agent for the China items, local trades for everything regulated).
