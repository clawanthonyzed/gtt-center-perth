## A. Operating rule: phlebotomists on the adaptive roster

Replacement for the adaptive rostering sentence (previously "one booking runs two phlebotomists and two therapists"):

"Staff are called in to match the confirmed bookings, morning and afternoon, phlebotomists included. GTT never rosters idle staff. A morning with one booking runs one phlebotomist and two therapists, not a full team. Up to nine bookings run one phlebotomist and four therapists. From the tenth booking both collection chairs run, with two phlebotomists. There is no phlebotomist in the afternoon. On lighter mornings a named second collector is on call for a difficult draw or a faint."

Roster logic card (replaces the earlier card):

"Staffing follows overlap, not headcount. Each client has three timed blood draws and two 45-minute wellness services, and pairs start 25 minutes apart. One chair carries up to nine clients because no two draws ever fall together, so one phlebotomist is rostered. From the tenth booking two clients share a time slot, two draws fall together and the second chair opens. One client: 1 phlebotomist and 2 therapists. Two to nine clients, one per slot: 1 phlebotomist and 4 therapists. Ten clients: 2 phlebotomists and 6 therapists. Eleven to eighteen clients: 2 phlebotomists and the full 8 therapists (4 massage and beauty, 2 nails, 2 hair). The roster is published one to two weeks ahead from the confirmed bookings."

Clinical safety wording (short, for the operations section): "A named second collector is on call on mornings with nine or fewer bookings. If a first collector cannot obtain a sample after two attempts, the second collector attends; if she cannot, the client is rebooked or referred back to her doctor or midwife in line with the pathology partner's protocol. A client who feels faint is seated or laid down, the Venue Manager attends immediately and the recovery chair is used; the collector continues with the next client."

Do not print: the pathology partner and Medicare and lab-employment rules may require the lab to supply collectors (internal dependency, kept in the decision log).

"**10% off after your test.** Every expecting mother who completes a glucose tolerance test with us can redeem 10% off a standalone wellness treatment or multi-service package. One redemption per completed test. Cannot be combined with other offers. Does not apply to the morning package, refreshments, retail or gift vouchers. Subject to availability."
