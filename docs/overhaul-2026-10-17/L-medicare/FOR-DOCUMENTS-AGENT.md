# For the documents agent (D): Medicare and pathology wording, ready to paste

Authoritative commercial language. Do not mention the repository, file names or "locked" dates. Say "expecting mother".

## Business plan, pathology and Medicare paragraph (replaces the earlier P11 paragraph where it conflicts)
"Pathology and Medicare. Blood collection for the glucose tolerance test is carried out by our pathology partner under the partner's Medicare approvals. Under the Health Insurance Act 1973, only an Approved Pathology Authority that operates an accredited laboratory can hold an approved collection centre, so the partner, not GTT Center Perth, applies for and holds the approval for our collection rooms, on an occupation agreement with us. Medicare pays for a specimen collected at an approved centre only where the person collecting is the pathologist or an employee of the pathology authority. We are therefore seeking a partner that places its own collectors in our rooms, with our hosts supporting each expecting mother through the two-hour wait. On that basis the glucose tolerance test is billed by the partner to Medicare and carries no pathology cost for GTT Center Perth. This is subject to written confirmation from the partner and from Services Australia."

## One-line version for slides
"Our pathology partner holds the Medicare collection-centre approval and bulk-bills the test; GTT Center Perth supplies the rooms and the experience."

## Risk wording (risk register / plan)
"Medicare risk. Medicare pays for the test only where the specimen is collected by the pathologist or an employee of the pathology authority at the authority's approved collection centre. If our partner will not supply employed collectors, bulk billing may not be available for specimens collected by our own staff; the fallbacks are a separate lab fee payable by the client (about A$25.85 at Medicare schedule fees) or GTT absorbing it (about A$12,251 a month). Mitigation: written confirmation from each laboratory and from Services Australia before fit-out."

## Do not say
- Do not say GTT "will hold" or "applies for" an approved collection centre or any Medicare approval.
- Do not say "employed or engaged" is enough for Medicare to pay; it is enough for centre approval only.
- Do not state a lab fee other than the Medicare schedule fee, and always label private lab fees "No fee quoted yet".
- Do not say a WA Health licence is "not required"; say "none found; confirmation requested".

## Possible upside line (only after a lab agrees in writing)
"If the partner supplies its own collectors, GTT's collector wage cost of about A$13,364 a month (two on shift, with on-costs) is avoided, subject to any market-value service charge."
