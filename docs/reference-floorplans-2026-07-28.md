## Single Most Useful Insight

**GTT's total circulation allocation may be under-provisioned relative to real industry convention.** Source #4 (Coohom) states circulation space typically makes up **30-40% of total floor area in full-service salons**. GTT's floor plan (`floor-plan-concept.md`) allocates only **16sqm to "Circulation/Hallway" out of ~207sqm day-one total (~7.7%)** — well below the 30-40% industry convention, even accounting for the fact that GTT's open-plan GTT Lounge (35sqm) and Reception (15sqm) absorb some functional circulation that a hair/nail salon wouldn't have. **This is worth Anthony/the eventual architect checking directly against the actual venue once selected** — not necessarily wrong (GTT's model differs from a pure hair/nail salon), but a real, calculated gap against the one industry benchmark found for this specific metric, not a fabricated concern.
