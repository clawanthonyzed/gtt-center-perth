**Subject:** RE: Western Diagnostic Pathology - glucose testing

Hi Carole,

Thanks again — the ACC/NPAAC room requirements were really useful, and we've checked our current concept plan against them already (a couple of small additions to make, nothing structural).

A few things to follow up on:

1. On the "remain in our waiting room" point — I've had a look through the actual NPAAC Guidelines for Approved Pathology Collection Centres (the Third Edition 2013, Tier 3B document), and the RCPA's clinical manual for the GTT itself, and neither seems to specify that a patient needs to stay seated or confined to a waiting area for the full two hours — the NPAAC document's only relevant clause is about collection areas having furniture so a patient can be seated or recumbent during the actual collection procedure. I want to make sure I'm looking at the right source, though — is the two-hour requirement set out in a specific WDP policy document, or is it more a general duty-of-care standard WDP applies to patients under your staff's direct supervision? I ask because it changes how we'd think about the model — if it's tied to your staff specifically being responsible for the patient, an arrangement where GTT Center Perth's own trained collection staff are directly and continuously responsible for the client throughout, one staff member with her the whole time, might address the same underlying concern.

2. Two things from earlier that are still open, if you're able to get to them: an indicative range for what a WDP-supplied staffing arrangement might cost commercially, and a sense of the minimum term or commitment involved — happy to wait for these whenever suits, just flagging they're still outstanding.

3. On the site-assessment criteria you mentioned (GPs on-site, FTE doctors, referral volumes, etc.) — worth flagging that our model doesn't fit that pattern directly. We're not a GP practice with organic walk-in throughput; the GTT is a mandated test every pregnant woman in the state needs, so our volume comes from a dedicated venue built specifically around it, with referral relationships we're building with midwives and obstetricians rather than GPs physically on-site. Wanted to make sure that's factored in accurately rather than measured against a standard medical-centre yardstick.

Thanks again for all the detail so far — really is helping us get this right.

Regards,
Anthony
