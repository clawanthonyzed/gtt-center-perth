## Design Principles

1. **Patient flow is one-way:** Entry → Reception → Lounge → Collection Room → Treatment Room → Exit. No backtracking.
2. **Collection room is adjacent to lounge** — phlebotomist walks 3 steps to client, not across the building. ADIPS-compliant draw timing depends on proximity.
3. **Treatment rooms are private** — no client sees another client in mid-treatment. Pregnancy modesty is non-negotiable.
4. **Nail station ventilation (LEV)** is a separate zone with dedicated extraction — cannot share recirculated air with treatment rooms.
5. **Lounge seats 8 simultaneously (historical design principle from the 8-client Scenario B era)** — the lounge must comfortably seat the current committed model's simultaneous client count (18/day at peak under Table 1's 25-min cadence, not just 8 — see docs/CURRENT-STATE.md); at minimum, all originally-specified 8 in the lounge at once.
6. **Hairdressing needs a plumbed basin** — dedicated backwash, not shared with medical sinks.
7. **Staff zone is separated** — staff room and WC are not accessible through client areas.

## Critical Design Requirements — Architect Brief

### Blood Collection Room

> **Cross-checked 2026-08-06 against Carole Rivers' (WDP) detailed ACC/NPAAC room spec — 5 gaps found, none previously flagged in this document.** Full compliance table and sourcing: `docs/VERIFICATION-TRACKER.md` item 1p. Checked explicitly against every item on Carole's list, not assumed compliant. The 5 new bullets below (marked **GAP, added 2026-08-06**) are concept-level flags for the architect brief, not a redesign — existing bullets are unchanged.

- **Clinical sink** — elbow-operated tap (infection control), not a bathroom vanity.
- **Privacy screens or fixed curtain track** between Chair A and Chair B.
- **Medical-grade refrigeration** — specimen fridge with temperature logging (not a bar fridge).
- **Centrifuge bench** — vibration-resistant solid bench, minimum 600mm deep, lockable under-bench storage.
- **Sharps disposal** — 2x sharps container wall brackets (bench level + stand level), yellow biohazard bins recessed if possible.
- **Lighting** — clinical LED downlights over each phlebotomy chair, colour rendering Ra >90.
- **Sound insulation** — Collection Room must not allow sound to pass to adjacent treatment rooms (medical privacy).
- **WDP compliance** — exact room spec must be confirmed with WDP before finalising. WDP has published licensed collection centre standards that govern minimum dimensions and fittings.
- **GAP, added 2026-08-06 — reclining chair or examination couch for vasovagal episodes.** Not specified anywhere in this document. Carole's spec explicitly calls for a reclining chair or exam couch separate from the 2 phlebotomy chairs, for a client who feels faint or has a vasovagal reaction during/after venepuncture. **Confirmed missing, exactly as flagged before the check** — needs adding to the Blood Collection Room brief, likely a small additional footprint (~2-3sqm) not yet reflected in the 18sqm target above.
- **GAP, added 2026-08-06 — wheelchair accessibility within the Collection Room itself.** Building-level accessibility is addressed elsewhere (Accessible WC per AS 1428.1, Reception's low counter section, 1.5m-minimum Circulation hallway) — but nothing in this document confirms the Collection Room's own doorway width, internal turning circle, or chair positioning accommodates a wheelchair/mobility-aid user. Needs explicit confirmation at architect brief, not assumed covered by the building-level provisions.
- **GAP, added 2026-08-06 — room ventilation not specified.** Lighting is specified (LED, Ra>90); general room ventilation/air handling for the Collection Room itself is not — ventilation is only discussed elsewhere in this document for the Nail Station's LEV extraction, a different requirement. Needs an explicit ventilation spec for this room.
- **GAP, added 2026-08-06 — impervious bench/floor surface not explicitly stated for this room.** The Treatment Rooms section specifies "smooth, impervious, non-slip" flooring, but scopes it to Facial/Beauty Rooms and the Nail Station Area only — the Blood Collection Room's own bench and floor surface imperviousness is not explicitly called out here, despite being the room with the strongest clinical rationale for it (venepuncture, specimen handling).
- **GAP, added 2026-08-06 — data/power points not specified.** Centrifuge, medical fridge, and any EMR/booking-system terminal in this room all need power and likely data points — not itemised anywhere in this document (contrast with the Nail Station Area, which explicitly specifies "minimum 2x 10A GPOs" per station).
- **PARTIAL, added 2026-08-06 — general storage cupboards.** Only "lockable under-bench storage" tied to the centrifuge bench is specified. NPAAC's own premises standard (C1.1(iv)) calls for a "suitable, secure storage area for supplies... accessible only to staff" — broader than just centrifuge-adjacent storage. Not confirmed whether the existing under-bench provision is sufficient or whether a separate supply storage cupboard is needed in this room.
- **CONFIRMED, added 2026-09-04 — founder decision, specimen storage stays physically segregated from the cafe/wellness-lounge food & drink operation.** NPAAC's Guidelines for Approved Pathology Collection Centres require that specimens "must not be stored with food, drink, or pharmaceuticals." This requirement is already satisfied by this document's own existing design, not a new structural change: the specimen fridge (medical-grade refrigeration, listed above) sits inside the solid-walled Blood Collection Room(s), entirely separate from the Cafe/Refreshments Counter's own "mini fridge" (a distinct, solid-walled 8sqm room per the Room Schedule above) and from the GTT Lounge's own cafe counter feature. The two rooms are adjacent in the layout (see the Conceptual Layout ASCII diagram) but are separate solid-walled spaces with separate refrigeration units — no shared storage unit or shared space exists between clinical specimens and food/drink anywhere in this design. Anthony confirmed this segregation directly on 2026-09-04 (see `docs/DECISION-LOG.md`); this note adds the explicit NPAAC citation and founder confirmation to the documentation trail, it does not change the room layout, since the layout already achieved this outcome for other reasons (the Blood Collection Room's solid-wall/privacy requirement, established 2026-07-31, and the Cafe's own separate kitchenette room, established earlier in this document's history).

- **Facial/Beauty Rooms (3, 4, 6-growth) — 2 sinks RETAINED.** Plain non-invasive facials do not trigger the Code, but **brow waxing and brow threading are both real, planned services here** (`services-pricing-locked.md`: "Brow wax + reshape," "Brow thread + reshape"). Whether professionally-executed waxing (no nicking) constitutes "tearing... of the skin" under the Code's definition is a genuine boundary question this agent cannot resolve with confidence either way — **retaining the 2-sink/hands-free-tap fix here as the conservative choice**, not because it's confirmed required, but because the alternative (removing it and being wrong) is the worse error.
  1. **Hand-wash sink** — hot/cold, hands-free tap, soap dispenser, paper towel dispenser.
  2. **Instrument decontamination sink** — separate, in a designated prep area distinct from the treatment area, per §7.2.
- **Nail Station Area — 2 sinks per station RETAINED (moved from Treatment Rooms section, applies to the Nail Station Area, see Room Schedule).** GTT's manicure/pedicure menu (`services-pricing-locked.md`) does not explicitly state cuticle technique, but standard industry practice for "classic manicure"/"gel manicure"/pedicure services includes cuticle trimming with nippers, which cuts cuticle tissue (skin) — this plausibly triggers the Code's "cutting... of the skin" definition. **Flagged as an assumption pending confirmation of GTT's actual cuticle protocol (push-back only vs. trim/cut)** — retained as the conservative default.
- **Hairdressing Area — SCOPED DOWN, no Code-mandated 2-sink requirement.** Hair cutting is cutting of hair, not skin — does not meet the Code's definition ("cutting... of the skin, mucous membrane or conjunctiva of the eye"). Razor work (which would trigger "shaving... of the skin") is excluded from GTT's scope per Anthony. The existing single backwash basin (per Hairdressing Area's own spec) is sufficient — this was never a Code requirement to begin with, now confirmed.
- **Dimmer lighting** — massage requires dark/ambient; facial may need clinical lighting option. Install dual circuit.
- **Sound insulation — genuinely weakened by the curtain-partition change, flagged not hidden.** Matches AMT Massage Code's "clinic rooms should be impervious to sound" requirement, which a curtain cannot achieve to the same standard as a solid wall. **This is a real trade-off of the 2026-07-31 fit-out cost reduction, not resolved here** — options (white noise/ambient sound masking, acoustic curtain fabric, spacing bays away from each other) should be discussed with the architect/interior designer at engagement, not assumed adequate by default.
- **Hooks and gown rail** — clients undress for massage. Minimum 3 hooks at entry.
- **Floor surface** — smooth, impervious, non-slip. **Now applies specifically to the Facial/Beauty Rooms and Nail Station Area (Code-triggering), not a blanket requirement across all treatment rooms** — Massage Rooms and Hairdressing Area may use a different floor finish since the Code no longer applies there, though a similarly practical/washable surface remains good practice regardless.
- **Blood Collection Room** — piercing (venepuncture) unambiguously triggers the Code's definition regardless of this scope narrowing. 2-sink/hands-free-tap fix and all other §7 requirements remain fully in force, unchanged.
