# GTT Center Perth — Industry Standards & Regulatory Reference Compendium

**Compiled:** 2026-07-28 | **Purpose:** Per Anthony's request — what industry-standard/regulatory reference documents exist for each service category (hair, beauty, nails, massage, reception, retail, phlebotomy) in WA/Australia. This is about **operational setup standards** (infection control, competency, equipment), not award pay rates (those are already covered in `financial-break-even-staff.md`/`hr-framework.md`). Every source below was checked live this session — flagged explicitly where a category has no clear single standard rather than guessing one exists.

## 1. Phlebotomy / Pathology Collection

| Document | Body | Real Source URL | GTT Center Perth Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Guidelines for Approved Pathology Collection Centres (3rd Edition, 2013) | NPAAC / Australian Commission on Safety and Quality in Health Care (ACSQHC) | https://www.safetyandquality.gov.au/resources/guidelines-approved-pathology-collection-centres-requirements-medical-pathology-specimen-collection-third-edition-2013 (PDF: https://www.safetyandquality.gov.au/sites/default/files/2022-08/tier_3b_requirements_for_medical_pathology_specimen_collection_third_edition_2013.pdf) | **NOT YET obtained/read.** This is the actual national standard for collection-centre premises, staffing, equipment, documentation, and collection procedures referenced generically in `pathology-collection-room.md` — that doc should be checked against this specific source, not general knowledge. |
| HLT37215 Certificate III in Pathology Collection (training package) | Australian Skills Quality Authority (ASQA) / training.gov.au | https://training.gov.au/training/details/HLT37215 (unit list: https://training.gov.au/assets/HLT/HLT37215_R4.pdf) | Referenced correctly in `staff-plan.md`/`phlebotomist-job-posting.md` as the minimum qualification. Note: training.gov.au states explicitly "no licensing, legislative, regulatory or certification requirements apply to this qualification" — it's a training standard, not itself a legal mandate; the legal mandate comes from being an approved collector under WDP/PathWest's own NATA accreditation, which is a separate layer. |
| AS/NZS 4187:2014 — Reprocessing of reusable medical devices in health service organisations | Standards Australia | Paid standard (store.accuristech.com / standards.org.au) — no free full-text found | Already referenced in `pathology-collection-room.md` for sterilisation/AS 4187 compliance — confirmed this is a real, current standard (not superseded at time of search), but it is a **paid standard**, not freely downloadable. GTT Center Perth needs to purchase/access the actual standard text before opening, not rely on secondary summaries. |

## 3. Massage

| Document | Body | Real Source URL | GTT Center Perth Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| AMT Massage Therapy Code of Practice | Association of Massage Therapists (AMT) | https://www.amt.org.au/downloads/practice-resources/AMT-code-of-practice-final.pdf | **Real, free, directly downloadable — not yet obtained.** Covers infection control/hygiene, draping, informed consent, record-keeping, professional boundaries, advertising, WHS. This is the closest thing to a binding professional-body standard for massage in Australia (no government licensing body exists for massage therapy itself). |
| **Flagged gap — pregnancy massage specifically:** search explicitly found there is **no dedicated regulatory body or enforced minimum standard specifically for pregnancy massage** in Australia — training/guidelines exist at individual course-provider level, not as a single citable national standard. GTT Center Perth's "pregnancy massage specialisation" hiring requirement should therefore be verified per-candidate (course provider, hours of CPD) rather than pointed at one authoritative document, since none exists. Not guessing a standard into existence here. |

## 4. Reception / Customer Service

**Flagged — no industry-specific regulatory/competency standard found**, and this is expected: reception is a generalist administrative role, not a licensed trade. The relevant references are:
- Clerks — Private Sector Award MA000002 (pay/conditions, already covered in `staff-plan.md`) — https://www.fairwork.gov.au/employment-conditions/awards/awards-summary/ma000002-summary
- The new beauty-field-experience requirement (per Anthony's 2026-07-28 instruction) is a GTT Center Perth-specific hiring bar, not tied to an external competency standard.
- No separate "code of practice" exists for reception roles the way it does for skin-penetration trades — not invented here.
