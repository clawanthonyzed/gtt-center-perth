## D. Why Each Top Candidate Could Work

**6/325 Harborne Street, Osborne Park:** the only candidate in the entire search that is simultaneously (a) inside the Preferred size band, (b) confirmed inside the rent budget with real, current figures, and (c) in the #1 priority suburb with its documented freeway access and parking-stock advantage. Its weakness is that it's a genuine blank-slate shell — full plumbing, partitioning, and clinical fit-out from zero — which is a real capex and timeline cost the `floor-plan-concept.md` fit-out estimate (A$228,142-457,559) already anticipates for a scenario like this, not a reason to disqualify it.

**25 Mills Street, Cannington:** the only candidate found with an existing minor-theatre/treatment-room configuration and full staff amenities already in place — the layout most directly analogous to what the venue brief specifies, reducing both fit-out cost and construction time versus a shell. Its weakness is the missing rent figure, which is a real, unresolved gap, not a minor one: this candidate cannot be properly compared against the budget until that number exists.

## E. Major Risks and Unknown Information

- **Rent for 25 Mills Street is entirely unconfirmed.** Cannot be scored against the budget threshold until an agent provides it — this is the single largest open item blocking a real Tier 1 vs. Tier 1 comparison.
- **Parking count at 6/325 Harborne Street is not disclosed**, only described qualitatively as "ample." Given `location-scouting.md`'s parking framework, this needs a specific number before the property can be scored past the graduated-preference stage.
- **Neither Tier 1 candidate has confirmed zoning/permitted-use status for a health/beauty/wellness operation** — both require council confirmation before being treated as viable, per the Deal Breaker in the framework document. This is true of every candidate in this shortlist; zoning confirmation was out of scope for a listing-level search.
- **No plumbing/wet-area detail exists for 6/325 Harborne Street** beyond "none apparent" (retail shell) — the fit-out cost estimate assumption (full plumbing build) needs a site visit or agent confirmation to firm up, since an inaccurate assumption here has real capex consequences.
- **25 Mills Street's exact current configuration (2 vs. 4 consulting rooms) is ambiguous** across the search summaries found — needs a direct floor plan from the agent, not asserted as either number.
- **This shortlist itself has an inherent freshness limit.** Commercial listings change status quickly; several figures (particularly the Nedlands, Mount Lawley, and Wembley candidates in section B) were last verified 2026-07-28, over two weeks before this document's date — those are explicitly flagged "not re-verified this session" rather than presented as current.
