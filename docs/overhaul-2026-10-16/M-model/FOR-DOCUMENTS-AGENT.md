## A. Operating rules (L1, L2, L3, S4c, S11a, S11c, S13b, P3b, P10a, P16a, P19c)

- Cadence: one cadence only, the 25-minute pair cadence. First booking 07:00 (expecting mothers arrive fasted, 06:50). Pairs at 07:00, 07:25, 07:50 and so on; last first draw 10:20; nine pairs; 18 clients a morning at most. Full-capacity time slots are offered and booked 2 to 4 weeks ahead.
- Adaptive rostering (P16a): "Staff are called in to match the confirmed bookings, morning and afternoon. GTT never rosters idle staff. A morning with one booking runs two phlebotomists and two therapists, not a full team."
- No ramp-up (S11b, P19c): "GTT opens at full capacity, 18 expecting mothers each morning. A pre-launch waitlist and launch marketing fill the diary before the first day, so the model has no slow ramp-up period."
- Afternoon (P8d): "Afternoon services are stand-alone and pre-booked. They are not bound by the two-hour glucose test clock. Walk-ins are welcome when a therapist is free. The afternoon roster follows the pre-booked services."
- AM packages (P8e): "Every morning package includes any service on the menu that fits the 30 or 45 minute wellness window."

## C. Roster logic card (S10b, P16a)

"Staffing follows overlap, not headcount. Each client has two 45-minute services, and pairs start 25 minutes apart. One client: 2 phlebotomists and 2 therapists. Two to nine clients, one per slot: 2 phlebotomists and 4 therapists. Ten clients: 6 therapists. Eleven to eighteen clients: the full 8 therapists (4 massage and beauty, 2 nails, 2 hair). At about 12.6 booked clients the roster is 8 therapists because neighbouring double-booked slots put four first services in the room at once; if second-chair bookings fall in alternate slots, 6 are enough. The roster is published one to two weeks ahead from the confirmed bookings."
