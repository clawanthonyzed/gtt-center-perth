## Card C3 (plain English)

**Title:** 📐 Concept floor plan and China supplier sourcing strategy

**Text:**

📐 **Concept floor plan is ready.** One clean drawing of the venue at 18.5 m x 15.6 m (about 289 sqm, inside the 257 to 290 sqm range we are searching for). It shows:

- 🩸 Two blood collection rooms (Option A), with an inset for Option B (one big room split by a curtain)
- 🛋️ The open-plan lounge and the Private Refreshment Counter for GTT clients only
- 💆 Massage and beauty bays with walls, curtains and a handwash basin in each
- 🚽 Accessible toilet, staff room, storage
- 🚫 Clinical waste and the specimen fridge kept apart from the refreshments, as decided

📋 **Shopping list by room** (FF&E schedule): every piece of furniture, fixture and equipment, with the quantity and which budget line it comes from (fit-out, furniture and signage, equipment, clinical equipment, IT). No new prices.

🇨🇳 **China sourcing strategy:** what is sensible to import direct (lounge furniture, decor, partitions, curtains, cabinets, signage, massage and beauty beds, pedicure chairs), and what must be bought in Australia or proven first:

- ⚕️ Blood-collection chairs and similar medical items need to be on the Therapeutic Goods register. If we import one ourselves, we become the legal supplier.
- ⚡ Electrical items need the RCM safety mark.
- 🚰 Taps, basins and sinks need WaterMark.
- 🔥 Wall and ceiling materials must meet the building code fire rules.

Also covers Alibaba, Made-in-China and 1688 (with an agent), checking suppliers, samples, independent inspection, shipping (shared or full container), import duty (often 5 percent, nil under the China trade agreement with the right paperwork), GST on imports, and payment protection.

⚠️ **Things to know**
- It is a concept only. An architect and building surveyor must confirm it for the real site.
- The full room list adds up to more than the drawing can hold with proper corridors, so a few rooms are drawn a little smaller than their targets. A site near 300 sqm may be needed. 
- Nobody has been contacted. Nothing ordered or paid.

👉 **Needs your decision:** approve the "sensible to import" list, and choose how to buy (our recommendation: an Australian-based sourcing agent for the China items, local trades for everything regulated).
