# Legal Risk Protocol: Pregnancy Complications (P16d) - DRAFT for the solicitor and insurer

Prepared 2026-10-16 by Grace. This is a draft for legal and insurance review and a medical practitioner's clinical sign-off. It is not clinical or legal advice. Align with the existing emergency-plan.md, consent-form.md and gtt-clinical-protocol.md in the repo (those documents were not re-read in this pass; reconcile before use). Clinical thresholds below are placeholders for the lab and a doctor to set.

## 1. Principles
1. The clinical test, specimen collection and the client's clinical care during collection stay with the pathology partner and its collectors. GTT's wellness staff do not give clinical advice.
2. Wellness services never interrupt a blood draw, and the 60 and 120 minute marks come first.
3. GTT is a wellness venue, not a medical service; every document says so.
4. Safety over service: any doubt, stop the service and escalate.

## 2. Risk events and responses

| Event | Prevention | Response | Escalation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Faintness or collapse (fasting) | Fasted client arrives 10 minutes early; seat, not stand; water allowed per lab; client told to say if dizzy; staff trained to recognise faintness | Stop service, lie client down with legs raised, call the lab collector, first aid trained staff attend, note time | 000 if not rapidly recovering, unconscious, fitting, chest pain, bleeding, severe headache or pregnancy concern; tell the lab; notify next of kin |
| Low blood sugar (hypoglycaemia) while fasting or after glucose drink | Exclusion screening (section 3); lab protocol on the glucose drink | Follow the lab collector's direction; GTT first aid; do not give food or drink that would void the test without the collector's agreement unless the client is unwell, then safety first | 000 if altered consciousness. Document |
| Nausea or vomiting after the drink | Tell client about it; bucket and wipes in each zone | Stop service, call collector; the lab decides on retest | Lab and incident register |
| Fall or trip | Non-slip floors, clear aisles, handrails, seating that supports pregnant clients | First aid, do not move if head or abdomen injury, call 000 if abdominal pain, bleeding or reduced fetal movement | Hospital, incident report |
| Adverse event during massage, nails, hair or beauty | Pregnancy-trained therapists, no prohibited techniques or products (clinical list to be set with a doctor or midwife), patch tests, ventilation, temperature control, side-lying or supported positions | Stop, remove product, first aid, call 000 if needed | Incident report, insurer |
| Needle-stick or sharps injury (staff) | Lab collection manual, sharps containers | Lab and GTT first aid and post-exposure steps | WorkSafe WA notification if notifiable; workers compensation |
| Labour, bleeding, abdominal pain, reduced fetal movement | Intake questions; staff told never to wait and see | Stop everything, call 000, notify the lab | Hospital, incident report |
| Allergic reaction (products, latex) | Intake allergy field | Stop, remove, call 000 if breathing or swelling | Incident report |
| Privacy breach (health information) | Privacy policy, access limits | Contain, assess, notify | Privacy officer; notify if required (TO CONFIRM under Privacy Act) |

## 3. Exclusion and referral criteria (draft)
These are to be set by the lab's clinical lead or a medical practitioner, not by GTT. Placeholder list for review:
- No valid request form: no service starts.
- Known diabetes already diagnosed in this pregnancy, or insulin or medicines that make fasting unsafe: the clinical team decides; GTT does not proceed with the test and wellness packages unless cleared.
- Symptoms on arrival (bleeding, severe pain, reduced fetal movement, severe vomiting, dizziness, fever): refuse and direct to medical care.
- Multiple pregnancy, high-risk pregnancy flagged by the client's doctor, or doctor advice not to receive massage: treatments adapted or declined.
- Clients under 18 or clients with medical contraindications to specific treatments: individual assessment. TO CONFIRM.
- Companions and children: see CHILDREN-COMPANION-POLICY.md.

## 4. Consent and disclaimers (for solicitor drafting)
- Informed consent form signed at booking and checked on arrival: pregnancy status and stage, health conditions, allergies, medicines, understanding that GTT is a wellness venue, who is responsible for what, emergency plan.
- Honest limits: Australian Consumer Law guarantees of due care and skill (ACL s60) cannot be excluded for consumer services, and the Civil Liability Act 2002 (WA) governs liability for negligence. A disclaimer cannot remove liability for negligence. A solicitor should advise on what an enforceable acknowledgement and risk warning can and cannot do. TO CONFIRM.
- Pregnancy-specific advice: client confirms she has been advised by her doctor or midwife that massage and treatments are suitable.
- Telehealth and fasting: no GTT advice on fasting; refer to the lab's instructions.

## 5. Incident reporting
1. Immediate: first aid, 000, protect the client, call the lab collector.
2. Within the shift: incident register entry (who, what, when, witnesses, actions, outcome), photographs only where needed and consented.
3. Within 24 hours: Venue Manager notifies the owner entity and the insurer (per policy conditions); lab informed of any event in its care.
4. Statutory: WorkSafe WA for notifiable workplace incidents; Privacy Act for notifiable data breaches; AHPRA or other regulator only through the registered practitioner involved. TO CONFIRM the thresholds with the solicitor.
5. Review: monthly, learning log, retraining.

## 6. Insurance (questions for the broker)
- Public and products liability covering pregnant clients, fasting clients and falls.
- Professional indemnity for wellness practitioners and any registered nurse; confirm practitioners' own cover is in place and covers pregnancy treatments.
- Does cover respond to events during pathology collection (lab's insurer versus GTT's), and to the waiting period spent in GTT's lounge?
- Workers compensation (mandatory in WA) and personal accident.
- Cyber and privacy cover for health information.
- Entity named on the policies must be the trading entity (see ENTITY-RECOMMENDATION-MEMO.md).

## 7. Staff requirements
First aid and CPR certificates for all staff on shift, pregnancy-specific training, recognition of faintness and hypoglycaemia, emergency drills, adaptive rostering that never leaves a client unattended during the visit.
