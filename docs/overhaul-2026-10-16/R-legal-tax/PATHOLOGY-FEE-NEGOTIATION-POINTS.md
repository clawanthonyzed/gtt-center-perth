# Lab Fee Negotiation Points for the Next Pathology Correspondence (P20c)

Prepared 2026-10-16 by Grace. Draft only; nothing has been sent. Anthony sends all correspondence himself.

## Reality check first
GTT earns zero from pathology billing: the lab bills Medicare or the patient (pathology-partnership-brief.md). Under the Medicare rules found (PATHOLOGY-LICENSING-AND-ACCREDITATION.md) GTT cannot buy tests wholesale and resell them: the pathology service is the lab's own supply to the patient, and the lab holds the collection centre approval. So "wholesale per-test pricing" has meaning only in these forms:

1. The fee a non-Medicare client pays the lab (private fee), so GTT can quote it.
2. What the lab contributes to the cost of running its collection room and collectors (room, staff, consumables), if any, subject to Part IIBA limits.
3. Terms that protect GTT's margin (volume tiers, consumables, courier).

Any money between lab and GTT must be at market value, not linked to the number of tests requested by doctors, and GTT must not be connected to any requester (see REFERRAL-LAW-AND-TELEHEALTH-MODEL.md).

## What to ask, and in what form (all three: PathWest, WDP, Clinipath)
Ask each in writing, by email, with the same questions, and ask for a written reply on letterhead or email so they can be compared.

| # | Ask | Form requested |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Who holds the Medicare collection centre approval for our room and on what occupation document? Who must employ the collectors for you to bill Medicare? | Written confirmation |
| 2 | Private (non-Medicare) fee for the full GTT, per client, and whether it varies with volume | Fee schedule, A$ |
| 3 | Consumables supplied free: tubes, glucose drink, forms, labels, transport bags, sharps | Itemised list |
| 4 | Courier: one pickup per day, frequency, cost, who pays | Terms sheet |
| 5 | Collection staffing: lab-employed collectors supplied to our room, cost to lab or to GTT, minimum term | Indicative range and term |
| 6 | Any room or collection fee the lab pays GTT or GTT pays the lab, and the basis (independent market valuation) | Draft clause |
| 7 | Volume commitments: expected 18 clients a morning at full booking, what tiers or rebates apply to the client price, not to doctors | Tier table |
| 8 | Exclusivity: one lab per premises and its length; termination rights | Draft clause |
| 9 | Results turnaround and reporting back to the referring doctor | Service levels |
| 10 | Insurance: who covers the collector and the client during collection and the waiting period | Certificates |
| 11 | Fees and taxes on the collection centre approval, who pays | Statement |

## Points to avoid asking
- Per-referral payments, rebates tied to GP referrals, free items for referrers.
- Any price that depends on the number of requests from a particular doctor.
