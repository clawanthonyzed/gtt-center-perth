# Accountant Brief: ATO Private Ruling on GST Treatment of GTT Packages (P20d)

Prepared 2026-10-16 by Grace for the accountant. Draft for Anthony to hand over. It is not advice. Nothing has been lodged or sent.

## 1. Who applies, and prerequisites

1. **Applicant:** the entity that will make the supplies. This depends on the entity decision (see ENTITY-RECOMMENDATION-MEMO.md). A ruling applies only to the entity named, so decide the entity first. If the trading entity does not yet exist, TO CONFIRM with the ATO whether the proposed entity can be named or whether the trustee or the intended company must apply.
2. **Lodged by:** the registered tax agent or BAS agent as the entity's agent, signing the agent declaration, or the entity itself.
3. **Scheme must be real:** the ATO cannot rule on hypothetical situations; a proposal is acceptable only if the facts are reasonably certain (ATO, Applying for a private ruling). So finalise the package structure and have the pathology partner's role in writing (even in draft heads of terms) before lodging.

## 2. Form and channel (ATO, page updated 8 October 2026)

- Form: Private ruling application form, https://www.ato.gov.au/forms-and-instructions/private-ruling-application-form. A letter is also accepted if it covers the same information.
- Online: tax agents lodge through Online services for agents (or SBR-enabled software); a business with an ABN lodges through Online services for business. Individuals without an ABN cannot lodge online.
- Fax or post: Small and medium businesses and private entities over A$2 million turnover, fax (02) 6225 0906; individuals and micro business under A$2 million, fax 1300 139 011; post ATO, PO Box 3000, Penrith NSW 2740.
- Guidance: https://www.ato.gov.au/about-ato/ato-advice-and-guidance/ato-advice-products-rulings/early-engagement-for-advice/applying-for-a-private-ruling and the form instructions https://www.ato.gov.au/about-ato/ato-advice-and-guidance/ato-advice-products-rulings/early-engagement-for-advice/applying-for-a-private-ruling/reference-guide-for-private-rulings/how-to-complete-the-private-ruling-application-form
- Small business route: the same form in Online services can be used to seek early engagement, https://www.ato.gov.au/about-ato/ato-advice-and-guidance/ato-advice-products-rulings/early-engagement-for-advice/small-business-and-not-for-profit-organisations
- Legal basis: Divisions 357 and 359 of Schedule 1 to the Taxation Administration Act 1953 (cited in ATO form material; accountant to confirm).

## 3. Facts to include

1. Entity, ABN, GST registration status and date, trust or company details.
2. Business: pregnancy glucose tolerance test waiting lounge in Perth, WA. Up to 18 clients each morning (07:00 first booking, 25 minute pair cadence), afternoon standalone wellness services.
3. Pathology: an approved pathology authority (WDP, PathWest or Clinipath, TO CONFIRM which) provides the test, bills Medicare or the patient, takes responsibility for the clinical result. State exactly who collects the specimen and who employs the collector (this is open, see PATHOLOGY-LICENSING-AND-ACCREDITATION.md).
4. What GTT sells and to whom: wellness lounge access plus wellness service for a 30 or 45 minute window; refreshments; afternoon services. Price points A$250 and A$300 for AM packages, GST inclusive or not (state). Payment in full at booking.
5. Who performs each element, with qualifications and AHPRA registration (massage therapists, nail and hair staff, any registered nurse).
6. Any payment between GTT and the lab (room, collection fee, none), and the basis (market value).
7. Proposed invoice (section 4.3 of S38-10-GST-STRUCTURE.md) and the alternative with a nursing line.

## 4. Documents to attach

- Draft or signed pathology partner agreement or heads of terms and the lab's confirmation of who bills and who collects.
- Package descriptions, price list and sample invoice.
- Staff roles and qualifications schedule (position descriptions).
- Floor plan showing the collection room separate from the lounge.
- Any lab correspondence confirming that GTT does not bill pathology.
- The accountant's own analysis (ATO asks tax professionals to show research and opinion).

## 5. Exact questions to ask

1. Is a supply by [entity] of a package comprising lounge access and wellness services, where the glucose tolerance test is separately provided and billed by an approved pathology authority, a taxable supply in full under s9-5, with no GST-free part under s38-7 or s38-10?
2. If a registered nurse employed by [entity] collects the specimen at the request of a medical practitioner and [entity] charges the client separately for that collection, is that charge a GST-free supply of a nursing service under s38-10 (item 10), or is it a part of the approved pathology practitioner's medical service under s38-7? (Ask only if the nursing variant is pursued.)
3. If [entity] supplies collection staff or room use to the approved pathology authority, is that a taxable supply to the authority, and is the ATO's third-party rule (health services supplied to a business are not GST-free) correctly read?
4. Where [entity] sells a bundled package that includes any GST-free element, is the supply mixed or composite under GSTR 2001/8, and what apportionment methods are acceptable under s9-80?
5. Does Division 165 apply to the arrangement as described? (Ask whether the ATO will rule on this; accountant to confirm availability.)
6. If refreshments are included in a package, how are GST-free basic food items to be valued within the package price?

## 6. Timing

ATO service commitment: 80 per cent of private rulings finalised within 28 calendar days of receiving all necessary information; for complex rulings the ATO aims to contact the applicant within 14 days to agree a due date (ATO service commitments, https://www.ato.gov.au/about-ato/commitments-and-reporting/service-commitments/commitments-to-service/service-commitments). The 28 day clock starts only when the ATO has everything, so a complete application matters. Plan on two to three months in practice for a complex health issue, TO CONFIRM with the accountant.

## 7. What the ruling will and will not do

- It binds the ATO for the entity, scheme and period described. If what GTT actually does differs substantially from the application, the ruling does not apply.
- It does not cover pathology billing, Medicare or licensing.
- Timeline fit: lodge once the entity and lab terms are settled, and before the first taxable sale. Until the ruling arrives, plan and price on the fully taxable basis.
