## Slide and Page: GST structure summary (L5, S12b, P19d support)
"GST. The glucose tolerance test and its specimen collection are provided and billed by our pathology partner as a GST-free medical service. GTT Center Perth's own supplies, the Wellness Lounge, treatments and Private Refreshment Counter, are taxable and priced GST-inclusive. We register for GST and claim back the GST on our fit-out, equipment and supplies. Our financial plan already assumes the entire package is taxable (the GST-stress case), so any GST-free element confirmed by the Australian Taxation Office would be additional profit, not a plan requirement. Our accountant will seek a private ruling from the Australian Taxation Office to confirm the treatment."

## Page 12: replacement for doctor referral perks (P12a, P12b)
"Healthcare referrals. GTT Center Perth does not pay, reward or offer perks to any doctor, midwife or other practitioner for referrals. Federal law prohibits benefits that induce pathology requests, and we keep clinical decisions independent. We provide neutral information to doctors and midwives so they can tell their patients that the service exists, and every expecting mother remains free to choose her own doctor and her own pathology collection site."
Telehealth line for Page 15 (P15d): "Expecting mothers who do not yet have a referral can see their own doctor, midwife or an independent telehealth doctor of their choice. GTT has no commercial relationship with, and receives or pays no money to, any doctor or telehealth service."

## Page 16: risk protocol summary (P16d)
"Risk protocol. Every client completes a pregnancy health declaration and consent form, and a request form check, before any service. Wellness services never interrupt a blood draw. Staff are first aid and CPR trained and are trained to recognise faintness and low blood sugar in fasting clients. Any collapse, fall, bleeding, pain, reduced baby movement or doubt stops all services immediately and triggers the emergency plan (000 where needed, and the pathology collector). Every event is recorded in an incident register, reported to our insurer within 24 hours, and reviewed monthly. Public and products liability, professional indemnity and workers compensation insurance cover the venue, and the clinical care of the test remains with the pathology partner."

The Australian Taxation Office aims to finalise most private rulings within 28 days of receiving all information. Until a ruling is received, we price and plan on the fully taxable basis."
