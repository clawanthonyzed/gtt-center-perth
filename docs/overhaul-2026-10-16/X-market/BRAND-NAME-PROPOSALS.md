# Refined brand name proposals (P1c)

Checked on 2026-10-16. No name has been registered, reserved or filed. This is research only, not legal advice; a trade marks attorney must clear any name before spend or filing (the attorney step is deferred by Anthony, item G9).

## Starting point

The naming work holds SOLENA as the conditional leader (71.6%), ELOWEN second and ELOWYN third. This paper builds on SOLENA: the first three proposals keep its sound and brand world, and the other four are new names in the same soft, European, sunlit territory. Brief: luxury pregnancy wellness lounge, feminine, easy to say for an Australian ear, able to stand without "GTT".

## Recommendation

1. Lead with SOLENARA if Anthony wants to stay on the SOLENA line: it clears the .com.au, .au and the IP Australia exact-word search, which SOLENA does not. Run a similarity check on SOLUNAR BEAUTY and SOLUNARA before spending anything.
2. BELMIRA is the cleanest of all on the three tests and the strongest "no overlap" alternative.
3. Keep ELOWEN and ELOWYN from the earlier work as the fallback pair; their findings stand.
4. Before choosing: a spoken test (say the name on a phone call and see if the caller can spell it), and Instagram handle and Facebook page checks (not run; those sites block automated checks).

## Limits of these checks

Exact-word searches only. ABN Lookup returned fuzzy matches and the notes list the closest ones. A trade marks attorney would also search phonetic and class-similar marks. ATMS searched the exact string only. No name has been registered.
