# MIWM (Melbourne) benchmark (P6c)

## Identification

MIWM is Maternal and Infant Wellbeing Melbourne, miwm.com.au, Suite 2, Level 6, 369 Royal Parade, Parkville VIC 3052, phone (03) 8652 7222 or (03) 8677 9580. Confidence: high. The site, the GTT package page and the fees page all use that name and the GTT package matches Anthony's description (a Melbourne business sold as a GTT plus wellness experience).

Conflict with the plan: the business plan and feasibility files call it "Mums in Waiting Melbourne", established 2019, a "pioneer". No such business was found by search; the directory profile (village.ai) gives a founding year of 2023, and the clinic received a City of Melbourne Small Business Grant (2025) and was a Telstra Small Business Awards state finalist (2024). The name and the 2019 date in the plan should be corrected before the plan is reissued. Where the plan says "reported operating profitably", no public source for profit was found.

## What it is

A boutique, GP-led women's health and perinatal clinic (founded by Dr Amber Hart, GP and lactation consultant) offering women's and children's GPs, paediatrics, breastfeeding medicine, sleep and settling, physiotherapy, osteopathy, dietetics, perinatal psychology, immunisations, iron infusions, day spa services, antenatal and postnatal classes, and a "Mother-Baby Day Stay". The glucose tolerance test is one of many services. It is a clinic with a GTT product, not a GTT venue.

## Digital booking flow

Booking is through HealthEngine: a "Book Now" button on the website opens the HealthEngine booking engine, the patient picks a practitioner and a time and confirms, then pays on the day. Phone booking is also offered. No pre-payment step, no pre-intake forms and no automatic reminder detail was visible publicly. Compare GTT Center Perth: full prepayment at booking, intake form and consent collected at booking, tablets in the lounge. A tighter funnel.

## Lessons for GTT Center Perth

1. Demand exists: a boutique clinic sells out weeks ahead with a premium GTT product. Our pre-launch waitlist and 2 to 4 week booking window mirror that.
2. Booking friction: HealthEngine pay-on-the-day is easy for patients but exposes the clinic to no-shows. Our prepayment protects the 18 morning places.
3. The A$50 "standard" tier is a low-price door-opener that we do not have. It is a cheaper way to fill the clinic; we keep the venue premium.
4. Do not copy: a bulk-billed GP consult is part of their model; ours is not (no doctor on site).
5. Online presence: they are visible through directory listings (HealthEngine, Healthdirect, Doctify). Plan our own Google Business Profile at opening, and consider directory listings for the afternoon menu.
6. A deeper benchmark would need their Google review count, Instagram content mix and Meta Ad Library check, not run in this pass.
