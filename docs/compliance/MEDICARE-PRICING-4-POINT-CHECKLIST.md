## Point 1. Fee and gap before the consult

**Rule.** No law caps a private fee (R21, L). The duty is to tell her clearly before the service. The MBS midwife notes expect "informed financial consent ... including providing details regarding their fees and any out-of-pocket costs" (L). A fee not told in advance invites a misleading-conduct claim under ACL s 18 (L).

**What GTT does (Path A).** The booking page shows, above the pay button: the A$130.00 consult fee, "Medicare benefit about A$32.95", "your gap about A$97.05", and "our fee is set by GTT, not by Medicare". She ticks "I understand the consult fee and my gap" before paying. The confirmation email repeats it. The 48-hour reminder repeats the fee in one line.

**What GTT does (Path B).** No consult, so no consult fee. Instead she sees the no-benefit warning and ticks it before paying, and signs the s 23DNL notice at check-in before the draw (HIA s 23DNL, L). The lab slip goes with the sample. GTT never uses the s 23DNL words for a Path A client (for her a benefit is payable).

**When.** Booking (before payment), confirmation email (same minute), reminder (48 hours before), check-in (Path B only, before the draw).

**Evidence kept.** BOOKING-DISCLOSURE record: client ID, path, wording version ID, tick time stamp. Path B also: S23DNL-NOTICE (signed) and LAB-SLIP copy.

**Wording.** PART 3, section A.

**If it fails** (tick missing, wrong wording version, Path B notice not signed):
1. Path A: before the consult starts, the venue manager shows her the fee lines on screen and gets the tick; if she does not agree, rebook or refund the consult fee in full.
2. Path B: **no draw** until the notice is signed. If a sample was already taken without it, tell the lab in writing that day (slip), tell the client, and log it in the INCIDENT-AND-FIX log.
3. Fix the booking page the same day; Anthony approves the corrected line.

## Point 2. GTT Medical's own itemised account after the consult

**Rule.** A Medicare benefit is not payable unless the account or receipt carries the prescribed particulars (HIA s 19(6), L). They are: her name; the date the service was rendered; the amount charged; the total paid; any amount outstanding (Regs s 49); a description that identifies the item, "MBS item 82105" (s 50); the midwife's name and practice address or provider number, and a statement that she provided the service (s 51) (L). The benefit goes to the person who incurred the expense (HIA s 20(1), L), so she pays in full, then claims.

**What GTT does (Path A).** Checkout takes A$292.15 (or A$342.15) and gives a PAYMENT RECEIPT that says "not a Medicare account". After the consult, the midwife presses "consult complete" and the system emails the MEDICARE ACCOUNT from GTT Medical, dated the day of the consult. The lounge part is a separate GTT Lounge tax invoice. One payment screen is fine; one combined receipt with no consult line is not.

**What GTT does (Path B).** One GTT Lounge tax invoice for A$272.15 or A$322.15, marked "Lounge and service package. No Medicare item. This is not a Medicare account." The lab issues its own account. <!-- factcheck:ok -->

**When.** Account: same day, after the consult ends (a same-morning consult: before the draw is fine). Never at booking.

**Evidence kept.** PAYMENT-RECEIPT copy; MEDICARE-ACCOUNT copy (GTT Medical), linked to the CONSULT-NOTE.

**If it fails:** no account by end of day: the venue manager tells the midwife, who releases it the next morning with the correct date of service. Wrong date, missing provider number or missing item: cancel and reissue the same day with the correct particulars; log it. A consult that did not happen: no account; refund the A$130.00.
