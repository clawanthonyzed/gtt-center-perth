## Point 3. The consult is real, clinically relevant care with notes

**Rule.** Item 82105 is a "Short antenatal professional attendance by a participating midwife, lasting at least 10 minutes". Note MN.13.16: benefits are "only payable for clinically relevant services", and midwife services are subject to Medicare audit and Professional Review (L). Professional Services Review looks at conduct "unacceptable to the general body of the members of that profession" (HIA s 82(1)(d), L). The fee level is not the test; thin notes, short consults and a consult that exists only to unlock a package are.

**What GTT does (Path A).**
1. One client per consult; the midwife sees her in the one consult room.
2. The 25 minute block holds a consult of at least 10 minutes (planned 20) plus the note.
3. Content, every time: pregnancy history and risk factors; why the glucose test is offered and what it involves; fasting and safety advice (staying fasted if the test is straight after); the pathology request, only if the test is clinically right for her; how results come back (the abnormal-results phone call, a generic message for normal results); her questions.
4. If the midwife decides the test is not right for her: no request; the consult is still real care and is billed; the lounge part is refunded under the booking terms.
5. Note written the same day with start and end times, signed in the record.
6. The midwife holds Medicare participating status and a provider number for GTT's address before her first billed consult (READ-FIRST: hired with that status or a plan to get it). **Lawyer to check** any other Medicare requirement for midwife billing (not read this round).

**What GTT does (Path B).** No consult and no clinical advice beyond the draw. The phlebotomist checks the outside referral names the test and is signed and dated by the requester, and records "outside referral sighted". Questions about results go to her own doctor or midwife.

**Who and when.** Midwife, at the consult and same day. Weekly: the lead midwife (or the second midwife, swapping) audits 5 random notes (PART 3).

**Evidence kept.** CONSULT-NOTE (start time, end time, content headings above, request yes or no and why); REFERRAL-CHECK line for Path B.

**If it fails:**
- Consult under 10 minutes (late arrival, emergency): do **not** bill 82105. Rebook her at no cost, or refund the A$130.00. Note why.
- Note missing or thin: the midwife completes it the same or next day from memory, marked "late entry, [date]". Two misses in a month: retraining and a written reminder.
- A pattern of every consult ending in a request with no individual reasons: the monthly review asks the midwives to show the clinical reasons; **lawyer to check** if it persists.

## Point 4. Honest wording

**Rule.** GTT must not mislead about price or Medicare (ACL s 18; s 29(1)(i) false or misleading representation about price; s 29(1)(m) about rights or remedies) (L). Under the National Law s 133(1)(a) GTT must not advertise a regulated health service, or a business that provides one, in a way that is misleading, penalty up to A$120,000 for a company (V). Ahpra guideline 4.2: the public reads "free" as absolutely free; a cost recovered "through other sources such as Medicare" is not free (V). Any discount must show its terms and conditions (s 133(1)(b), V).

**Banned words (all channels, both paths):**

| Never say | Why | Say instead |
|---|---|---|
| "Medicare fee", "Medicare price", "Medicare rate" | Suggests Medicare set or approved A$130 | "Our consult fee is A$130.00, set by GTT" |
| "free consult", "free after Medicare", "Medicare covers it" | Ahpra 4.2; the gap is about A$97.05 | "Medicare benefit about A$32.95; your gap is about A$97.05" |
| "Path B saves you money" or "same price, no hassle" | Path B gets no Medicare benefit; compare like for like or not at all | Show each path's own price and benefit only |
| "fully rebatable", "claim it all back" | False | "You can claim part of the cost from Medicare" |
| Exact rebate without "about" | Rebates can change on 1 July | "about A$55.00" |
| "Best price", "lowest gap in Perth" | Unverified comparison | Nothing |
| A rebate figure for Path B | No benefit is payable | The no-benefit warning |

**What GTT does.** Every price and Medicare line lives in the WORDING-REGISTER with a version ID. Booking page, checkout, emails, receipts, accounts, welcome card and social posts use only register lines. New or changed lines need Anthony's approval (Publishing Shield) and my review.

**Who and when.** Grace keeps the register; the venue manager checks the live booking page weekly; Anthony approves changes; monthly full scan (PART 3).

**Evidence kept.** WORDING-REGISTER (line, version, approved by, date, where used); screenshots of the live pages each month.

**If it fails:** a banned word is found live: take it down the same day, replace it with the register line, log it, and check whether any client relied on it (for example, someone told "free"). If a client was misled, offer the choice of a refund of the consult fee or proceeding at the correct price, in writing.
