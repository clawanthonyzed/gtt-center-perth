## C. If a check fails
1. Stop the faulty step that day (wording off the page, no Path B draw without a notice, no account without a note).
2. Fix and reissue (account, notice, wording) the same day or the next morning.
3. Log it in the INCIDENT-AND-FIX log: date, what failed, client affected, fix, who.
4. Tell Anthony the same day if a client was charged wrongly, was told something false, or had a sample taken without the Path B notice.
5. Two failures of the same kind in a month: retrain, and I review the step.
